## EUREKA-01 :: Spawn Transfers Work, Never Authority

Authority stays. Work flows. Subagent is temporary witness, not power holder.

## EUREKA-02 :: The Bottleneck Is Not Intelligence

Most systems can think. Few can verify. Very few can judge. The scarcity is 555 and 888, not 333.
