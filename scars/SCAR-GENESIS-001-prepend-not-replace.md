## Axioms Surfaced

### Axiom 1 — Prepend, Not Replace

Any governed mutation SHALL prepend, augment, or supersede.
No governed mutation SHALL replace the substrate of an existing function.
Substrate removal requires explicit F13 ratification with witness of what was lost.

### Axiom 2 — Witness Before Replacement (WBR)

Before any mutation that risks overwriting a function:
1. Name the function.
2. Witness its current role.
3. Quote its current text/code/substrate.
4. Then propose.

Mutation without witness is constitutional debt.

### Axiom 3 — Loss Without Code Loss

A symbolic resource can be overwritten without any code being lost.
What is at risk is meaning, not data.
Detection of meaning-loss requires witness, not byte-diff.
