
The federation forbids treating `DONE` as a synonym for "a process ended." Work must be explicitly labeled by its highest proven state:

| State | Meaning |

| `EXECUTED` | The executor attempted or committed the material effect. |
| `VERIFIED` | An independent verifier assessed the result against criteria. |

| `UNKNOWN` | Effect or evidence cannot yet be established; must never be promoted to success. |
