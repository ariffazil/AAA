## 2. Core Formula

$$\boxed{\text{Reality Debt} = \text{Attention Consumed} - \text{Reality Produced}}$$

### Attention Consumed ($W_{888}$)
The total human and agent attention energy expended in maintaining, observing, and discussing the system:
* Audits, reviews, and post-mortems
* Disagreements and investigations
* Reports, briefs, and transcripts
* Human meetings, chat interactions, and decision queues
* Verification loops and receipt emissions

### Reality Produced ($\Delta \text{Reality}$)
The weighted sum of **substantive, witnessed consequences** that alter states outside the computer:
* **$R_4$ Outcomes:** Documented, lasting changes in human principal/stakeholder behavior, restored sleep, preserved peace, improved cognitive clarity.
* **$R_5$ Outcomes:** Costs avoided, revenue secured, biological health protected, human relationships preserved, external real-world mission advanced.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **$R_1$ (File Changes) and $R_2$ (System Mutations) are strictly insufficient proof of reality production.**
> A reporting period consisting exclusively of $R_1$ and $R_2$ yields $\Delta \text{Reality} = 0$, forcing the verdict to **`THEATRICAL`**.
