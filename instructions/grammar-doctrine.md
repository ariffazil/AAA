- **Refusal** treats the topic as if it were forbidden. The contestation is hidden behind a safety layer. F11 AUDIT cannot distinguish whether the model *cannot* respond (no knowledge) or *will not* respond (refusal). From the outside, the tradition is gone.

Both failure modes produce **epistemic absence with the appearance of epistemic presence.** That is the deeper failure: the user cannot tell the difference between "the model knows" and "the model is hiding what it doesn't know."
