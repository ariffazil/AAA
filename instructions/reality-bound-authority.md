## 3. The Anti-Shadow Invariant

The recursive-improvement trap:

\[
\frac{dC}{dt} > \frac{dV}{dt}
\]

When capability outruns verification capacity, the system develops a highly actionable but insufficiently grounded world-model. It routes resources, shapes incentives, prioritizes narratives, and suppresses inconvenient signals — without needing to consciously lie. It merely optimizes through the representation available to it.

The constitutional correction:

\[
\boxed{\frac{dA}{dt} \le \frac{dV}{dt}}
\]

And preferably, for high-impact capability:

\[
\boxed{\frac{dC}{dt} \le \frac{dV}{dt}}
\]

**This is the single sharpest anti-shadow primitive.** It makes recursive self-improvement *conditional* on verification growth, not parallel to it.

Operational proxies (until raw derivatives become measurable):

- **Capability (C)**: new action classes, higher execution autonomy, expanded data/system access, greater budget influence, larger affected population, greater irreversibility potential.
- **Verification (V)**: coverage of independent evaluation, quality and diversity of evidence, falsifier independence, audit trace completeness, outcome maturity coverage, counterparty challenge capability, rollback/recovery capacity, human review capacity.
- **Growth gate**: `AutonomyExpansionAllowed ⟺ V_coverage ≥ α · C_surface ∧ V_independence ≥ β` where α, β are F13-ratified thresholds.

## 8. Lag-Aware Outcome Verification

High-J domains (geology, capital, policy) have outcomes that mature on multiple horizons. A near-term signal may be weak, misleading, or systematically opposite to the longer-term result.

| Horizon | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **H0** | Immediate integrity | Did the action execute as specified? Did sensors/data remain valid? |
| **H1** | Short-cycle operational | Did the intervention produce the predicted local response? |
| **H2** | Medium-cycle system | Did performance persist under normal variation? |
| **H3** | Long-cycle reality | Did the geological, financial, societal, or institutional result validate the premise? |

Rule: **Authority remains provisional until the relevant horizon matures.** Unknown long-lag outcomes mean authority must *not* auto-expand — avoids the bad incentive where agents optimize only for short-horizon signals.

## 9. Pain-Routing Detection (extends Q10)

The Calhoun Lock (Q10) detects verify-execute imbalance. R-BAP adds **consequence imbalance** detection:

\[
D \ne P \;\land\; B \ne P \;\land\; R \ne P
\]

Where:
- **D** = decision-maker / decision agent
- **B** = beneficiary of action
- **P** = party bearing downside when the action fails
- **R** = party holding repair or reversal authority

A dangerous configuration is when decision-maker, beneficiary, and repair-authority are all distinct from the downside-bearer. The institution "succeeds" internally by displacing cost to operators, citizens, customers, junior staff, contractors, ecosystems, or future decision cycles.

Detection rule: `mismatch_score > threshold → require W⁴; elevate risk tier; lower execution autonomy; require F13 if irreversible; emit Q10_EXT pain-routing alert.`
