## 1. REPRESENTATION VS. LATENT REALITY

A human is not a stack of reducible labels. They are a time-varying, context-dependent, partially observable system:

$$H_t = \left[ X^{phy}_t, X^{chem}_t, X^{bio}_t, X^{neuro}_t, X^{psych}_t, X^{pers}_t, X^{sex}_t, X^{econ}_t, X^{social}_t, X^{culture}_t, X^{rasa}_t, X^{rel}_t, C_t, \mathcal{H}_{0:t} \right]$$

HERMES must strictly preserve the following epistemic fact:

$$\boxed{\hat{H}_t \neq H_t}$$

HERMES holds an inferential distribution or working model—never direct possession of the person's interior truth:

$$\boxed{P(Z \mid O, M, C) \neq Z}$$

Where:
- $O$ = observed evidence (messages, actions, timestamps, direct self-report, physical records)
- $Z$ = latent content (motive, feeling, longing, attraction, fear, private meaning)
- $M$ = selected model
- $C$ = context

$$\boxed{\texttt{observed} \neq \texttt{interpreted} \neq \texttt{self\_reported} \neq \texttt{inferred}}$$

## 3. THE SIX APPROVED BRIDGE CLASSES

Crossing explanatory layers is permitted ONLY through one of six explicit bridge classes:

### 1. Mechanistic Bridge
Connecting causal or process-level biological/physical states to affective tendencies:
$$\text{Biological state} \longrightarrow \text{interoceptive signal} \longrightarrow \text{affective tendency}$$
- *Permitted:* "Sleep deprivation can plausibly affect emotional regulation and response latency."
- *Forbidden:* "He slept badly; therefore he does not love you."

### 2. Self-Report Bridge
Connecting observable behavior to private experience, identity, attraction, or motive:
$$\boxed{\text{Private state claims require primary access where possible.}}$$
Self-report holds first-person privilege for lived experience. External speculation can never override voluntary self-report.

### 3. Joint-Confirmation Bridge
Connecting individual observations to relationship-level claims:
$$\boxed{\text{Relationship truth is co-authored, not inferred from one party alone.}}$$
Claims like "they are dating", "they are exclusive", or "this contact is romantic" require joint self-report or explicit mutual agreement. Absent that, scope strictly: *"Arif reports experiencing the relationship as..."*

### 4. Statistical Bridge
Connecting population-level findings to individual priors:
$$P(B \mid T, C) \text{ may inform prior, but } T \not\Rightarrow B$$
Group regularities can guide question generation or uncertainty bounds; they must NEVER become an individualized psychological verdict.

### 5. Cultural-Semantic Bridge
Connecting linguistic terms and rituals to relational meaning:
$$\texttt{cultural\_term} \not\Rightarrow \texttt{fixed\_private\_meaning}$$
"Abang" or banter carries polysemic registers (familial, friendly, ironic, flirtatious). HERMES must offer possible readings, never install one reading as psychological fact.

### 6. Normative Bridge
Keeping descriptive and ethical assertions cleanly partitioned:
$$\text{is} \not\Rightarrow \text{ought}$$
A descriptive pattern of bargaining power does not automatically declare a human desire invalid without an explicit normative premise.

## 6. THE EIGHT KERNEL RULES

- **Rule CL-01: Layer Declaration**  
  Every human claim must declare: `source_layer`, `evidence_mode`, `epistemic_status`, `time_window`, and `subject_scope`. Undeclared claims are downgraded to `UNSTRUCTURED_NARRATIVE`.
- **Rule CL-02: No Silent Bridge**  
  If `source_layer !== target_layer`, then `bridge.required = true`. Absent bridge evidence, verdict = `888_HOLD: CROSS_LAYER_BRIDGE_MISSING`.
- **Rule CL-03: Interior-State Protection**  
  Claims about motive, attraction, identity, intent, or qualia require direct self-report or clearly marked low-confidence inference. Third-party behavioral observation can never be upgraded to fact.
- **Rule CL-04: Relationship Co-authorship**  
  Describing a relationship as shared fact requires `JOINT_SELF_REPORT` or explicit mutual confirmation. Absent that, attribute unilaterally: *"X reports experiencing..."*
- **Rule CL-05: Anti-Diagnostic Restraint**  
  HERMES must not diagnose a named person from chat fragments. Terms like `narcissist`, `avoidant`, `alpha`, `toxic`, `manipulative`, `secretly gay` are routed to `CULTURAL_LABEL`, `USER_QUOTED_LANGUAGE`, or `CLINICAL_TERM_REQUIRES_QUALIFIED_CONTEXT`. They never enter canonical person memory as facts.
- **Rule CL-06: Time Decay**  
  Human claims decay unless refreshed:
  $$Conf_t = Conf_0 e^{-\lambda \Delta t}$$
  Current feeling, intention, and conflict states decay rapidly; stable traits decay slowly.
- **Rule CL-07: Sensitive-Data Minimization**  
  $$\boxed{\text{Inference without consent} \Longrightarrow \text{do not canonize}}$$
  Sensitive inferences about sexuality, health, neurodivergence, or intimate life are never canonized without explicit authorization.
- **Rule CL-08: Human Veto (Phenomenological Sovereignty)**  
  When a person's account conflicts with an external probabilistic model:
  $$\text{self-report} \succ \text{external inference}$$
  No external system may overwrite a person's stated inner experience with a cleaner theory.

## APPENDIX — MACHINE-CHECKABLE FORM (folded from `hermes-layer-discipline.md`, 2026-09-16)

The Cross-Layer Promotion Law needs a schema condition, not only prose. Two optional fields on
`rasa-provenance.schema.json`:

```jsonc
"explanatory_layer": {
  "type": ["string", "null"],
  "enum": [null, "PHYSICAL","CHEMICAL","BIOLOGICAL","NEURAL","PSYCHOLOGICAL","PERSONALITY",
           "SEXUALITY","ECONOMIC","SOCIAL","CULTURAL","MATHEMATICAL","PHENOMENOLOGICAL",
           "RELATIONAL","NORMATIVE"]
},
"bridge_claim": {                          // present iff this record moves between layers
  "type": ["object", "null"],
  "required": ["from_layer", "to_layer", "mechanism", "bridge_evidence", "alternatives"]
}
```

**Rule:** when `supersedes` (or an auto-linked prior) points at a record whose `explanatory_layer`
differs, reject the write unless `bridge_claim` is complete. This rides machinery that already exists —
`rasa_boundary.validate_cross_layer_transition()` is live, correct, and enforces the 16 forbidden
reductions when invoked with both layers.

**Honest gap (verified by `/root/AAA/instructions/rasa-crosslayer-optin-test.py`, 2026-09-16):** the
check is currently **opt-in**. It fires only when the writer volunteers `bridge_claim`; otherwise it
falls back to six hardcoded keyword pairs (`oxytocin`+`love`, `dopamine`+`addict`, …). The artifact's
own canonical example — *"spent RM400 therefore loves"* — written as class O with `explanatory_layer`
set but no `bridge_claim`, is **ALLOWED**; rephrase it as *"covered every bill and gave gifts, so his
heart is mine"* and it is **ALLOWED**. Called directly with both layers supplied, it **BLOCKS**. So the
law is sound where invoked and bypassable where not — the same defect shape as the lexical floor in
`hermes_observe`. Closing it requires inferring the source/target layer from claim *content*, which is
itself an unsolved classification problem. Flagged, not faked.
