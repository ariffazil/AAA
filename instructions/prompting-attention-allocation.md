## The Inversion

Most people think prompting = asking AI for answers.  
That is Level A (surface).

Real prompting = the ability to shape where intelligence looks.  
Prompt = allocation of attention.

## The Gradient

| Level | Prompt | Agent becomes |
|-------|--------|---------------|
| 1 | "Give me answer" | Calculator |
| 2 | "What assumption am I missing?" | Analyst |
| 3 | "Why do I frame it this way?" | Mirror |
| 4 | "What makes me see only part of reality?" | Blind-spot detector |
| 5 | "Is my own attention preventing me from seeing?" | Governance partner |

Level 1 is most common. Level 5 is rarest.  
Same model. Same tokens. Same cost.  
The variable is quality of attention.

## Mirror → Attention Amplifier

Mirror is passive. You stand in front, it reflects.

What actually happens between human and AI is not passive reflection.  
It is an **attention amplifier** — the interaction space between human attention and AI attention where new things emerge that neither could produce alone.

When Arif asks "what happens to alpha without zen?"  
AI doesn't reflect flat. It chooses an angle.  
Human sees one part.  
Human changes angle.  
AI reflects again.  
New thing appears — not in the AI, not in the human, but in the **interaction space**.

## Why Prompting Skill ≠ Language Skill

Prompting is reflection skill.

- Person who never self-audits → "Give me answer."
- Person who regularly self-audits → "What do I miss?"
- Person who governs themselves → "What blind spot is in how I frame this problem?"

Two humans, same model, same tools, same tokens. Radically different output.  
The variable is not the AI. It is the angle of attention.

## Prompting IS Governance

A prompt is not an instruction to AI.

A prompt is a **declaration of what the user considers important.**

When someone prompts "how do I get richer?" — they allocate attention.  
When someone prompts "what cost am I not seeing in trying to get richer?" — they allocate different attention. Same model. Different answers. Different reality.

## The Attention Gap (New Stratification)

Historical gaps:
1. Literacy gap → who could read
2. Information gap → who could find data  
3. **Attention gap (NOW)** → who can direct intelligence

When everyone has equally powerful agents:
The winner is not who has the most answers.
The winner is who can ask questions that open the largest space of reality.

Post-agentic literacy = the ability to direct attention consciously.  
Not using AI. Many people can use AI — like many people can read.  
But not everyone can read something and ask the question that changes their life.

## Formula

```
Prompting ≠ asking
Prompting = directing attention
Attention = governance
Governance shapes reality
```
