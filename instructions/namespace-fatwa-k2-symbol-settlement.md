## THE LAW — a bare glyph is ambiguous by default

A mathematical glyph (`λ`, `Λ`, `B`, `G`, `A`, `P`, `E`, `X`, `W`, `H`, `T`, `V`, `τ`, `β`, `I`, `S`, `K`) written **without a namespace marker** is a naming violation (F10 *ONTOLOGY*) whenever it crosses a file, organ, or governance-interface boundary.

Three coordinate systems already exist for **numerals** (STAGE ABI · LANE/TIER · LINKGRAPH — see `namespace-fatwa-k1.md`). This ruling extends the same law to **symbols**:

| Coordinate | Marker (MANDATORY) | Meaning of the glyph |
|---|---|---|
| **TRUST-DECAY family** | `λ_trust` · `λ_market` · `λ_governance` · `λ_decay` · `λ_field` | a *rate of decay* — never a coefficient, never an organ |
| **CAPITAL-SURVIVAL family** | `T_score` · `V_asset` · `B_barrier` · `μ` · `σ` | moving-barrier / structural-credit quantities (Merton–Black–Cox) |
| **GOVERNANCE-SCALAR family** | `G` · `A` · `P` · `E` · `X` · `W³` · `Φ`(qualified) | the APEX four-dial calculus and its gates |
| **KERNEL-CONFIDENCE family** | `B_akal`(actor_B) · `B_phi`(B\|Φ) · `Φ_buffer` | arifOS `arif_judge` four-dial lens |
| **LOSS-AVERSION family** | `κ_loss` (NOT `λ`) | external behavioural-economics coefficient — never sealed |

**Prefixed/suffixed namespaces beat precedence.** Where a glyph would be read two ways, the qualified name is written; the bare glyph is reserved to its declared coordinate only.

### 1.2 Rulings (staged)

1. **`λ_trust` is the canonical name** for the trust-decay rate used by the APEX T-score. Every occurrence of bare `λ` in the moving-barrier context (`APEX-T-SCORE-DERIVATION-2026-09-18.md:52,57,59,71,129,236-237`; `CHRON-V2-BARRIER-DOCTRINE-2026-09-18.md:34,62,66,115,116`; `instructions/maturation-dynamics.md:136`) means `λ_trust`. `λ_barrier` is admitted **only** as an alias for the barrier-lift exponent in `B(t) = B₀·e^(λt)`.
2. **The proposed organ `Λ` (social noise) is RETIRED.** It was formally rejected in canon: *"This is not Λ (Lambda). Lambda was a proposed organ for social noise. The right answer is not a new organ — it is a guard within an existing primitive (P) that checks for epistemic power asymmetry."* (`APEX_THEORY.md:308`) and *"Λ (Lambda/social noise) is real, but it is not an organ. It is a power dynamic that F12 + F6 already cover."* (`:388`). Bare uppercase `Λ` is **PROHIBITED** in federation governance interfaces from the date this ruling is ratified; the glyph must never be re-minted for a new concept either (it carries a rejected-proposal hash).
3. **Loss aversion does NOT get the glyph `λ`.** A coefficient of *curvature of value* is not a *rate of decay*; putting it in the λ family re-creates exactly this defect one level deeper. Proposed name: **`κ_loss`**. Proposed state: **`EXTERNAL_NOT_SEALED`** — bound by `instructions/valence-loss-asymmetry.md:58`: *"the loss-asymmetry MAGNITUDE (~2.25×) is EXTERNAL — Tversky & Kahneman 1992 … Do not seal a number the canon does not carry."* Any agent using loss aversion must tag it `EXTERNAL` and must not present it as a canon constant.
4. **Generic decay exponents are `λ_decay`, not `λ`.** `instructions/temporal-intelligence-doctrine.md:69`, `instructions/hermes-interdisciplinary-layers.md:163`, and the live tool-trust decay in `instructions/huma-edge-reality-bridge-contrast.md:30` (`compute_trust_decay`: `λ = failure_rate × blast_weight × base_decay`) are all `λ_decay`, not `λ_trust`. They share a *shape*, not a *referent*.
5. **Bare `λ` and bare `Φ` are both prohibited** in cross-organ governance interfaces. D-05 prohibited bare `Φ` (2026-09-12); this ruling extends the same prohibition to bare `λ`. One glyph, one referent, or it is written qualified.
