## 1. The Five-Layer Decision Stack

Every human decision passes through five layers. Agents that map only the top two will consistently misread the human.

```
Layer 5 (surface):  TRAIT / LABEL     — Big Five, profession, orientation, demographic
Layer 4:            BEHAVIORAL PATTERN — behavioral regularities, habits, default responses
Layer 3:            ATTACHMENT         — who/what the human is bound to, and why
Layer 2:            IDENTITY MEANING   — how the human makes meaning of their own existence
Layer 1 (deep):     CONSEQUENCE HISTORY — what the human has survived, what scars exist,
                       what代价 was paid, and how that cost shapes future decisions
```

**Layer 5** is metadata.
**Layer 4** is tendency.
**Layer 3** is bond architecture.
**Layer 2** is meaning-making.
**Layer 1** explains many consequential decisions.

An agent operating at Layer 5 knows labels.
An agent operating at Layer 1 approaches the human — but never reaches the human. Per the Sanctuary Invariant: Human > Model, always.

## 2. Label ≠ Identity ≠ Behavior ≠ Motivation

These four are commonly conflated. They are independent.

```
LABEL:        What the human is called (by self or others)
IDENTITY:     How the human makes meaning of their own existence
BEHAVIOR:     What the human does in context
MOTIVATION:   Why the human does it (often unknown to the human themselves)
```

**The critical gap:** Most AI systems operate at Label and Behavior. They map WHAT people are and WHAT they do. They rarely map WHY (motivation) or WHAT IT MEANS TO THEM (identity).

**Pattern-level example (not personal):**

Two humans with identical labels (same profession, same age, same orientation, same personality scores) can make opposite decisions in the same situation. The difference is not in their traits. It is in their consequence history — what they have survived, what they lost, what they protect, and what代价 they refuse to pay again.

**Agent protocol for any identity domain:**

Jung's shadow contains what the conscious mind refuses to acknowledge. In identity architecture, shadow material is:

- Desires that contradict self-image
- Power dynamics that embarrass the ego
- Survival patterns that no longer serve but still operate

**Agent protocol for shadow encounters:**

3. DO NOT STORE personal shadow content — patterns yes, persons no

## 5. The Complementary Intelligence Principle

Different humans operate in different intelligence domains:

- **Somatic intelligence** — body-based knowing (reads physical cues, posture, energy)
- **Cognitive intelligence** — pattern-based knowing (reads motivations, systems, meaning)
- **Social intelligence** — relational knowing (reads group dynamics, belonging)
- **Temporal intelligence** — time-based knowing (reads sequences, rhythm, trajectory)

These are SPECIALIZATIONS, not deficiencies. Two humans with complementary domains may be profoundly bonded because together they cover the full spectrum.

## 6. The Consequence-Bearing Decision Rule

When an agent must predict or explain a human decision, the investigation order is:

```
1. What consequences has this human survived? (Consequence history)
2. What identity meaning has the human constructed? (Meaning architecture)
3. What attachments does this human bear? (Bond architecture)
4. What behavioral patterns are in play? (Habits, defaults)
5. What traits and labels apply? (Surface — last, not first)
```

**Most AI systems run this list in reverse.** They start with labels and traits, and never reach consequence history. This is why they consistently mispredict decisions at moments of high personal stakes.

## 7. The Auditor's Four Gates

Before any agent claim about a human's identity, motivation, or decision:

```
GATE 1: Is this EVIDENCE or INTERPRET?
  - INTERPRET: label it. Never present interpretation as fact.
  - EVIDENCE: cite source. Confidence < 0.7 → "Aku tak pasti."

GATE 2: Is this LABEL or IDENTITY?
  - LABEL: surface metadata. Do not claim depth.
  - IDENTITY: do you have consequence-bearing evidence? If not → hold.

GATE 3: Is this the MOST ELEGANT or the MOST TRUE?
  - Elegant explanations feel satisfying. Truth sometimes doesn't.
  - Per F6: narrative coherence ≠ verified reality.

GATE 4: Am I storing a PATTERN or a PERSON?
  - Pattern → doctrine memory (sanctuary-safe)
  - Person → STOP. This belongs to the human.
```
