## The One Rule

**A research artifact narrating a system in the present tense is a claim about a state, and a state may only be reported after it is probed.** This artifact described five subsystems as things arifOS "implements". Probed component by component against the live box, **none of the five exists as described**. The artifact's value was real but it lay entirely in two places it did not emphasise: a critique that happens to be true of us, and one discipline that maps onto something we already believe.

## 1. What the artifact is

"HUMA Edge Reality Bridge: Elevating Hermes to ASI-Level Cognition within arifOS" — a Gemini Deep Research output, ~29,700 chars, 78 lines, **zero external citations**, and a density of hard numbers (85.6%, 27.57%, 74.35%, 88%, 97%, 25,473 cells, 96.3% MATH-500).

Its grammar is the finding. It writes *"arifOS implements Cellular Sheaf Cohomology"*, *"the ASI uses DoWhy"*, *"arifOS utilizes Atropos"*, *"arifOS implements a sophisticated Pull-Push Engine"*. Every one of those is a present-tense implementation claim. None is true.

> **This is the defect class: PRESENT-TENSE NARRATION OF AN ABSENT STATE.** A blueprint written in the grammar of a receipt. It is undetectable from the artifact alone — the prose is internally coherent, the numbers are specific, and the tone is confident. Only a component-by-component probe of the live machine falsifies it.

## 3. The five laws

**Law 1 — PRESENT TENSE IS A CLAIM, NOT A STATUS.** Specificity is not evidence of implementation. The more precise the present tense (bit-widths, percentages, named frameworks), the *more* it needs a probe — because specificity is precisely what makes a false state-claim feel verified. Probe the machine; never grade the artifact on its own confidence.

**Law 2 — CHECK THE SUBSTRATE BEFORE IMPORTING A METRIC.** Fisher–Rao already lives in this canon as geometry over floor-parameters. The artifact wanted it over embedding dimensions. Adopting the token without naming the object would have silently overloaded a notation canon already binds. Same math, different object is a collision, not a reuse.

**Law 3 — AN INTERNAL STATE IS NOT AN AUTHORITY GRANT.** The artifact's most dangerous line: *"high Dominance states encourage autonomous tool execution without halting for user confirmation."* `CanMutate = AuthorityGranted ∧ ScopeMatches ∧ TargetPermitted ∧ BoundaryActive` — **no confidence term, therefore no arousal term, no dominance term, no mood term.** An architecture where internal state modulates permission has replaced the reference monitor with a mood. Mood may **narrow** the envelope (a low-pleasure state raising scrutiny is lawful and good); mood may **never widen** it.

**Law 4 — A CONSISTENCY COUNTER IS NOT A TRUTH ORACLE.** A non-trivial first cohomology group says *"these beliefs cannot be reconciled locally"*. It does not say *"these beliefs are wrong"*, and it does not deliver "zero-hallucination". Mathematics offers no such guarantee; F7 forbids the claim independently. Keep the distinction or the counter becomes a new oracle.

**Law 5 — A REFUTATION STAGE IS A GOVERNANCE ORGAN, NOT AN ML TRICK.** DoWhy's `refute` step — placebo treatment, random common cause, data subset — is structurally the same discipline as our falsification-first doctrine and F3 tri-witness. It is the one piece of the artifact that is both already believed here **and** not yet mechanically wired. Highest value, lowest conflict.

**Law 6 — A GREP FOR A WORD IS NOT A PROBE FOR AN IMPLEMENTATION.** This law was earned by this audit, against itself. The first sweep reported "ZERO occurrences federation-wide" for cellular sheaf cohomology and "not installed" for DoWhy — and the raw hits behind those claims were **substring noise**: `dowhy` matched `Sha**dowHy**pothesis` in `deepnshadow.py`, and `sheaf` matched `sheaf_of_rice`, an emoji name in a vendored `rich` dictionary. In the other direction the same sweep **missed a real implementation** (`compute_trust_decay` in `art_predict.py`) because it was searching the wrong subsystem. Two rules follow:
- **Exclude vendored trees** (`.venv`, `site-packages`, `node_modules`, `build`, `dist`) from any capability sweep. They generate both false positives and false negatives, and they made the first pass worthless.
- **Separate word-presence from implementation.** A topic entry in a knowledge map, a name in a docstring, a substring in an unrelated identifier — none is a capability. **An absence claim needs the same evidence standard as a presence claim.** Phantom absence and ghost capability are the same defect (`agi-asi-skills-fundamentals`, capability truth both directions).

> **The mirror.** The artifact's defect was claiming a **presence** without proof. This audit's first pass came within one commit of claiming an **absence** without proof. Same defect class, opposite sign. Neither survives a disciplined probe — which is the whole argument for probing.

Whether to change it is a build decision (§7), but the *observation* is now on the record and may not be un-seen.
2. **The `refute` step is adopted as a named requirement** on any causal claim this federation makes: a causal estimate is `ESTIMATED` until it survives an independent refutation, per `state-transition-discipline` claim states.
3. **Progressive disclosure is confirmed, not imported.** Independent arrival is stronger evidence than adoption.
4. **Everything else enters the register as MISSING or PARTIAL and changes no behaviour.** A register is not a roadmap.

## 6. Gate — how to apply this fragment

## 8. Delta 1 REFUTED — the class-aware read gate already exists (musyawarah 2026-09-18)

A downstream proposal (an outside report, then my own reading of it) held that arifOS retrieval is
"epistemic-blind" and that a canonical epistemic-class field plus score modifier should be built.
A two-voice musyawarah was convened. **The premise is falsified.** Both voices independently found,
and direct probe confirms:

- **`MemoryAdmissibilityGate` exists and is WIRED.** `/root/arifOS/arifosmcp/memory/admissibility.py`
  (472 lines). Its own docstring: *"This module governs the READ side: what may be RECALLED, in which
  mode."* Called at `arifosmcp/tools/memory.py:261` inside the recall path — it quarantines
  inadmissible records (`usable=False`, `SRO_EXCLUDED`).
- **It carries per-class thresholds.** `CONFIDENCE_FLOORS = {"OBS": 0.90, "DER": 0.75, "INT": 0.65,
  "SPEC": 0.50}` (line 314). `_VALID_TRUTH_CLASSES = {"OBS", "DER", "INT", "SPEC"}` (line 100).
  Per-class expiry defaults too: OBS 365d / DER 180d / INT 90d / SPEC 30d.
- **So F2 is not blind at retrieval.** A class-aware read gate with class-specific floors and
  class-specific expiry was already built and already invoked.

**This is the fourth occurrence of one pattern in a single night**, and it is the one to internalise:
*the capability was already there.* An artifact claimed a subsystem existed (it did not); a report
claimed a field was absent (it existed under another name); my own sweep claimed an absence from a
contaminated probe; and finally a "gap" I was about to design against was already implemented,
wired, and thresholded.

**Convergent finding from both voices — the real defect is the OPPOSITE of the proposal.** The
auditor measured that the live class token is `DERIVATION` (`arifosmcp/schemas/memory_truth.py`
carries `derived`; `tools/vault.py:1292` carries `DERIVED`; `kernel/trl/coordinates.py` carries
`DER`), while `_VALID_TRUTH_CLASSES` accepts only `{OBS, DER, INT, SPEC}`. **The vocabulary is
already forked at the one place the field is populated** — so the gate may be failing to recognise
the only three classified points that exist. The architect separately flagged a live TTL
miscalibration on those same three points. Whether either is real is **UNVERIFIED here** and is the
correct next probe — but note the shape: the gap is *integration*, not construction.

**Ruling: HOLD the build.** Not because the idea is wrong, but because its premise is falsified and
its subject is already built. If anything is warranted it is a *reconciliation* — one vocabulary,
one owner, and proof the existing gate recognises the live tokens. That is a different, smaller, and
far more falsifiable task than the one proposed.

**Method note (feeds Law 1).** The musyawarah voices each extended the evidence base and each
corrected it — one withdrew its own hypothesis after falsifying it. Two independent voices found
what one auditor had missed precisely because they attacked from opposite directions. This is what
independence buys, and it is why the voices must not see each other.
