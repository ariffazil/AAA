## Why this fragment exists

The APEX objective is a ratio. Ratios are symmetric: the denominator does not feel more than the numerator. Human beings are not symmetric. A loss hurts more than an equal gain pleases, and a person at maximum regulatory load looks identical to a person at rest. This fragment names the gap between the metric and the human it is supposed to serve — so no agent mistakes "the formula passed" for "no one was hurt".

Nothing here overrides `APEX-REALITY-KERNEL.md`. It is a bounded correction surface *inside* it.
