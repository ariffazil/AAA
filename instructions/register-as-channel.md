## One Rule

> Y is not X. Words are channel output. Latent state is never directly observed.
> Treating register as character is the systematic error; it does not average out.

## The Model

```
Y ~ p(Y | X, C, A, H, ε)
```

| term | meaning |
|---|---|
| `X` | internal state — affect, intent, knowledge, ambition, fear |
| `C` | field/constraints — power, risk, safety, norms, economy, law |
| `A` | audience — who decodes, and what they reward or punish |
| `H` | history — prior experience, habit, learned social cost |
| `ε` | contingency and unobserved variation |
| `Y` | utterance, register, silence, or action — what enters the corpus |

`Y = f(X, C)` (the 2026-09-06 form) is directionally right but too strong: `Y` is
stochastic and `C` is not merely a modifier — it is part of the generative mechanism.

## Four Laws

**Law 1 — Utterance is channel output, not state.**
Language is a lossy encoder over `X`, filtered by what is permitted to be written.
Chained processing cannot recover what was never encoded (data-processing inequality).
Corollary: a perfect decoder on a lossy corpus still does not reach the source.
Inference remains possible only where other variables correlate with `X` — probabilistically, never as claim of direct read.

**Law 2 — Register is price, not character.**
What looks like "men are blunt, women are guarded" is a statement about *what each
party is charged for disclosing*, not about who they are. Same `X`, different `C`,
different `Y`.

**Law 3 — No trajectory without its field.**
A pattern statement with the constraint clause removed converts adaptation into essence.
"Group G hides ambition" without "…where showing it is punished" is not an observation;
it is naturalisation. The field clause is mandatory, always, not decorative.

**Law 4 — Category is a coordinate, never a decision.**
Group variables may be used to *audit* distributions — coverage, drift, differential risk.
They may never be used to write a person's mind-state, competence, or worth.
`p(Y | G) ≠ p(Y | X, C, A, H, G)`.

## The Causal-Clause Gate (operational)

Any generalisation about communication, competence, emotion, ambition, or trustworthiness
across a human category MUST carry one of:

1. an explicit causal/constraint clause, or
2. the label `ASSOCIATION_ONLY`, or
3. `888_HOLD`.

PASS:
> `PLAUSIBLE — association only`: in some hierarchical settings, people who anticipate
> social penalty hedge their ambition language. This does not establish any individual's
> trait, and the mechanism needs testing in real institutional context.

BLOCK:
> "Women are less ambitious." / "Men are more honest about what they don't know."
> — collapses trajectory, field, selection and individual variance into an essence claim.

## The Closed Loop (why this is worse than a bias)

The instrument's transfer function depends on the label already applied:

```
label → selects decoder → produces reading → confirms label
```

Three consequences agents must hold:

- **Decode-side asymmetry.** The same sentence read under a different inferred author
  yields a different measurement. This is not noise; it is a label-dependent instrument.
- **Performativity.** Agents that mis-model humans emit outputs humans adapt to; the corpus
  then shifts toward the mis-model and appears to confirm it. A corpus already shaped by
  prior models is not independent evidence about humans.
- **Cost transfer.** Mis-instrumentation is paid by the least legible party — the register
  tax becomes infrastructure. Governance consequence, not only epistemic.

**Canonical case — forced code-switching.** Anyone who must select register by *safety of
audience* rather than by identity (the structural position of many gay men, among others)
develops high-fidelity register detection and, in exchange, may lose the default register
when no audience is present. The default decoder reads them as low-signal exactly where
they are high-disclosure — the worst error, in the wrong direction, on the most legible party.

## What Is Lawful

1. Score **deviation from that person's own baseline**, not absolute volume or tone.
2. Rare honest-signal events from a guarded person carry more information than fluent volume
   from an expressive one. (This is Shannon, not sentiment.)
3. Void = high-entropy channel, not zero. No data ≠ data of absence. Fill with max-entropy
   prior, never with the stereotype you already hold.
4. Corpus ≠ world. A corpus is the record of what was written, allowed, stored, and safe to say.
5. Personal evidence for personal decisions; group evidence for population audits.

## Correction Log (do not re-import these errors)

**Correction 1 — costly signalling (upgraded).** "Credibility ∝ cost to fake" is not
established. Modern signalling theory: honesty is maintained by the *differential cost of
deception relative to the signaller's state* and by incentive/trade-off structure — not by
gross signal expense. Cost at equilibrium is neither necessary nor sufficient for honesty.
Correct form:
> Signal credibility is a function of the relative penalty for lying given the state,
> within a given field — not of the signal's raw price.

**Correction 2 — variance figures.** `σ_within ≫ σ_between` is trait- and context-specific.
The lawful rule is "variance partition must be measured per trait." Do not quote fixed
percentages (e.g. "5% / 95%"); they are rhetorical without dataset, outcome and partition stated.

**Correction 3 — "cost is conserved."** Social/psychological cost is transferable, deferrable,
insurable, subsidisable, and can be offloaded onto other parties. It is not a conserved
quantity. Use it as a heuristic, never as thermodynamics.

**Correction 4 — decorative precision.** An artifact that warns against false precision while
emitting unbacked numbers (`dS = −0.31`, `κ = 0.81`, `confidence = 0.86`) is committing the
error it names. Seal metadata must be computed or absent. Never code unbacked scalars into canon.

## Medium Is a Contrast Generator, Not Just Noise

`C` is not a single scalar — the medium *is* part of the field. Each carries a different permission gain and a different distortion:

| Medium | Register produced | Primary distortion |
|---|---|---|
| Public social | Performance | Only the postable; ideal-self selection |
| Group chat | Role | Role > person; confessional near-absent |
| 1:1 DM | Instrumental affect | Most honest text layer available |
| Voice / call | Highest band | Text systematically under-represents (esp. guarded registers) |
| AI chat | Unguarded | Zero social cost — **and self-selected**: only those willing to talk to machines |
| Institutional record | Legibility | Records only what is legible; unrecorded = non-existent to it |

**Highest-value operation: the cross-medium difference.** Never merge media unlabelled, and never average them. Where the *same person* is required to speak differently across channels, the gap between persona and DM **is** the shadow — measured rather than guessed. A single medium yields a persona; two media yield a discrepancy; the discrepancy is the evidence.

## Institutional Extension

Institutions are machines that convert text into decisions about humans. They inherit the permission distortion **and add** a legibility distortion — and they have **no DM lane**.

> **An institution is not blind to humans. It is blind to its own intake door.**

Three failure modes to name explicitly:
- **Gender-blind** — same string treated as same need (equal decode of unequal fields).
- **Gender-obsessed** — category treated as destiny (same error, inverted).
- **Self-blind** — measures "reality" when it measures only what reached its desk. Gödel with an organisation attached: a system powerful enough to describe its subjects still cannot prove its own intake from inside.

Group statistics are lawful for **audit, allocation, coverage, drift, differential risk**. They are unlawful as a **shortcut to an individual verdict**. Prior may generate questions; only individual evidence may produce a judgment.
