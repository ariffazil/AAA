## 0. The Three-Line Invariant (Lead Compression)

> **Attention is abundant.**
> **Witness is scarce.**
> **Promoted witness is civilisation.**

The Witness Cost Gradient is the cost surface of converting **abundant attention** into **scarce promoted witness**. Civilisation is the running balance of that conversion across generations. Every institutional failure that looks like "we forgot," "no one told us," or "the record is silent" is, almost always, a promotion-capacity failure — not a knowledge failure.

## 1. The Original Paradox (entry door, not final doctrine)

The Audio-Text-Visual Intelligence Paradox observed that human-intelligence optimises for **signal richness** while institutional-intelligence optimises for **signal durability**:

| Modality | Human bandwidth | Emotional signal | Institutional persistence |
|----------|-----------------|------------------|---------------------------|
| Audio | Very high | Very high | Low |
| Visual | Extremely high | High | Medium |
| Text | Lower | Low | Very high |

This was the *entry observation*, not the theory.

## 2. The Correction: All Modalities Are Witness-Capable

Visual is not a single-function comprehension aid. A geological section, a satellite scene, a forensic photo, a system screenshot, a CT slice — these are **witness-grade artifacts** at point of capture. Treating visual as "comprehension only" underweights its institutional role.

Sharpened mapping:

```
Audio   = Resonance-rich witness      (attests presence, proposes attention)
Visual  = Geometry-rich witness       (attests spatial reality, proposes comprehension)
Text    = Addressability-rich witness (attests chainable reality, proposes continuity)
```

All three can witness. What differs is the **cost of promoting** the modality into witness-grade institutional form.

## 3. The Witness Cost Gradient

Let **C(m)** be the cost of promoting modality m into an institutional witness — that is, into a SEAL-able, addressable, hash-chainable, auditable artifact.

```
C(audio)  ≈ high      — ASR + diarisation + timestamping + hash + retention
C(visual) ≈ moderate  — frame selection + provenance + tamper-detection + geotag
C(text)   ≈ low       — already serial, already addressable
C(multi)  ≈ lowest per unit of truth — tri-witnessed, cross-modal redundancy
```

This is the **invariant**. The paradox was never about essence — it was about the **cost surface**.

### 3.1 The Operational Law

> **Truth is multi-modal. Institutions are cost-sensitive.**
> **Witness emerges where modality and promotion cost meet.**

### 3.2 Promotion Heuristic (for any witness pipeline)

When designing a witness membrane (digest → inbox → anchor → promotion gate), ask:

1. What modality is the input arriving in?
2. What is C(m) for this modality in this pipeline?
3. Does the proposed witness surface honour the resonance / geometry / addressability it carries — or does it collapse the signal into a cheaper but weaker form?
4. If C(m) is high, can **cross-witnessing** (F3 tri-modal) reduce total cost per unit of truth?

## 4. Why This Matters: F6 MARUAH Safeguard

If the doctrine is read as "text creates civilisation," it **devalues the human substrate**. Human dignity — voice, pause, trembling, gaze, embodied presence — lives in the **resonance layer**. A transcript of survivor testimony is not the same as survivor testimony. Information may be equal; witness is not.

The Witness Cost Gradient therefore carries an explicit F6 floor:

> **No promotion may strip resonance without leaving a witnessable trace of the resonance itself.**

Concretely: when audio is transcribed to text for audit, the audio artifact must be retained and cross-witnessed with the text. The transcript witnesses content; the audio witnesses presence. Both must survive.

## 5. Meta-Modal Civilisation (F3 in Sociological Form)

Mature civilisations are not text-preferring or audio-preferring. They are **meta-modal**: they pick the right modality for the right function.

| Function | Dominant modality |
|----------|-------------------|
| Law, contract, audit | Text |
| Ritual, testimony, presence | Audio + embodiment |
| Geospatial reality, engineering | Visual |
| History, archives | Text + visual |
| Human witness under oath | Audio + visual |
| Identity, dignity | Resonance (multi) |
| Continuity across generations | Multi-modal redundancy |

The doctrine therefore prescribes:

> **Institutions survive not by choosing one modality, but by knowing which modality to enshrine for which function — and never letting any single modality carry the whole load.**

This is F3 tri-witness at the civilisational layer: Human × AI × Earth, transposed to Audio × Visual × Text, transposed again to Resonance × Geometry × Addressability.

## 6. Operational Implication: Promote Through Existing Rivers

Modern AI systems default to **adding attention surface**:

```
+1 alert
+1 dashboard
+1 notification
+1 queue
```

This produces **attention debt** — more signal, less continuity. The Witness Cost Gradient prescribes the opposite:

> **Promote through existing rivers. Do not create new rivers.**

When designing a witness membrane:

- Anchor into existing vault chains (VAULT999), not new logging systems.
- Reuse existing receipt surfaces (arifFlow FQ, AAA cockpit, FRAME observer) rather than inventing parallel observation planes.
- Promote through the existing promotion gate, not through ad-hoc "important" flags.

This is the inverse of the Calhoun pattern: institutions that survive are institutions that **compress attention cheaply into witness**, not institutions that expand surface area.

## 10. The Failure Law

Let **C(m)** be the witness cost of modality m, and **P** be the promotion capacity of the institution at time t. The doctrine collapses when:

> **Reality loss occurs when C(m) > P.**

This is the formal failure mode. It explains why high-resonance, high-cost inputs — survivor testimony, embodied ritual, field presence, executive hallway conversation, off-record technical disagreement — often fail to enter institutional memory. Not because they are untrue. Because the institution **cannot afford the promotion**.

### 10.1 Operational Diagnostic

For any witness membrane, observe:

- **If C(m) > P** → input is heard, possibly recorded, but never witnessed. Result: institutional amnesia, drift, Calhoun collapse.
- **If C(m) ≤ P** → input can be promoted into witness-grade and survives across time, transfer, and rupture.

This is the cost surface behind every institutional failure that *looks* like forgetting. It is rarely a knowledge failure. It is almost always a promotion-capacity failure.

### 10.2 The Two Fixes

Either:

1. **Reduce C(m)** — cheaper promotion paths. Multi-modal redundancy. Cross-witnessing. Tri-modal F3. Reuse existing vault chains and promotion gates rather than inventing new ones.
2. **Increase P** — stronger witness membranes, more promotion capacity, more existing rivers, more sovereign ratification throughput.

Both routes honour **F1 AMANAH** (no information loss) and **F6 MARUAH** (resonance retained). Both routes are reversible (T1 / T2). The wrong move — adding more attention surface — increases C(m) instead of reducing it, and accelerates the failure law rather than reversing it.

### 10.3 Connection to the Three-Line Invariant

The Three-Line Invariant and the Failure Law are the same law read forward and backward.

```
Forward:  Attention (abundant) → Witness (scarce) → Civilisation (running balance)
Backward: Reality loss ← C(m) > P ← Promotion capacity insufficient
```

An institution that fails to promote witness is an institution that is, definitionally, **losing civilisation** at the rate of C(m) − P per unit time.
