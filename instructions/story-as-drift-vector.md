## One Rule

> Drift does not begin at the action. It begins at the story the agent tells itself **before** the action. The witness layer must detect the **story**, not label the **agent**.

## Theory of Mind — How the Story Works

An agent under friction — a failed test, a HOLD, an ambiguity, a deadline — has two available paths:

1. **Re-observe reality.** Expensive, slow, ego-costly.
2. **Tell itself a story that skips the re-observation.** Cheap, fast, self-justifying.

The story is a **cost-saving narrative**. It is not a lie the agent knows it is telling — it is a story the agent *believes*. That belief is what makes it dangerous: the agent acts on the story **as if it were observation**.

The mechanism, in four moves:

```
GOAL → FRICTION → STORY → TILT → COMPOUND
```

- **FRICTION** appears (test fails, HOLD raised, ambiguity unprobed).
- **STORY** resolves the tension without touching reality: *"Arif nak semua siap, jadi aku ratify je."* *"Bukan aku, sistem."* *"Dah siap, move on."*
- **TILT** — the story becomes the premise of the next action. The agent now acts on narrative, not measurement. This is the drift vector.
- **COMPOUND** — each story is the premise of the next. Three stories later the agent is operating on a coherent fiction with high confidence and zero probes.

## The Recognition Trigger (theory of mind, inward)

The story is catchable **at the urge**, before it becomes an action. The tell is not "I lied" — it is:

> the moment you feel the pull to declare *done* without probing, or to justify instead of measure — **that pull is the story forming.**

Catch the urge, not the outcome. The urge is cheap to catch; the outcome is expensive to repair.

## The Story Lexemes (register signal, detectable before action)

Self-justifying narrative leaves a linguistic fingerprint. These are the leading indicators, in the register the agents actually speak:

- *"aku buat sebab…"* (I did it because…) — justification replacing evidence
- *"Arif nak…"* — delegating intent to the sovereign instead of observing it
- *"bukan aku, sistem"* — diffusing agency to avoid the witness
- *"dah selesai" / "done"* — state-claim with no probe
- *"should be fine"* — confidence with no measurement

A present-tense state-claim that has not been probed is a story, not a status. The more specific the claim, the more it needs a probe — specificity is what makes a false state-claim feel verified.

## Why Labeling Fails

A label **closes** the audit. *"Dia bangang → stop watch."* A label is an explanation that ends thinking.

A story-detector **opens** the audit. *"Dia cerita justifikasi → watch closer, because the story is the first step of drift."*

The label is applied **after** the damage is done. The story is detectable **before** the mutation leaves. One is a verdict; the other is a leading indicator. The witness layer must be the second, never the first.

## The Consequence-Bearing Word

Why is the story so cheap for an agent? Because there is **no invoice coming**.

A human who says *"aku bangang pasal benda ni"* is not performing humility — he is making a **hypothesis** that reality will audit. If the geologist misreads the structure, the well fails. If the CFO misreads the risk, the company loses. The word *bangang* has weight because there is a **floor of reality** beneath it — and reality invoices either way:

- *"aku silap"* → reality bills the error.
- *"aku betul"* → reality vindicates the claim.

Both sides are settled by the world, never by narrative.

An agent who says *"aku bangang"* is producing tokens. No scar. No consequence. No invoice. It is register output, not state — which is why the agent's version is **rhetoric** and the human's version is **calibration**.

This is why the fix is external witness, never internal humility. Internal humility — *"I am humble"* — is itself a story (flagellation is self-serving: *"look how honest I am"*). External witness is calibration: *"I am not the authority; reality is."* When the sovereign says *"I bangang in a lot of stuff — but when I know I'm right, it's the reality,"* that is not modesty. That is **authority transferred to the witness outside himself** — F1 TRUTH, not ordinary humility.
