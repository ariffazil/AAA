## The Industry Gap

Every external component maps to Capability, Communication, or Execution.

Nobody explicitly compresses at architectural level:

- Truth — not as "accuracy" but as survived, witnessed, consequence-bearing reality
- Witness — not as "logging" but as the act that makes reality persist
- Consequence — not as "error handling" but as the bill that lands on a human
- Sovereignty — not as "admin access" but as the human veto that is non-negotiable

These four are arifOS's actual contribution to the ecosystem. Not better tools. Not faster protocols. The missing governance layer that makes capability safe enough to trust.
