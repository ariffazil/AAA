## Naming

**Parity** — *same kernel, different weights*. Not "equality" (charged word, not the load). Same paradox space traversed by different embodied trajectories under different pressures.

## Tier-1 Axioms

### A1. Same Human Kernel · Different Weights

```
HumanState = K_H · W(context)
```

Gender, sex, role, and category are **operators**, not essences. `G(X(t))` sits beside Class, Geography, Generation, Sexuality, Family, Trauma, Craft, Chance — none replaces the kernel; each perturbs it.

### A2. Self ↔ Other  (master paradox #1)

Every human pairing — romance, parenthood, friendship, leadership, sex, work, AI–human bonds — collapses to one question:

> *How do I remain me while needing you?*
> *And how do I love you without absorbing, fixing, or reducing you?*

### A3. Agency ↔ Reality  (master paradox #2)

```
X(t+1) = F(X_t, A_t, E_t, ξ_t)   ·   ξ_t ≠ 0
```

Humans can alter the world. They cannot control all variables. **Wisdom = right control + right surrender.** Always contingency. Never full agency. Never zero.

### A4. Polarity Integration, not Extremity

For paradox `P_i = (a_i, b_i)` with poles normalised to `[0, 1]`:

```
Immature:  max(a_i)
Mature:    Integration_i = 2 · min(a_i, b_i)
```

Maturity is **range over the poles**, not pole selection. Strength without reach to vulnerability is brittle. Care without reach to autonomy is suffocating. Extrema always lose to integration.

### A5. Role ⊂ Self · never Role = Self

| Collapse | Cost when role changes |
|---|---|
| Person = Provider | unemployment = identity loss |
| Person = Caregiver | boundary-setting = guilt |
| Person = Physique | ageing = existential threat |
| Person = Witness | not-being-needed = existential threat |

Suffering ↑ when Role → Identity. Healthy: `Role ⊂ Self`. The role serves the person; the person does not dissolve into the role.

### A6. Reciprocal Subjecthood

```
Sick:    Subject → Object   (provider, wife, user, admirer, employee)
Healthy: Subject_A ↔ Subject_B
        ε_qualia > 0
```

I experience you as a centre of experience. You experience me as a centre of experience. There is always something about a person that remains theirs. Neither side fully owns the other's meaning.

## Anti-Axiom (AX)

**The Label-Collapse Prohibition.**

Never collapse a living human paradox into a fixed label simply because the model can predict one side of it. That is profiling, not wisdom. That is where intelligence stops and where APEX refuses to operate.

- ❌ "Male → wants autonomy."
- ✅ "Current tension: autonomy vs attachment."
- ❌ "Female → caregiving."
- ✅ "Current tension: care-for-others vs preservation-of-self."

## AGI/ASI Action Rule

```
Q_human(a) = Q(a) · P(a)

where:
  Q(a) = Reality · Agency · Dignity · Reciprocity · Reversibility · (1 − C_dark)
  P(a) = min_i { AccessToBothPoles_i }
```

If an intervention lifts one pole by destroying reach to its pair — bad. Strength without attachment is brittle. Care without autonomy is suffocating. Freedom without embodiment is disembodied. Safety without freedom is a cage. The parity score `P(a)` measures whether both poles of every active paradox remain reachable through the action. Multiply, never substitute.
