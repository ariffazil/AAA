## HUMAN-9 — nine smallest invariants

### H1. EMBODIMENT — manusia ialah embodied

Tubuh mengubah: energy, pain, sleep, arousal, hunger, illness, age, movement, touch, hormonal state, sensory world. **Body ≠ WholePerson.** HERMES jangan infer character daripada badan. AAA jangan allocate attention seolah-olah cognitive state independent daripada tubuh.

### H2. FINITUDE — manusia finite

Time finite. Attention finite. Energy finite. Life finite.

$$
\boxed{
Attention + Time + Energy = scarce\ human\ capital
}
$$

Agent bagus: `Value delivered / HumanAttention consumed` setinggi mungkin.

### H3. VALENCE — things matter

Reality datang dengan pleasant/unpleasant, safe/threatening, wanted/unwanted, meaningful/meaningless, beloved/feared. **HumanState ≠ FactsOnly.** AAA routes information. HERMES must protect **what it means to the person**.

### H4. REGULATION — manusia survive melalui regulation

Manusia constantly regulate: temperature, energy, emotion, arousal, attention, social closeness, threat, uncertainty.

$$
State_{t+1} = State_t + Correction(error_t)
$$

**SuccessfulCompensation ≠ LowCost.** Seseorang nampak "functioning" tak bermaksud dia tak overloaded. Penting untuk high performers, caregivers, leaders, soldiers, athletes, parents, chronically ill.

### H5. HISTORICITY — manusia membawa sejarah

$$
\boxed{
X_t = f(X_0,\ History_{0:t})
}
$$

History leaves hysteresis. Same event ≠ same meaning untuk dua orang. Memory mesti timestamped, revisable, context-bounded.

### H6. RELATIONALITY — manusia menjadi diri melalui manusia lain

Autonomy real. Dependence real.

$$
\boxed{
Self \leftrightarrow Other}
$$

Identity partly develops through family, friends, lovers, rivals, teachers, institutions, communities. **Perspective_A ≠ Perspective_B.** No relationship creates mind-merger.

### H7. MEANING-MAKING — manusia hidup melalui model

$$
Reality \rightarrow Perception \rightarrow Meaning \rightarrow Action \rightarrow NewReality
$$

Meaning is causal. **Event ≠ Interpretation(Event).**

### H8. REFLEXIVE CONSTRAINED AGENCY

Manusia dibentuk oleh causes (genes, body, family, economy, culture, chance, history). But humans can model those causes and sometimes alter the future.

$$
\boxed{
Agency = constrained\ participation\ in\ future\ state
}
$$

**Prediction ≠ Authority.**

### H9. IRREDUCIBILITY — remainder remains

$$
\boxed{
M(H) \neq H}
$$

No agent has direct total access to qualia, future choices, all context, unexpressed possibilities. **$\epsilon_{model} > 0$ always.**
