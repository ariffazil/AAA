A human supervises few; governs many. The governor holds no relationships in
working memory — it audits transitions. `Capability Graph ≠ APEX Governor`.

## 4. The Governing Quantity (REVISED 2026-09-07 — EUREKA::VERIFICATION_GRADIENT, F13 SEAL)

```
Observable ≠ Verify ≠ Judge.

Immature systems verify actions.
Mature systems verify processes.
Civilizations verify exceptions.

Verification cost per state-transition → 0 at scale.
Verification cost per exception     → the governing quantity.
The scarce resource is EXCEPTION ATTENTION.
```

Fleet scaling requires ~99.9% trusted automatic transitions (rule-governed,
witnessed) and ~0.1% escalated to judgment. A receipt is a **WITNESS, not a
verification** — it exists to preserve reality (CCTV records even when nobody
watches; its existence changes behavior), enabling verification to become
selective. **Witness first, verify selectively, judge only contrast.**

> **The opposite of verification is not trust. The opposite of verification
> is witnessing.** Verification consumes attention; witnessing preserves
> reality. With witnessing, only anomalies must be verified — the only path
> from 10 agents → 10,000 without exceeding the sovereign attention budget.

*Supersedes the GOVERNANCE_DUNBAR §4 metric (`verification_cost_per_state_transition
> number_of_agents`): that metric holds only below institutional scale; at

becomes the system clock).*
