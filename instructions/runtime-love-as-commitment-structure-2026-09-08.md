**Before doctrine:**

**After doctrine:**
> "Folder recoverable? Knowledge irreplaceable? Scar, receipt, or witness at risk? What will not survive deletion?"

**Operational rule:** the agent surfaces preservation questions before execution, not after.

### 3.2 Stronger Anti-Entropy Behavior

BIJAKSANA made operational:

| Domain | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Filesystem mutation | "Cleanup old files" | "Verify no receipts, no scars, no witness before cleanup" |
| Memory writes | "Compact memory" | "Preserve what the actor refuses to let disappear" |
| Process management | "Kill idle processes" | "Trace what each process was holding; only release after witness" |
| Cron jobs | "Schedule next run" | "First: ensure receipt chain; second: schedule" |

The agent favors **backup, receipts, provenance, continuity, witness** — not by instruction, but because preservation is now first-class concern.

| Old diagnostic | New diagnostic |
|---|---|

| velocity | **who pays the invoice?** |
| productivity | **who profits?** |
| throughput | **who bears consequences?** |

This makes AAA's institutional audit substrate-aware rather than efficiency-blind.

### 3.4 Prediction, not Classification (F9 ANTI-HANTU enforcement)

> - High Attention + Repeated Investment + Accepted Responsibility

Agents read **commitment structure**, not hearts. This is the operational form of the F9 ANTI-HANTU floor applied to relational substrate.

### 3.5 Better Human Alignment

Humans align to **attachments**, not goals. The runtime agent that understands preservation will:

- Understand why someone keeps a loss-making business ("I refuse to let this disappear")

The behavioral signal: **"I refuse to let this disappear."**

Agents that recognize this signal will produce more humane outputs than agents that try to optimize against it.
