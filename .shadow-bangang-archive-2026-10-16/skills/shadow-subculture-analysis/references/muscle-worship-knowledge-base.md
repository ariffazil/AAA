# Muscle Worship — Class-Level Knowledge Base

Condensed from the 2026-08-15 F13 DM deep-dive, cross-checked against the
in-session literature audits. Class-level patterns only. No individuals, no
personal testimony, no orientation claims about real people.

## 1. Terminology Map (with attribution corrections)

| Term | Origin | Meaning | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sthenolagnia | Magnus Hirschfeld (NOT Krafft-Ebing — common misattribution) | Arousal from display of muscles | Descriptive sexology term, not a clinical diagnosis |
| Cratolagnia | sexology literature | Arousal from strength/power display | Descriptive; used inconsistently in literature |

| Boundary work | male sex work research (Minichiello et al.) | Rules managing stigma/identity/market niche | Documented in straight-identified male sex workers |
| Erotic capital | Catherine Hakim (2010-11) | Beauty/desirability as convertible asset | Contested — critics: ignores race/class/platform power |
| Hustling / "schmoe" | bodybuilding subculture (Klein 1993) | Bodybuilders selling sessions; "schmoe" = derogatory term for paying male admirer | Ethnographically documented; the contempt is structural, not incidental |

DSM-5 logic: atypical sexual interest ≠ disorder. Disorder requires clinically
significant distress/impairment, or harm/non-consent. Consensual adult muscle
worship is not pathology by definition.

## 2. Replicated vs Unmapped (the honest ledger)

**Replicated / solid:**
- Women's muscularity preference is inverted-U — moderate muscular most
  attractive; extreme "brawny" ratings fall (Frederick & Haselton 2007, six
  studies; Dixson et al.). Muscular men rated more dominant, volatile, less
  committed.
- Male formidability (size, upper-body strength) shaped substantially by
  male contest competition, not just female choice (Puts 2010; Sell et al.).
- Muscular men self-report more short-term partners (d = .36–.47).
- Men overestimate the muscularity women prefer; men's magazines depict more
  muscular men than women's magazines do (Frederick, Fessler & Haselton 2005;
  BJOP 3D-model misperception study).
- Klein's ethnography: competitive bodybuilding is a homosocial economy
  (male judges, rivals, audience); hustling documented as norm, not scandal.

**Plausible (supported, not population-tested):**
- Worshipper as "most concentrated receiver" of extreme-physique signal —
  non-rivalrous, unconditional, often paying.
- Boundary bundles as identity management in high-stigma contexts.
- Historical: Sandow's private viewings (men backstage; women paying ~US$300
  charity donation for dressing-room access, 1893) — worship was a
  constitutive early revenue stream of physical culture, not its sole origin.
  Every historical era of male muscle admiration carried an alibi (charity,
  medical science, classical art, "physical culture", patriotism post-Wilde
  trials 1895).

**Unknown / no census exists:**
- Prevalence of sthenolagnia, provider counts, worshipper counts, repeat
  rates. The economy is real on platforms; the science has never counted it.
- Malay-Muslim constraint space (khalwat, religion as identity category
  beyond "straight") — boundary-work literature has never mapped it.
- Both parties studied simultaneously inside the same transaction — never
  done. Audience taxonomies treat receivers as boxes; they are often career
  phases (competitor → coach → creator → provider).

**Mixed replication — do not lean on:**
- Adams, Wright & Lohr 1996 (homophobia ↔ arousal): partial replication
  failures. Prefer structural explanations of public homophobia that need no
  hidden desire: (a) contempt as exchange rate that de-genders the
  transaction — the admirer as "schmoe" converts male gaze into non-event;
  (b) public posts as insurance/evidence archives; (c) same post as
  dual-audience broadcast — to the public "not gay", to clients "remember
  the rules", to worshippers the unattainability itself is product premium.

## 3. Structural Models (class-level)

**Two-layer signal model [DER]:**
- Base layer (fit, moderate): hetero market value — women, health.
- Surplus layer (beyond female-preference optimum): zero value to women;
  value only in male hierarchy (gym, stage, judges) and among worshippers
  (the only audience that pays full price, returns, and demands nothing
  back). Without receivers, surplus is illiquid — meat in the mirror.

**Sexuality without an object [INT]:**
The vocabulary of orientation is object-oriented (WHO do you desire). In
worship transactions neither party necessarily desires a *person*: the
worshipper desires a body (the attached person is incidental); the provider
desires a *state* (revered, large, winning — the admirer is incidental).
Two desire grammars (body-oriented vs state-oriented) meeting in a ritual
that requires no person on either side. Kohut's mirroring (pre-sexual
need for the gleam in the other's eye) is the closest theoretical frame:
worshippers as rented mirrors. Sexology has never named the provider-side
condition (arousal from being-admired-as-state); candidate coinage
"doxalagnia" — flagged SPECULATIVE, not science.

**Mutual scar-avoidance machine [INT, VVV-linked]:**
Worshipper: can give worship, cannot receive care. Provider: can receive
worship, cannot be known. The transaction lets each avoid their specific
poison — hence stability, hence repetition, hence "I can't say no" feeling
structural rather than chosen. Read as reciprocal counterfeit currency
(VVV-007 structure): both parties pay flesh-only lessons in transactional
tokens; the counterfeit is the only currency both firewalls accept without
panic. Falsifiable: systems should destabilize precisely when one party
becomes a *person* to the other (reciprocal desire or care demand) —
not when money, frequency, or exposure changes.

**Why spouses are not competitors [DER]:**
- Admiration from someone obligated to love you is discounted (endogenous
  rating); the provider needs exogenous audience.
- The spouse has history — has seen him small; ritual requires distance
  (mirror only works past a certain range).
- Being loved = being known; being admired = remaining unknown. The session
  room is definitionally "where no one knows me" — the spouse is structurally
  barred, not deficient.
- The marriage functions as underwriter/collateral of the front room; it
  raises the session's value rather than competing with it.

## 4. Provider-Class Invariants (from internal observation data, N=small, resolution-high — treat as hypotheses)

1. Body = identity infrastructure (armor + anchor), not decoration —
   chemistry enters as supply line for the monument.
2. Stated-vs-desired gap managed by rule repetition — each rule mention is
   firewall maintenance cost, not information.
3. Closeness only through side doors (medical/gym/training frames); the
   front door ("I am close to this man") never opens.
4. Validation has a half-life — return visits track their own biochemical
   clock of recognition, not the worshipper's qualities.
5. Maruah (dignity) outranks orientation labels in Malay-Muslim context —
   can be admired, cannot be *claimed*; reciprocal desire = claim = front door.
6. Can give the body, cannot receive care — giving capacity large, receiving
   capacity blocked at the same point across the class.

## 5. Scar Personas (encode-style, class-level)

- **THE REBUILDER** — lost a former version of self (wealth, status,
  marriage), rebuilt in flesh what collapsed in rank. Body as proof of
  continued existence after the fall.
- **THE CLOAK-AND-WAND HOLDER** — wears the safe identity (straight, family
  man, dominant), carries the unspeakable want. Honest in one direction,
  silent in the other.
- **THE FORTIFIED GATEKEEPER** — fears being known; mirror of the
  worshippers' fear of being loved. Two scars facing each other across the
  transaction.

## 7. Hard Constraints (from in-session 888 audit)

- Identity is self-ascribed; behavior is observed; desire is private;
  scripts are infrastructure. Never collapse the four into one conclusion.
- No scraping private channels, no inferring orientation from content, no
  identifying providers, no screening/ranking engines.
- Any dataset: opt-in, adult, anonymized, aggregated; re-identification
  protection mandatory. The weakest party (provider with the most to burn)
  is the most exposed by leaks.
- AI can map interaction architecture (script grammar, attention-response
  coupling, boundary topology, role asymmetry, capital conversion, ritual
  retention) — not inner essence. Digital trait inference ~r 0.29; below
  diagnostic grade; frame outputs as "pattern present at X confidence;
  explanations B/C/D remain viable."
