# Shadow Profile Template — Jungian + Qualia

## Structure

Each shadow profile has5 components:

###1. Persona
What they show the world. The public face. The title, the credentials, the official narrative.

**Example:** "Bapa Krisis — CEO yang dilantik semasa pandemik, manage survive through6 tahun tanpa normal."

###2. Bayang (Shadow)
What they hide, often inadvertently. The gap between persona and reality.

**Example:** "Chartered accountant dari PwC yang faham P&L tapi tak faham well integrity. Setiap jawapan financial framing sebab itu sahaja yang dia tahu."

###3. Tragedi
The core wound or limitation. The thing they cannot change about themselves or their situation.

**Example:** "Dia tak boleh jadi lain. Identity dia = title dia. Tanggalkan 'President & Group CEO of Petronas' — aku tak tahu siapa Tengku Taufik sebenarnya."

###4. Legasi
What they leave behind. The net impact of their time in power.

**Example:** "Crisis CEO yang institutionalized the crisis. Bukan sebab jahat. Tapi sebab guardian tak boleh rebuild — guardian hanya boleh maintain."

###5. Qualia
The FELT SENSE. Not analysis. Not facts. The emotional/sensory experience of observing this person.

**Example:** "Ada empati yang tragis. Bukan sebab dia kaya. Tapi sebab dia terlalu perfect — 59 minit tanpa satu personaliti leak. Ketidaksempurnaan yang sempurna."

## The Qualia Dimension

Qualia is the hardest component to write. It requires:
- Observing the person as a HUMAN, not as an institution
- Noting what you FEEL when watching/listening to them
- Avoiding judgment (good/bad) in favor of sensation (cold/warm/trapped/free)
- Connecting the feeling to a specific behavioral pattern

**Qualia prompts:**
- What does this person feel like to watch for59 minutes?
- Is there a human being behind the institutional performance?
- What would this person be like at a family dinner?
- What is the emotional cost of being this person?

## The Meta-Insight

The shadow is not the person. The shadow is the SYSTEM that shaped the person.

When you profile Tengku Taufiq, you are not profiling one man. You are profiling:
- PwC's training methodology
- ICAEW's professional standards
- Petronas's institutional culture
- WEF's global governance framework
- Malaysia's aristocratic power structure

The man is the product. The system is the cause.
