# Institutional X-Ray Methodology

## When to use

When the user shares a corporate or government communication (video, transcript, speech, interview) and wants to understand what is happening underneath the public performance.

## The5-Layer Analytical Stack

### Layer1: Transcript Decode (F2 Truth)
- Run youtube-multimodal to acquire transcript
- Extract what was SAID (explicit claims, numbers, framing)
- Extract what was NOT SAID (silences, omissions, deflections)
- Extract HOW it was said (code-switching, precision patterns, deflection formulas)

### Layer2: Incentive Mapping (7-Layer)

| Layer | Question |
|-------|----------|
| 1. Narrative Inoculation | What attack is being pre-empted? |
| 2. Soft Warning | What is being prepared for without saying so? |
| 3. Policy Seeding | What future policy change is being normalized? |
| 4. Institutional Mythology | What origin myth is being constructed? |
| 5. Market Signal | What message is for international audiences? |
| 6. The Silences | What topics are deliberately absent? |
| 7. Venue Choice | Why THIS channel, not another? |

### Layer3: Physical Constraint Audit (7 Questions)

| # | Question | Why |
|---|----------|-----|
| 1 | Physical Burnout Rate | Paper commitments vs actual inventory |
| 2 | Sourcing Arbitrage | Actual landed cost premium |
| 3 | Planning Horizon Collapse | What triggers emergency rationing |
| 4 | Counterparty Insolvency | When non-dominant players fail |
| 5 | Capex-Dividend Cannibalization | Extraction before maintenance threshold |
| 6 | Industrial Cascade | 2nd-order effects beyond energy |
| 7 | Demand Destruction Math | Price elasticity vs political constraint |

### Layer4: Cognitive Typing (MBTI)
- Map communication patterns to cognitive type
- Identify: what type they APPEAR to be vs what they ARE
- Key insight: wrong type, wrong seat, wrong time

### Layer5: Shadow Profile (Jungian + Qualia)
- Persona: what they show the world
- Shadow: what they hide
- Tragedi: the core wound or limitation
- Legasi: what they leave behind
- Qualia: the FELT SENSE of this person

## The Meta-Rule

Human observers ask: Did he give a good answer?
Institutional X-ray asks: **What physical constraint is he preventing me from calculating?**

Every corporate communication is a firewall. The job is to identify what it is protecting, what physical reality it obscures, who benefits, and what happens when reality becomes undeniable.
