## Key clauses (direct quotes)

### SC4 — Tenant aircond maintenance
> "The Tenant shall be responsible for the repair of electrical bulbs or starters and to regularly clean or service and keep the air conditioners supplied to the premises in good working conditions during the tenancy period. To service all air-conditioners once every 6 months. A copy of the service contract or receipt to be forwarded to the Landlord."

### SC5 — Repair cost split
> "The Tenant shall be responsible for all minor repairs and routine maintenance (including but not limited to drain, basin or sink blockage) not exceeding RM200 per item per repair throughout the term of this tenancy. For any repairs exceeding RM200 per item per repair, the cost may be shared equally (50/50) between the Landlord and the Tenant, subject to mutual agreement and discussion prior to the repair being carried out. However, any damage or repairs required due to the Tenant's willful act, negligence, or default shall be fully borne by the Tenant regardless of cost. This clause shall not be enforced during the first 30 days from the commencement date of the tenancy."

**Key interpretation**: SC5 governs "repairs" and "routine maintenance" only. Replacement of a unit that cannot be repaired = capital replacement, not a "repair". The RM200/50-50 split does not apply.

### 3(f) — Fair wear and tear
> "To keep the said Premises and all the Landlord's installation therein in good and tenantable repair and proper working order, fair wear and tear is accepted."

**Key interpretation**: Age-related failure = fair wear and tear = excluded from tenant responsibility.

### Schedule B — Fixtures in good working condition
> "The said Premises is attached with the following fixtures and fittings that are in good working condition"

**Key interpretation**: Landlord warrants fixtures are in good working condition at handover. If an AC unit was already degrading at move-in, landlord breached this from day one.
