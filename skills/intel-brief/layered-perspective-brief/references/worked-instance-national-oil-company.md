# Worked Instance — a national oil company's four layers

> The procedure in SKILL.md applied end to end. Use it as the shape of a finished answer: one
> prose block per layer, each carrying its own evidence class, closing with falsifiers. Reuse the
> STRUCTURE for any institution; re-probe the FACTS, which move.

## Layer 0 — the read itself (institution)

Separate near-term cash from structural trend before saying anything. Cash reserves and a healthy
headline profit are a near-term fact; a declining production trend under a rising LNG line is the
structural one. Say both, and say which one the headline is hiding.

Then the **timing test**: list the action, the stated cause, and whether the stated cause was
actually present when the action was taken.

| Action | Stated cause | Present at the time? |
|---|---|---|
| A large workforce reduction | Weak prices / falling profit | No — announced while profits were healthy and prices were high |
| Assets farmed out to partners | Portfolio optimisation | Partly — the entitlement given up is far larger than the word implies |

Where the stated cause is absent, it is not the cause. Name the real one (here: a permanent
industry-margin compression) and show the timing that proves it. Consequence worth stating plainly:
a price recovery does **not** reverse it, because price was never the driver.

## Layer 1 — the group (staff)

Segment by mechanism, not sentiment — see the staff table in SKILL.md's shape. The instrument that
matters here is a placement form offering "remain" versus "register interest in exit". Registering
interest is not accepting, but it is visible to the panel that decides placement, so the answer is
strategic. **Read the form as a mechanism, not as a measurement.**

Union posture is a separate layer with its own history: an existing deadlock between management and
the state-chapter union predates the reduction, so attributing the conflict to the reduction
inverts cause and effect.

Bias to declare in one clause: review-site scores and forum threads are self-selected and mostly
predate the event; union statements are advocacy; no internal survey is visible to you at all.

## Layer 2 — the public

The public does not see the company; it sees a pump price, a regional grievance, and "these staff
are comfortable". Name those three surfaces and where the institution stands on each.

Then the **transmission channel**, which is the actual stake:

```
reduced dividend -> narrower federal fiscal space -> thinner fuel and power subsidy -> price at the pump
```

The household never experiences the institution; it experiences the last link. Say the chain and
say which link carries the damage. Mention volume for this topic spikes at announcement moments and
then goes quiet — **quiet is not resolved**, the topic has simply migrated to a frame where the
institution is invisible.

Where an ethnic or regional entitlement anxiety runs through the discourse, treat it as a live
frame that shapes politics — and do not convert it into a prediction.

## Layer 3 — the sovereign

He is an insider of the institution in question: disclose it. He is also knowable only through what
he built — the models, dossiers, open letters and articles in his own archive. Say that out loud,
mark the reading as a reading, offer the alternative reading, and invite correction. Never a
psychological verdict; never "the silence means X".

## Closing — falsifiers, then one question

Name the two to four observable events that would break the read, and state that all of them sit
outside anyone's control:

1. The apex court on the merits of the governing statute (leave granted, merits undecided).
2. The new organisation structure / operating day.
3. The next quarterly results.
4. The state election that turns on the resource question.

Until one lands, anyone claiming to know how this resolves — including you — is unverified. Then at
most one question, and only if the answer changes what you bring next.

## Probe list (never recall these; re-fetch each time)

- Court posture and any new orders
- Regulatory/aggregation questions between the national and state bodies
- Farm-out, JV and licensing-round outcomes
- Production, LNG volume and dividend figures

Primary documents (company media releases, exchange announcements, the court's own orders) outrank
wire copy. A wire's characterisation of a legal status is a paraphrase; a wire's pairing of two
figures from different bodies is an argument.
