# Family Group Advice Pattern — verified 2026-08-15 ("Dear NABILAH" group)

**Trigger:** sovereign asks the agent to give advice *directly to family members* in a
family group chat (e.g. "Bagi nasihat kat Nabilah and Arif Fazil"). The agent is
speaking TO third-party humans in the room, not briefing the sovereign.

## Before writing a word — reconstruct the people

Never advise strangers. Pull person cards first (T0, ~2 reads):

1. `/root/memory/people/FAMILY/*.md` — family person cards (nabilah-fazil.md etc.)
2. `/root/memory/H4-identity/FAMILY_FAZIL.md` — family structure, roles, F5 epistemic status

Extract for each person: **role in the family system** (not MBTI — function),
**their own recorded words** (doctrinal pattern tables carry verbatim quotes),
**what they carried during the last crisis**.

Known facts this session (verify against files, don't trust memory alone):
- Nabilah = younger sister ("Akak" is the sisters' honorific among the girls, NOT
  elder — Arif is anak sulung). Family coordinator: clinics, schedules, shielded
  the youngest from illness news, "Saya tolong duit jugak, abg bendahara".
- Arif = "abg bendahara" (eldest = resource holder). Proof of love has defaulted
  to money; presence is the deficit.

## Output shape (verified — group accepted it, "Omgg so esfp" = it landed)

1. **One line of license** — "sebab Arif suruh aku cakap, jadi aku cakap ja."
   The agent names WHO authorized it before advising. Without this the advice
   reads as a bot lecturing the family.
2. **Advice per person, labeled** — one short paragraph each, second person
   ("hang"), grounded in their *observed* role from the cards, not generic wisdom.
   For the coordinator: "orang yang urus orang lain selalu takde siapa tanya balik".
   For the provider: presence > transfers.
3. **Advice for the pair jointly** — name the system, not the individuals.
   Use the family's OWN recorded doctrine back at them ("kita kan satu team" —
   team trains weekly, meets not only on match day).
4. **Length cap ~120 words.** It's a group chat, not an essay.

## Follow-up turn: the personality-label reply (ESFP/INTJ)

When the group answers with a personality label ("Omgg so esfp"), do NOT re-lecture:

- One light line owning it (bot gets typed too — roll with it, mild self-deprecation).
- At most ONE real observation, softly (caretakers test "E something").
- If the sovereign then replies with a single frame word — **"Thermodynamics"** —
  that is the register he wants: physics metaphor for family dynamics. Answer IN
  the metaphor, 2–3 lines: one entropy generator / one reducer / closed system
  drifts without deliberate energy transfer / each side must learn to read the
  other's silence. No emoji spam, one ⚛️ at most.

**Rule:** single-word steering from Arif = switch register instantly, keep it
under 3 lines. He grades the frame, not the content.

## Pitfalls

- ❌ Assuming "Akak" = elder sister (it's the sisters' honorific; Arif is eldest).
  Arif already corrected this once (2026-08-15, in the person card).
- ❌ MBTI-psychologizing the family. Labels come from THEM ("so esfp"); the agent
  may riff once when invited, never diagnose.
- ❌ Quoting the medical/WhatsApp source material in the group. Cards carry F5
  detail — surface the *pattern* (coordinator, bendahara), never the transcript.
- ❌ Long-form advice in a group chat. If it exceeds a phone screen, it's a DM,
  not a group reply.
