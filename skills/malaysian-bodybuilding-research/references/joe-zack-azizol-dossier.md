## Training Journey

- **School days:** 48 kg body weight — labeled a "drug user" (penagih) by authorities during a roadblock due to extreme thinness
- **Confidence hit:** describes himself as having very low self-confidence at school
- **Gym start:** post-school, learned by YouTube + imitating other gym-goers. "No widespread social-media knowledge sharing then" — i.e. pre-Instagram era in M'sian fitness scene
- **4-month transformation:** achieved desired physique in first 4 months of consistent gym work
- **Diet discipline:** cut fast food (his prior favourite), disciplined eating patterns
- **Coached phase:** recruited by two gym-owner trainers who refined his technique and nutrition
- **Coaching role:** now coaches "pelapis baharu" (new generation of athletes) — passed through what he learned
- **Injury history:** foot injury from gym routine — did not stop training
