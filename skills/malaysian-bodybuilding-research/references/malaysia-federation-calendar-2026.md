## Notes on specific federations

### IFBB Pro / NPC Malaysia
- Promoter: Hugo Chan (globalclassicmy@gmail.com)
- 2026 has 4 Malaysia shows: May JB, Jul KL Natural, Oct KL (Pro Qualifier)
- Pro Qualifier awards 8 Pro Cards — most prestigious Malaysian NPC event of 2026

### WFF Malaysia
- Active on Facebook (/wffmalaysia) and Eventbrite
- Unique categories: Denim Jeans Model, Bermuda (not found in other federations)
- 3 Malaysia events in 2026: Jun Battle of Titans, Sep Jeans & Bermuda, Dec Omniverse (TBC)

### PCA Malaysia
- First-ever PCA Pro Qualifier in Asia — Oct 11 at KLPAC
- Strong UK-based federation with growing Asia presence (Indonesia, Thailand, Philippines)
- Categories include Mixed Disability and Mr Class (height-based like bodybuilding but less extreme)

### INBA/PNBA Malaysia
- Drug-tested (WADA compliant) — one of the few with international-standard testing
- Unique category: Cosplay Fitness
- 2026 events already completed (Jul); no Aug+ events announced

### WNBF Malaysia
- President: Sim Chun Hean; VP: Edward Chuah
- Muscle Mash International Championships is their flagship — date TBA for 2026
- 780+ athletes registered, 29 Pro Cards earned, 17 educational seminars

### MBBF / WBPF
- National federation under Olympic Council of Malaysia
- Runs Mr Malaysia state-level → national pipeline (Mr Sabah → Mr Sarawak → Mr Malaysia)
- Malaysia hosting 58th Asian Championships? No — Bangkok hosting; Malaysia competing
