### Evolution / mating science
- **Puts 2010, "Beauty and the Beast" (Evol & Human Behav)** — male
  formidability traits (size, upper-body strength ~90% greater, 80% more
  arm muscle mass) shaped primarily by MALE CONTEST competition, not
  female choice. Deep voices/facial hair signal DOMINANCE to men more
  than attractiveness to women. Men 60% more lean mass; dimorphism rivals
  gorillas.
- **Frederick & Haselton 2007, "Why Is Muscularity Sexy?" (PSPB, 6 studies)**
  — inverted-U: moderate muscularity most attractive; "brawny" drops.
  Muscular men rated sexier but more dominant/volatile/less committed.
  Short-term partners more muscular than other partners (d=.36–.47).
  Muscular men: more lifetime partners, more affairs (partial r .20–.28).

### Sociology / history
- **Klein 1993, "Little Big Men"** — ethnography, elite SoCal gyms
  1979–86. Bodybuilding audience = MEN. "Hustling": economic rationale +
  psychological ("appreciated"). Klein's frame: narcissism/homophobia/
  hypermasculinity — later critiqued as over-pathologizing, closet-logic
  (Ellex/queer-sport-history critique). Meta-ethnography (~20 studies)
  finds broader motives: self-discovery, community, coping, enjoyment.
- **Sandow (1890s)** — private post-show viewings; men touched/"fingered"
  muscles; women paid ~US$300. Fig-leaf photos posed/lit for male buyers.
  Ziegfeld planted fake het romances (Lillian Russell). Post-Wilde-trials
  repackaging: "imperial manliness" — homoeroticism kept, label changed.
  Mr. Olympia trophy = Sandow's own body (Dying Gladiator pose).
  ⇒ Worship was CONSTITUTIVE to one major early revenue stream; "industry
  born FROM worship" = HYPOTHESIS, not proven sole origin.
- **Kinsey** — behavior/thought/feeling not aligned; identity ≠ behavior.
  Kinsey scale critiqued for conflating attraction+behavior.
