# Petronas-Petros Leak Case (Mohd Khairul Akmal)

**Source: Malaysian court reports (Malay Mail, The Edge, Free Malaysia Today), July-Aug 2026.**

## Case Details

**Defendant:** Mohd Khairul Akmal Jasni, 41. Former Business Performance Unit Manager at Petronas. Applied for Head of Upstream Finance position at Petros (monthly RM25,000 salary).

**Alleged Offence:** On June 8, 2024, between 3:19pm and 3:21pm at Jalan Pinang condominium, attempted to send confidential document via his work email to two senior Petros officers.

**Document Leaked:** "Q1 2024 Upstream Business Performance – Operational and Financial"

**Legal Charge:** Section 203A(1) Penal Code read with Section 511 (attempting to disclose confidential information).

**Whistleblower Timeline:** Complaint received by Petronas via whistleblower email channel on Dec 6, 2024 at 10am — SIX MONTHS after the alleged incident.

**Testimonies:**
- **Laila Badriah Razali** (Petronas Senior Manager for Complaint Management): Received complaint from complainant's personal email. Confirmed document was sent from Khairul's work email to Petros CEO/CFO email accounts.
- **Jumsuri Basri** (ex-Petronas IR Manager): Testified that check revealed Khairul sent confidential Q1 2024 upstream document to Petros senior officers on June 8, 2024 at 3:19pm.

**Context:** Petronas-Petros dispute over gas rights in Sarawak is ongoing. Sarawak government granted full gas rights to Petros in 2024. Petronas challenged this at Federal Court (Jan 2026).

## Why This Matters for Structural Analysis

The leaked document title ("Upstream Business Performance – Operational and Financial") indicates:
- Production volumes per field/asset (actual, not public estimates)
- Revenue per barrel breakdown
- CAPEX allocation per upstream asset
- Operating margins per PSC
- **Cost per boe** — most sensitive upstream metric

If these numbers showed weakness (aligns with dimensional audit findings: revenue -17%, capex collapse -23%, net capex above depreciation collapsed 72%), then Petros — as Sarawak's proposed gas aggregator — would have had leverage against Petronas in negotiations.

## Key Insight

This is NOT just an employee leak case. It's evidence that:
1. Petronas internal performance metrics were concerning enough to potentially jeopardize ongoing negotiations with Petros
2. An insider who was applying for a job at the rival company attempted to share internal data
3. The document covered Q1 2024 — BEFORE the current FY2025 results confirmed the deterioration trajectory we documented

The leak attempt reveals what Arif suspected: Petronas's upstream financial health was worse than public disclosures suggested.
