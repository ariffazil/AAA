# WEALTH Four-Layer Intelligence (DEEP_RESEARCH_PLUS_WEALTH)

Arif's Master Prompt, ratified 2026-08-06. Applies to every federation research deliverable. He explicitly said: "Give this to every federation agent."

## Core rule
NEVER PRODUCE A REPORT. PRODUCE FOUR STACKED LAYERS.
Failure conditions (any one = FAIL): facts only · opinion only · financial model only · narrative only.

## Layer 1 — OBSERVED REALITY (OBS only)
- Answer only: what can be proven?
- Output: facts, timeline, financials, governance, projects, partnerships.
- No interpretations, no conclusions, no adjectives, no strategic language.
- Completion test: every claim must survive deletion of all opinions.

## Layer 2 — DEEP RESEARCH (DER only)
- Question: what patterns exist inside the facts?
- Build: trend analysis, dependency analysis, capital allocation map, power map, partner map, timeline map, concentration map.
- Explain how the system works. Not whether it is good or bad.

## Layer 3 — WEALTH (WEALTH tools)
- Required outputs: capital_health, capital_diagnose, capital_entropy, capital_primitive — covering NPV, governance capacity, veto concentration, responsibility ledger, beautiful mouse, stress index.
- For every tool: report input, output, coverage, known defects.
- If a tool result conflicts with evidence: flag VERDICT_CONFLICT. Do not resolve it.

## Layer 4 — HUMAN COGNITIVE READ (INT only)
- Not "what happened?" Ask: who benefits? who pays? who decides? who can veto? who bears consequences?
- What assumption must be true? What would falsify the thesis? What remains unverifiable?

## Mandatory sections
1. WHAT THIS REPORT GOT WRONG — the report attacks itself: missing evidence, alternative explanations, control-set gaps, tool blind spots, unknowns.
2. CONTROL SET — every score answers "compared to whom?" Forbidden: metric → verdict. Required: metric → peer distribution → percentile → verdict.
3. BINDING CONSTRAINT — exactly ONE load-bearing variable, stated as "Binding Constraint: <variable>" with the reason every other risk flows through it.
4. OMEGA QUESTION — reduce the entire report to one uncomfortable question that survives after all numbers are removed.

## Final verdict format
OBS / DER / INT / UNKNOWN + BINDING CONSTRAINT + FALSIFICATION TEST + OMEGA QUESTION.
Never output BUY/SELL/STRONG BUY/SEAL.

## Motto
"Do not tell me what the asset is. Tell me what the asset is hiding."

## "How does institution X lie via statistics" pattern (Arif, 2026-08-08)

When Arif asks *"how institution lies on that statistik"* / *"how X institution fudges"* — the answer is **NOT pure reasoning**. The right move is to **drive the analysis through WEALTH MCP tools**, specifically `capital_entropy(mode='metric_purpose_audit')` + `capital_entropy(mode='power_consequence_map')`. The tool's design IS the answer — it audits declared purpose vs measured KPIs vs excluded outcomes. If the audit returns INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE, that verdict IS the proof: the institution's stated purpose doesn't align with what it measures.

**Workflow (proven 2026-08-08 on Malaysia DOSM GDP question):**
1. Feed `capital_entropy(mode='metric_purpose_audit')`:
   - `declared_purpose`: the institution's stated goal (e.g. "Measure welfare of all Malaysian citizens")
   - `kpi_list`: the institution's actual KPIs (GDP, manufacturing, exports, median income)
   - `excluded_outcomes`: what the institution doesn't measure (informal economy, base year lag, E&E concentration)
2. Tool returns `verdict: INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` + `_w0_evidence_gate.gate: CAUTION` + 3 reflection questions: *"Are the KPIs measuring the purpose, or have they become the purpose? / What outcomes are excluded from measurement? / Would the behaviors change if the KPIs were removed?"*
3. That INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE is the institutional-lie receipt — the auditor refused to score because purpose/metric misalignment would be a lie.
4. Then deliver bahasa-manusia prose around it: "DOSM tak tipu dengan dusta. DOSM tipu dengan apa yang dia TIDAK ukur." Pair the tool verdict with the 4 DOSM-specific mechanics (advance-vs-final revision drift, informal economy 24.5% unmeasured, base year 2015 outdated, E&E concentration 40% hidden).

**The four DOSM-specific lie mechanics** (Malaysia 2026 case, see `references/malaysia-economic-data-sources.md` for sources):
1. **Advance vs final revision drift**: advance estimate 7 weeks after quarter, actual ~1 month later. Q1 2026 advance=5.3% → actual=5.4%. Pattern: release conservative, revise upward → PM gets a "surprise positive" headline.
2. **Informal economy unmeasured**: shadow economy = 24.5% of GDP (World Economics 2026); informal workers = 3.45M (53.2% in informal sector per DOSM own survey); 40% workers have no EPF/SOCSO. GDP 5.8% is measured against the formal 75% only.
3. **Base year lag**: constant 2015 prices — structure has shifted (digital, AI, services); deflator doesn't reflect. India had the same issue (Peterson Institute 26-3: deflator lag caused 2.5% real overestimation).
4. **Concentration hidden in sectoral averages**: E&E = 40% of exports, top 3 partners = 50%. GDP "diversified" but revenue captures flow to one sector, mostly foreign-owned (Intel, AMD, Micron, Infineon).

**Pattern verdict shape (use this when reporting the audit):**
- Quote the tool verdict verbatim (`INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` + gate CAUTION + reflection questions).
- Plain-language BM/EN read: "system tak salah, tapi system's design memang buta kepada 24.5% ekonomi."
- BINDING CONSTRAINT = the misalignment mechanism (base year + informal + concentration), not a number.
- OMEGA QUESTION that survives number-deletion: *"Macam mana nak govern negara yang statistical system-nya buta kepada 24.5% ekonominya?"*

## Malaysian SOE / national-corporate analysis pattern (Arif, 2026-08-09)

When Arif asks about a Malaysian SOE (Petronas, TNB, Telekom, PNB, etc.) or says *"focus in [company] yang BANGANG bagi lingkup negara sekali"*:

1. **Frame national-political, not just corporate.** Arif never wants pure balance-sheet analysis for SOEs. The question is always: how does this entity serve the state's political economy? What is the feedback loop between government fiscal position and the SOE's financial structure? Start with the government's fiscal need, THEN model the SOE's position.

2. **Stale data audit — check what official data exists BEFORE building a model.** For Petronas: last official group financials = FY2025 annual (Feb 27, 2026). No Q1 2026, no H1 2026 released. If the user says *"2026 ni satu data rasmi financial apa pon x Dak"* — they're right. Model assumptions must be explicitly labelled UNKNOWN where 2026 data is missing. Never build a confident trajectory model on FY2024/2025 data as if it's current.

3. **SPV debt is ON-balance-sheet (not off-book).** Malaysian SOEs use Labuan SPVs (PETRONAS Capital Ltd, PETRONAS Global Sukuk Ltd) to issue guaranteed notes. Under MFRS/IFRS 10 consolidation rules, these SPVs are wholly-owned → debt appears on Group balance sheet. When Arif says *"Tiada off-balance sheet shenanigans di sini"* — treat this as ABSOLUTE. The ACTUAL insight is NOT that debt is hidden off-book. It's that:
   - Parent company may be net debt while Group shows net cash (parent borrows, cash lives in subsidiaries)
   - GMTN/MTN programs are FACILITIES with unused capacity — report both drawn AND capacity
   - Financial guarantees (RM12.9B for Petronas) are disclosed in notes, NOT on balance sheet
   - The $30B GMTN program is NOT "off-balance-sheet debt" — it's unutilized capacity that COULD become debt
   - If the user asks *"berapa banyak off balance sheet debt"* — they mean "show me the facility capacity" OR the contingent guarantees, NOT claim there's hidden off-book debt

4. **Net debt trajectory modeling (SOE-specific):**
   - Start with actual audited cash (from balance sheet, NOT from memory or estimate)
   - Include BOTH on-balance-sheet borrowings AND note the contingent/program capacity
   - Model government dividend pressure as a SCENARIO variable (moderate vs heavy vs wreckage), not a fixed number
   - The crossover question: when does the government's fiscal hole force the SOE to draw on hidden capacity?
   - Key variable: Brent crude vs Budget assumptions (Malaysia 2026: Budget assumed $60-65/bbl, actual $85-90)

5. **Subsidy-PETRONAS feedback loop (Malaysia-specific):**
   - High oil = higher PETRONAS CFFO (good for SOE) BUT higher subsidy bill (bad for gov fiscal)
   - This creates a paradox: oil price up helps SOE profitability but hurts the government's net fiscal position
   - BUDI95 quota cuts = subsidy relief signal; full removal = politically toxic pre-PRN
   - Watch: Monthly subsidy burn rate announcements from MoF / Dewan Rakyat

6. **Output format:** Deliver in BM/EN mix (Arif's natural code-switch). Lead with the answer, not preamble. Tables for verified data. Plain-language verdict in everyday Malay. Never start with "Here is" or "Let me" — just give the answer.

7. **DIMENSIONAL ORTHOGONAL AUDIT (mandatory for deep-dive requests):**
   When Arif says "dimensional orthogonal financial audit" or asks for deep structural analysis,
   run 6 orthogonal dimensions and cross-check for contradictions:
   - **Dimension 1: Treasury & Bonds** — drawdown/repayment/net borrowing, bond issuance vs prior year
   - **Dimension 2: Cash Flow Trace** — CFFO vs CAPEX vs dividend vs net borrowing (where did money go?)
   - **Dimension 3: CAPEX vs Depreciation** — net investment above depreciation, capital commitments trend
   - **Dimension 4: OPEX Structure** — cost of revenue, admin, other expenses, financing costs vs revenue delta
   - **Dimension 5: FX Translation** — OCI translation losses (hit equity not P&L), currency impact
   - **Dimension 6: Dividend vs Capacity** — payout ratio, FCF after dividend, dividend/CFFO
   
   **Orthogonal cross-check rule:** All 6 dimensions must tell a consistent story. If they contradict
   (e.g., "has cash but still borrowing"), that contradiction IS the finding — trace it to the root cause.

8. **GROUP vs COMPANY (PARENT) DISTINCTION (mandatory for SOEs):**
   NEVER report only the consolidated group position. Always extract BOTH:
   - Consolidated Group balance sheet (all subsidiaries included)
   - Company (Parent) balance sheet (standalone parent entity)
   
   For Petronas specifically:
   - Group may show net cash RM80B+ while Parent shows net debt RM5-18B
   - Parent's RM101B in intercompany receivables are NOT truly liquid
   - Parent borrows via Labuan SPVs while cash lives in subsidiaries
   - This circular financing pattern is the KEY structural insight for SOE analysis
   
   When user asks "is X in net debt?" — answer with BOTH group and parent positions.

9. **BOND PROCEEDS TRACING (forensic technique):**
   When bond issuance is detected, trace the proceeds through cash flow statement:
   - Was it refinancing (repaying maturing bonds) or net new borrowing?
   - Did proceeds go to CAPEX (investment) or dividend/liquidity (extraction)?
   - If CFFO surplus exists but borrowing still occurs → parent-level liquidity problem
   - Cross-check: revolving credit drawdown changes (doubling = short-term liquidity crunch)
   
   Key question: "If company has RM85B CFFO and RM204B cash, WHY borrow RM14B net?"
   Answer pattern: group cash trapped in subsidiaries, parent needs external funding for dividends.

10. **ASSET MONETISATION DETECTION (SEARAH/JV/farm-in pattern):**
    When SOE announces JV formation, asset transfers, or farm-ins:
    - Check if assets reclassified as "held for sale" in AFS
    - Determine if JV is "financially self-sufficient" (borrows on its own → off parent balance sheet)
    - Distinguish: SALE (cash in) vs ORBITAL TRANSFER (asset moves, exposure remains)
    - Connect to parent net debt: is monetisation covering dividend obligations?
    
    For Petronas: SEARAH JV (Eni 50:50) = orbital transfer of RM30.3B upstream assets.
    EnQuest farm-in US$833M = direct cash injection. Both serve to clean parent leverage.

**Pitfalls:**
- **Don't use estimated/derived cash figures when audited balance sheet is available.** Petronas FY2025 cash is RM204.4B (audited), not RM175B (estimate). Always extract from the actual balance sheet in the AFS PDF, not from FRA summaries or memory.
- **Don't confuse "program capacity" with "outstanding debt."** The $30B GMTN program is capacity. The drawn amount is what's on the balance sheet. Report both.
- **Don't frame as "corporate analysis" when the user wants "national scale."** Arif's "laiing negara sekali" means include government fiscal, subsidy, political economy — not just the SOE.
- **Don't report group net cash as the SOE's true position without checking parent level.** Circular financing (parent borrows, cash lives in subsidiaries) means the group number is misleading. Always check both levels.
- **NO fabricated off-balance-sheet narratives.** When Arif says "Tiada off-balance sheet shenanigans di sini" — that is a HARD STOP. All SPV debt consolidated under MFRS/IFRS 10 is ON-balance-sheet. Labuan SPVs (PETRONAS Capital Ltd, PETRONAS Global Sukuk Ltd) are wholly-owned → consolidation required → debt sits squarely on Group balance sheet. Do NOT frame parent-level liquidity stress as "hidden leverage via SPV trickery." Parent liquidity ≠ group solvency. Both can coexist without any accounting fraud or off-book classification. When the sovereign pushes back on your structural framing, BACK UP — cite the actual consolidation rule, not speculative architecture.
