## Lessons encoded in `federated-patch-seal`

1. **Peer audit before merge is mandatory.** OpenClaw caught 3 architectural bugs in Kimi's patch that would have shipped.
2. **Kimi may auto-commit while you're waiting for opencode.** Check git log before assuming nothing happened.
3. **opencode may hit step limit.** Don't respawn — finish the remaining 20% manually.
4. **The F13 gate is bilateral.** When agent blocks, report. When Arif says "seal", execute. No middle ground.
5. **Probe-variant methodology** (4-variant matrix) is the only way to isolate a type-shape bug from a logic bug.
6. **`forge_vault(mode="receipt")` requires URL elicitation unless actor_id="ARIF".** SOVEREIGN actor bypasses the elicitation step.
