# Tersurat/Tersirat Writing Technique

## Definition
- **Tersurat** = what is explicitly written (surface layer)
- **Tersirat** = what is implicitly understood (deeper layer)

## Application in Political Writing

### Explicit Layer (Tersurat)
- Corporate governance critique
- Data, numbers, facts
- Specific allegations with evidence
- Legal/regulatory references

### Implicit Layer (Tersirat)
- Systemic critique
- Patterns of exploitation
- Power dynamics
- Historical parallels (without naming)

## Example: PETRONAS Letter

**Tersurat (explicit):**
"CEO dapat extension kali ketiga walaupun prestasi merosot"

**Tersirat (implicit):**
"Corak eksploitasi yang berulang — satu pihak sentiasa mengambil, satu pihak sentiasa memberi"

The reader understands the deeper meaning without it being stated directly.

## Pitfalls

1. **Direct naming** — Can be libelous. Use "satu pihak" or "seorang" instead of names.
2. **Literal analogies** — Geopolitical comparisons backfire. Use structural patterns instead.
3. **Too subtle** — If the implicit layer is too hidden, readers miss it. Balance is key.
4. **Cultural sensitivity** — Some metaphors resonate differently across cultures. Test with diverse readers.

## Validation
- Ask: "Can a layperson understand the explicit layer?"
- Ask: "Can an educated person infer the implicit layer?"
- Ask: "Is there plausible deniability for the implicit layer?" (important for legal safety)
