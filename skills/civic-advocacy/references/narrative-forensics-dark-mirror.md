## The three-layer epistemic ladder (binding)

| Layer | Meaning | Example (PETRONAS rightsizing) |
|---|---|---|
| [EVIDENCE] | Directly observable | "it is a necessary one"; "polycrisis driven by global pressures"; "fairly, objectively, systematically"; "market-competitive package — exceeds minimum statutory requirement"; 5,000 staff / 10% framed as the story while hiring-freeze-until-Dis-2026 buried |
| [INTERPRET] | Reasonable hypothesis | Communication shows narrative-amplification structure; AI-assisted writing is one plausible mechanism |
| [UNKNOWN] | Not knowable from outside | Whether the CEO personally uses ChatGPT/agents; whether internal 555 (boardroom verification) is genuinely dead |

**Rule:** EVIDENCE is claimable. INTERPRET is defensible when framed as hypothesis. UNKNOWN must be stated explicitly — never bridge EVIDENCE → "therefore AI" without the alternatives list.

## The fallacies (what Arif corrected)

1. **Consistency ⇒ AI.** Alternatives producing identical symptoms:
   - Centralized comms department
   - Consultant-produced narrative pack
   - Top-down talking points
   - Strict media training
   - Tight corporate comms governance
   Verdict: consistency is a *symptom*, not a *fingerprint*.

2. **"333-only institution."** From outside you can only say: "public output shows 333 dominance and little visible 555 friction." The boardroom may still host verification you cannot see. Claim what the evidence supports; label the rest INTERPRET.

3. **Tool-hunting.** "Does the CEO use ChatGPT?" is a weak forensic question. The strong question is structural: **where is friction still allowed to live?** / **can the org produce its best argument against itself?**

## The measurable signature — cost of variation

- Human PR: every surface variant costs (write, review, edit). Variation cost is high and *nonlinear*.
- AI-assisted: cost of variation → ~zero. One template, many channels, cheap surface adaptation.
- **Test:** corpus-wide lexical-frequency sweep. Look for: one euphemism spelled identically across all channels; jargon clusters peaking uniformly; numbers always selected to support the narrative; high repetition + low independent variation.
- Instrument: cadence-monitor (`petronas-cadence-monitor-findings.md` in the atlas) — not vibe.

| Signal | Human (high variation cost) | AI-assisted (variation cost ≈ 0) |
|---|---|---|
| Variant phrasing across channels | High (per-writer style) | Low (one template) |
| Jargon cluster distribution | Spread by department | Dense, uniform |
| Euphemism spelling | Varies by context | Identical everywhere |
| Numbers with context | Sometimes | Always selected to support narrative |

## Propaganda: broadcast → adaptive

- Broadcast: one message printed a million times (printing press → radio → TV → social).
- LLM: a million personalized variants, one per soul. Each looks like a personal conversation, not propaganda.
- Consequence: detection by "sameness" fails; detection must measure *adaptation cost* instead.

## The strategic frame — fight the euphemism, not the AI

- Every euphemism is the price an institution pays to avoid reality.
  - "rightsizing" exists because "retrenchment" carries implications it cannot face
  - "polycrisis" (external cause) vs "our decisions" (internal cause)
  - "market-competitive package" (fear of being called cruel)
  - "remove work that is not necessary" (because "lay off workers" is too honest)
- Each euphemism is a small confession: *I fear my own reality.*
- Counter-weapon: plain language. MakcikGPT translates "rightsizing" → "buang orang." The translation IS the friction.

## The decisive 555 test

> Can the organization produce its best argument against itself?

- Yes → system still has self-correction; friction lives.
- No → self-correction function is dead; failure arrives before the numbers admit it.
- When internal friction dies it migrates outside (journalists, MakcikGPT, open letters, citizens). That migration is not an attack — it is a transplant of a dead organ into a living body. [DER]
