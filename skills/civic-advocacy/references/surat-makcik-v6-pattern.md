## The 12-paragraph Marhaen structure (load-bearing)

Use this as the spine. Each paragraph has a specific emotional + rhetorical job.

| # | Job | Opening line pattern |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Introduce the makcik | "Nama saya Makcik. Saya tidak penting..." |
| 2 | Why writing | "...bukan sebab saya faham ekonomi. Saya menulis sebab saya tidak boleh tidur lagi." |
| 3 | Specific weekly scene | "Saya isi minyak setiap Khamis. RM30 seminggu..." |
| 4 | The pain (children/family) | "Saya ada anak. Lelaki. Umur 32..." |
| 5 | Connect pain to institution | "Dia kata nak kerja dengan PETRONAS, Makcik. Dahulu..." |
| 6 | Universal paradox (no naming) | "Saya tak benci siapa. Saya cuma nak tanya..." |
| 7 | One killer number | "...saya cuma faham satu angka — 70.5 peratus..." |
| 8 | Three Kerusi critique | "Saya tahu tiga orang yang duduk di kerusi..." |
| 9 | Single ask in human language | "...satu benda yang Parlimen boleh buat tanpa kos..." |
| 10 | Why writing again | "Saya menulis bukan sebab saya berani. Saya menulis sebab saya penat." |
| 11 | Connect to silent majority | "...saya nampak mereka di pasar pagi. Di klinik. Di surau." |
| 12 | Closing for THEIR CHILDREN | "Saya nak anak saya boleh tengok muka saya di meja makan malam tanpa perlu buat-buat kuat." |

Total: ~1500 words. 6 pages A4. Fits in one sitting for a tired MP.

## The killer number — how to present it

Don't lead with arithmetic. Lead with the OBJECT.

**DON'T:** "PETRONAS FY2025 payout ratio = 70.5%, calculated as RM32.0B / RM45.4B × 100..."

**DO:** "saya cuma faham satu angka — 70.5 peratus. Nombor tu muncul dalam banyak tulisan. Nombor tu bermaksud: pada tahun kewangan 2025, daripada setiap RM100 yang PETRONAS untung, RM70.50 keluar sebagai dividen sebelum sempat bernafas. Tinggal RM29.50 untuk negara. Untuk anak-anak kita."

Then the BODY experience:
> "kalau saya ada RM100 di poket, dan RM70.50 keluar sebagai dividen kepada orang lain sebelum saya sempat bernafas, saya bukan lagi punya RM100. Saya punya RM29.50."

This is the makcik doing the math by hand. Reader follows.

## The Three Kerusi pattern (load-bearing for institutional critique)

When critiquing Malaysian institutional failure, name THREE chairs. The triangulation creates structural force.

For each chair, give:
1. **Name + title**
2. **Amanah given** (what the position requires)
3. **Amanah dikhianati** (how it was violated, with verified facts)

**Critical:** Don't attack character. Attack STRUCTURE.

**Critical 2:** Each dikhianati claim must be arithmetically/sourced verifiable. Wrong facts collapse the whole chair.

### Piercing question per chair (use sparingly)

| Chair | Question |
|---|---|
| CEO | "kalau dia ada ibu — apa ibu dia rasa?" |
| Chairman | "apa yang dia nampak apabila dia tengok cermin?" |
| Shareholder | "siapa yang dia takut sebenarnya? Rakyat, atau pasaran?" |

These questions make the chair a HUMAN, not a target. The reader does the rest.

## Voice rules — anti-AI-feel checklist

Apply BEFORE writing. If any check fails, rewrite.

- [ ] No "Sebagai warganegara yang prihatin" — use "Nama saya Makcik"
- [ ] No "Saya menulis dengan rasa rendah hati" — use "Saya menulis sebab saya penat"
- [ ] No big data tables in body — use one killer number
- [ ] No corporate closing — use "Itu saja. Terima kasih sebab baca surat panjang ni."
- [ ] No "kami menggesa" — use "saya mohon dijawab"
- [ ] No "kami menuntut penjelasan" — use a piercing question
- [ ] Sign as "Makcik — seorang warganegara Malaysia", NOT full name + IC
- [ ] No bullets in body — use prose
- [ ] No jargon (payout ratio, PAT, leverage, fiscal year) without immediately translating
- [ ] At least 3 SPECIFIC physical scenes (not abstract "rakyat biasa")

## The single ask

Don't make it a list. Don't make it technical. Make it ONE thing the recipient can do without cost.

For PETRONAS: "Sediakan satu undang-undang yang jelas: bila mana-mana syarikat milik negara membayar dividen lebih daripada 65 peratus daripada keuntungannya, syarikat itu wajib isytihar secara terbuka..."

Notice the WHY of 65%:
> "Garis 65 peratus itu sudah pun wujud dalam amalan — kerajaan sendiri sudah turunkan dividen FY2026 ke RM20 bilion. Apa yang tiada ialah pagar perundangan supaya garis itu kekal."

This is the makcik's gift: she noticed that the 65% line already EXISTS in practice (because the gov already cut to RM20B). She's just asking for the legal fence.

## What NOT to do (learned the hard way)

1. **Don't lead with charts** — v4 had 5 visualizations and was rejected as "too data-heavy". One killer number > five charts.
2. **Don't write formally polite** — v5 was a perfect surat rasmi and was rejected as "feels AI". Polite = AI to F13.
3. **Don't put AI stuff in the header** — kill the human voice immediately.
4. **Don't name CEO Bakke PMX in body** — name them ONCE in the ask if needed, otherwise let the reader infer.
5. **Don't use bullet lists** — bullets read as corporate. Prose flows.
6. **Don't sign with IC number** — sign as "Makcik — seorang warganegara Malaysia".
7. **Don't be self-deprecating** — "rendah hati" reads AI. Be tired, not humble.

## When to skip this and use v5 (surat rasmi) instead

Use v5 (full formal protocol) when:
- The recipient is a SPECIFIC committee chair (e.g., PAC Chairman Mas Ermieyati)
- F13 has explicitly asked for "rasmi" or "formal"
- The audience is MPs who expect Malaysian government correspondence conventions
- F13 wants NAIO awareness (formal CC list works better)

Use v6 (full Marhaen soul) when:
- The audience is the general public
- F13 wants to reach through emotion, not protocol
- The artifact will be shared on social media / WhatsApp
- F13 wants the makcik voice to carry

Use v4 (chart-heavy with Marhaen voice) when:
- The audience is sophisticated (think tanks, analysts)
- Data visualizations are needed for credibility
- F13 wants both emotional AND analytical reach
- 26-page SOVEREIGN briefings (proven pattern)
