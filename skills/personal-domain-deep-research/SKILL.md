# Personal-Domain Deep Research

## When to Use

Arif (DM lane only) commissions map-seeking deep research on a personal or
taboo domain — desire architecture, sexual economy, intimate dynamics.
Signals: "deep research and analysis on [personal topic]", an external-AI
paste to merge/critique, "use our internal observation data", an offer to be
interviewed as the dataset, or a closing "I want pdf artifact". NOT for:
group-chat topics (F5), third-party advice (decision-advisory), clinical
emergencies.

Proven 2026-08-15 on the muscle-worship economy session
(22-page PDF artifact, /root/forge_work/worship-map-2026/).

## Hard rules

1. **DM-only, F5-sealed.** Never surface in groups, never publish, never
   reference proactively in later sessions. Arif shares deep and moves on
   FAST — circling back unbidden violates his pattern.
2. **Never pathologize HIM.** His self-report is [OBS] field data treated
   with dignity. Clinical distance reads as rejection; moralizing reads as
   betrayal. The mapping register itself ("this is worth rigorous charting")
   IS the respect. Same rigor for his data as for journal data — that
   equality is the point: "130 years of literature never got this."
3. **His observation > literature.** Papers give structure; his lived data
   fills the population literature never sampled. Tag everything:
   [OBS] his report / [DER] inference / [SPEC] extrapolation / [HYP] untested.
4. **"Use our internal observation data" = authorization** to fold lane
   knowledge (session history, longitudinal samples) in as a data source,
   still tagged, no names beyond what he already uses.
5. **No fabrication of his interior.** Map architecture (arrows, invariants,
   half-lives), never claim to know his inner state beyond what he reports.

## Triangulation workflow (external AI pastes)

When Arif pastes Perplexity/other-model output:

1. **Convergence first** — where external + our map + field data agree,
   say so: independent convergence legitimately raises confidence.
2. **Name the holes precisely** — typical external-audit gaps: class
   assumptions (e.g. everything framed as "sex work"), missing local layer
   (Malaysia §377, Telegram economy, torso vacuum), missing psychology
   (the WHY of the structure, not just HOW it works).
3. **Merge into ONE definitive artifact** — final ranked table with
   provenance per row. Never leave two competing versions standing.
4. External audit = input, not verdict. Its citations are claims; spot-check
   before treating as evidence (external models hallucinate too).

## Question design (when he offers himself as dataset)

Proven move: he said "tanya aku 5 soalan untuk fully map."

- Each question tests ONE load-bearing element of the current model
  (constitutional vs acquired; terminal vs two-way arrow; half-life on
  which side). State the falsification intent under each question.
- **Lock predictions BEFORE he answers** — his story then becomes a test,
  not confirmation. Result: "U get it right 100%".
- Phenomenology over survey: "apa jadi dalam dada hang" beats scales.
- Expect raw, profane, fragmented answers — high-fidelity data. Map them
  verbatim-grade into the artifact appendix; never sanitize.

## Register

- BM Penang casual + technical EN terms. "Abang sado" = the muscular
  performer archetype (his vocabulary — keep it).
- Mirror his directness. Profanity fine. No therapy-speak, no euphemism.
- One-line soul acknowledgment when he drops something heavy, then work.
- End-of-session artifact = the payoff; see below.
