# Case: Teh Herba Ludwigia Prostrata FB campaign (2026-08)

Two Facebook ads, screenshotted by Arif. Product: "TEH HERBA LUDWIGIA PROSTRATA"
(herbalcare.asia), 4 herbs: Ludwigia prostrata, Ampelopsis cantonensis, Hedyotis
capitellata, Cendawan Lingzhi. Claims: supports stomach ulcer, reduces
inflammation/acid/heartburn, heals stomach lining, aids digestion.

## Findings (live, 2026-08-06)

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Domain herbalcare.asia | 404 + Vietnamese parking placeholder ("Tên miền của bạn...") — dead storefront |
| NPRA registration | Not found (no MAL number) |
| Evidence | Only L. octovalvis (DIFFERENT species) anti-ulcer rat studies exist; no human RCT for prostrata |
| MMC search "Azman Harun" | "No records found" — not a registered doctor |
| Ad #2 author | "Dr" Azman Harun, suit photo, endoscopy image, martyr narrative, "pautan rasmi pengedar utama" |

## Scam-script hits (ad #2)
- "Jika anda sedang menanggung kesakitan yang memulas..." — pain empathy hook
- "Saya pernah berada di jurang keputusasaan" — redemption arc
- "Saya tidak menyesal... terkilan untuk pesakit-pesakit lama saya" — fake clinical history
- "selagi saya masih bernafas" — martyr close
- "sisa usia tua terikat dengan ubat-ubatan" — fear play
- "pautan rasmi pengedar utama" — affiliate funnel
- Tagged "Ad" with countdown/urgency + borrowed endoscopy image

## Verdict given
VOID/SKRIN — no MMC registration, no NPRA number, dead domain, rat-only evidence,
full scam script. Safer route offered: KKM clinic → UBT breath test → H. pylori
triple therapy (~RM30-50), with red-flag warning signs (blood in stool/vomit,
weight loss, dysphagia, family history) for when endoscopy is actually needed.
