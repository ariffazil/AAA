## Workflow

1. **Classify the ask.** "What does it mean / map them / why do they X" → this skill. If it is a *decision* under uncertainty, also load `counseling/decision-advisory`.
2. **Pull VVV first.** Read void entries; identify archetypes in play (ARMOR_AS_IDENTITY, STATED_VS_DESIRED, FORTIFIED_HEART, COUNTERFEIT_EXCHANGE…). Map them onto the *other* party unless he asks about himself.
3. **Literature pass.** Search before synthesizing. Hunt: ethnographies, evolutionary psych (who the signal's audience is), clinical (minority-prevalence), sexology history (pre-modern origin), platform/market studies. Record exact numbers with citation anchors.
4. **Build the map in three moves:** invariants (what never moves across individuals) → axes (state vector, low/high poles — never a fixed "type") → scar personas (archetype names, VVV-style).
5. **Name the lexical gap.** Where the domain has no word, coin one, tag `[SPEC] coinage`, and note the gap itself is data (unnamed = unstudied).
6. **Declare falsifiers.** Every structural claim gets "what observation kills this". A map without falsifiers is F2 risk.
7. **Cultural delta.** Western literature describes Western constraint architecture. Before concluding, enumerate what the local frame changes (Malaysia: maruah as the guarded category beyond "straight", adat display-suppression, khalwat risk, no exit ramp → deeper repeat economy, Telegram-first).

## External-AI paste → delta analysis (recurring pattern)

When Arif pastes a Perplexity/ChatGPT/sibling answer for cross-examination:
1. **Validate honestly first** — list what it got right (he pastes it because it carries citations).
2. **Enumerate the structural deltas it cannot see** — usually: (a) it resolves ambiguity as a bug when ambiguity is the *product*; (b) its rankings are population-generic, not segment-split (married/religious vs single); (c) it misses local honor-architecture (who can ask, who must never be seen asking, which platform carries the asking); (d) it treats safety framing as the goal when the system runs on managed risk.
3. **Ship the merged map**, not a rebuttal. Don't relitigate what was already agreed.

## Void-question design (when he says ask me N questions)

- **One pre-declared discriminator per question** — know which hypothesis each answer kills vs keeps.
- **NEUTRAL VOCABULARY PITFALL (validated by external critique this session):** do NOT embed your theory's coinage (terminal arrow, counterfeit, half-life) inside the question — the respondent narrates himself in your vocabulary and the answer confirms nothing. Ask in raw sensory/experiential language (apa jadi dalam dada), interpret afterward.
- Sequence least → most sensitive; permit fragments and tak tahu.
- Post-answer: score every hypothesis honestly (SUPPORTED / PARTIAL / KILLED / STILL OPEN). A testimony that kills half the map is a *win* — report it as one.

## Privacy laws (hard)

- DM-only domain. Persistent files (skills included) carry **patterns + published numbers only** — no names, places, or biographical identifiers from testimony, ever.
- Never infer or assert any individual's orientation/diagnosis; hold identity ≠ behavior ≠ desire ≠ commercial role as four separate layers.
- When advising conduct toward third parties (see reference manual): consent, discretion, non-entitlement, easy-refusal, clean-exit frame — care inside the system's constraints, never a key to bypass them.

## Pitfalls

- **Homophobe = secretly gay** — Adams 1996 showed a phobia-arousal link but replications are mixed. Analyze the *structure* (contempt as exchange rate; posting as insurance) without asserting hidden desire. The system runs on the question staying unanswered.
- **Sampling effects** — worship-grade physiques are chemically selected (FFMI ceiling ~25 natural); observation on that sample measures the selection pipeline, not natural traits. Folk causality is usually backwards.
- **Dysmorphia-default** — real but minority-clinical; not the universal explanation for extreme building.
- **Two-instrument minds** — apparent contradictions (confident public display + private inadequacy; homophobia + male clients) are *engines*, not paradoxes. Resolve by finding the two ledgers and their separate auditors.
- **Don't collapse the four layers** (identity/behavior/desire/commerce) into one verdict — that collapse is the mistake every pop analysis of these domains makes.
