| Compound | Dose | Frequency | Form |
|---|---|---|---|
| **MOTS-c** | 5–10mg subq | 5 days on / 2 off | Injectable |
| **NAD+** | 100–250mg subq | 2–3x/week | Injectable |
| **CoQ10 (Ubiquinol)** | 100–200mg oral | Daily with fatty meal | Capsule |
| **Magnesium Glycinate** | 400–600mg elemental Mg | Night, 30min before bed | Capsule |

**Cycle:** MOTS-c 4–6 weeks on, 2–4 weeks off (avoid receptor desensitization).

**Why these four work together** — Mitochondrial ETC assembly line:
```
MOTS-c (AMPK signal: "produce more ATP")
  ↓
NAD+ → Complex I → CoQ10 → Complex III → Cyt C → Complex IV → ATP
                                                ↓
                              Mg²⁺ cofactor for ATP synthase (Complex V)
                              ATP only biologically active when bound to Mg (Mg-ATP)
```

**Analogy:** NAD+ = petrol, CoQ10 = fuel pump, Mg = engine block, MOTS-c = accelerator foot. Skip any one → chain breaks.

## Specific Compound Notes

### MOTS-c
- 16 amino acid mitochondrial-derived peptide
- Activates AMPK → fat oxidation, glucose uptake, ATP production
- Half-life in plasma <1–2h but downstream AMPK effects persist
- **NOT a pre-workout stimulant** — exercise-mimetic with cumulative effect
- WADA Section 4.4 — banned in competitive sport
- **Insomnia management**: Inject AM (<10am). Metabolic activation into evening hours = core temp elevated = blocks deep sleep.

### NAD+
- Dose ladder: 25mg sensitive → 50mg starter → 100mg maintenance → 250mg upper → 500mg clinical only
- First 1–3 doses: flushing, mild headache (transient, vasodilation)
- Take AM — energy boost causes insomnia if evening
- Hydrate 500ml before/after injection
- Subq bioavailability ~60–70% vs IV
- "Sensitive" individuals start 25mg

### Semax & Selank
- Russian-developed, clinically studied in Russia
- Available as: nasal spray (clinical) AND injectable (grey market)
- **Default to injectable when discussing with Malaysian gym crowd** — they import injectable
- Standard subq dose: 0.1ml each
- Often paired (Semax = focus, Selank = calm — non-sedating anxiolytic)

### CoQ10 (Ubiquinol)
- **MUST take with fat-containing meal** — fat-soluble, needs fat for absorption
- Ubiquinol form (reduced) > Ubiquinone for bioavailability
- 100mg = maintenance; 200mg = therapeutic

### Magnesium Glycinate
- 400–600mg elemental Mg per day
- Glycinate = best absorbed, least laxative vs citrate/oxide
- 30min before bed → calming effect from glycine + Mg-ATP activation
- ATP only biologically active when bound to Mg (Mg-ATP complex)

## Pitfalls

### PITFALL: Don't expand beyond user's source
Already documented above. Hard rule.

### PITFALL: MOTS-c timing — AM, not pre-workout
If user reports insomnia on MOTS-c, fix timing first, not dose. Inject <10am. The metabolic activation persists into evening hours; core temperature stays elevated = blocks deep sleep.

### PITFALL: Subq vs IV vs Nasal
Three delivery routes exist for many peptides. Ask user which they use BEFORE discussing dose, half-life, or bioavailability. Default for Malaysian gym crowd = subq injectable.

### PITFALL: Injectable vs Clinical form
Russian Semax/Selank clinical form is nasal spray. Grey market injectable is 0.1ml subq. Different PK profiles. Don't assume the user uses the clinical form.
