# Pre-Stage Wisdom — 30 Minutes Before Stepping on Stage

Reference content for athlete prep card / poster. 10 things every amateur bodybuilder needs to know in the final 30 minutes before stage call. Battle-tested, no fluff.

---

## The 10 (pre-stage execution)

1. **Pump-up window = 90 min before call.** 5 min light cardio (jog in place, jumping jacks) + 5 min push-ups/burpees. Do not overdo — burned glycogen = flat muscles on stage. Stop at 60 min before.

2. **Water sip — last 200ml at 30 min before.** Empty stomach + flat midsection + visible vascularity. After that, sip only to wet mouth. Bathroom plan must be locked in.

3. **Tan second coat = 10 min before call.** Thin layer over the first base coat. Smooth it with a damp cloth or latex glove. Do not overpaint. Touch-up only — blotchy patches from sweat will show.

4. **Pose rehearsal with eyes closed.** 60 seconds per mandatory pose: front lat spread, side chest, rear lat spread, side triceps, front double biceps, most muscular, abs & thighs. Feel the contraction — muscle memory beats visual mirror.

5. **Mandatory pose order matters.** Lead with **front lat spread** (creates biggest visual impact first). End with **abs & thighs** (judges remember the last image). Save most muscular for the middle.

6. **Stage walk = 3-second hold on first pose.** Confident eye contact with the judges. Shoulders back, chest up, slight smile. No rushed transitions.

7. **Breath control = vacuum.** Exhale fully, hold 5 seconds. Use during transitions. Coach will cue if you trained it.

8. **Carb sip = 50ml honey water or 2 glucose tabs at 20 min before.** Tight muscles, full look. Skip if you know your body reacts with bloating.

9. **Don't compare yourself to others in pump-up room.** Each athlete's peak is different. Stay in your own prep. No staring at neighbors.

10. **Trust your peak.** If you got here, you did the work. Last 30 minutes = execute known variables. No experiments.

---

## Post-stage recovery (within 1 hour of stepping down)

- Water + electrolytes (500ml with pinch of salt + squeeze of lime).
- Carbs: white rice / banana / honey / sports drink. Glycogen tanks are empty.
- Protein: 30g within 60 min (whey shake or eggs).
- Reverse diet starts the next day. Slow carb/calorie climb — do not binge.
- Sleep 8+ hours tonight. Cortisol is high, recovery is priority.

---

## The "don't do" list

- ❌ No new foods at peak week. All meals 3+ weeks before.
- ❌ No new supplements at show day. Caffeine timing stable since prep week.
- ❌ No social media during waiting. Anxiety feeds comparison.
- ❌ No arguing with judges or organizers. Decisions stand.
- ❌ No alcohol for 48 hours post-stage. Muscle protein synthesis tanks.

---

## Coach corner (for the trainer standing with athlete)

- Cue pump intensity, not pose count. Quality > quantity.
- Remind about posing routine timing if music routine involved.
- Final water check: clear urine or close to it. If athlete looks dry, give 50ml max.
- After last pose: **"Breathe. Relax. Walk tall."** The judges saw the work. Don't second-guess.
