## The Seven Layers

### 1. Animal Biology — Intrasexual & Intersexual Selection

- Handicap principle (Zahavi 1975): costly signals = honest signals. Badan stage-day = display yang mampu bayar cost yang lain tak mampu.

### 2. Hormonal — Testosterone
- 19-carbon steroid → androgen receptor → mTOR → muscle protein synthesis
- Testosterone drives: muscle mass, bone density, aggression, dominance, sex drive
- Primate analogy: alpha male = biggest → social defeat suppresses subordinate test
- Badan = weapon + shield + psychological warfare

### 3. Physics of Muscle
- F = ma: more cross-section (hypertrophy) = more force capacity
- Mechanical tension = primary stimulus for hypertrophy (weight = force × distance)
- Thermodynamics: muscle = most metabolic expensive tissue (~13 kcal/kg/day maintenance)
- Surface area to volume ratio: bigger bodies cool differently → thermoregulation = fitness signal
- Wolff's Law: bone adapts to mechanical loading → permanent record of training history

### 4. Display Theory — Lek Mating

- Judges/audience = choosers. Athletes = lekking males.
- Bodybuilding = HYPERSTIMULUS: beyond natural survival limits (6-8% BF, supraphysiological mass)
- Supernormal signal: more extreme than wild displays
