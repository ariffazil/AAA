# MOTS-c Peptide — Session Knowledge (2026-08-20)

Condensed from SADO-group + Syed DM sessions. For future MOTS-c / peptide questions.

## What it is
Mitochondria-derived peptide, 16 amino acids, identified USC 2015. Activates AMPK ("metabolic master switch"). Exercise-mimetic: exercise and fasting raise MOTS-c naturally; levels decline with age.

## Evidence tiers (be honest)
- Mechanism (AMPK, fat oxidation, glucose uptake): SOLID — cell + animal
- Exercise capacity (old mice running capacity up): MICE only
- Insulin sensitivity: mice + 1 small Korean human trial
- Fat loss / longevity in humans: NO large RCT
- WADA: banned in competitive sport (metabolic modulator, S0/4.4 — Korean athlete case 2024)

## Half-life (the question Syed actually asked)
- No solid human PK study exists. Do NOT fabricate a number.
- Small peptide in plasma: cleared fast, rough estimate under 1-2 hours
- BUT the EFFECT is not tied to plasma presence — AMPK/metabolic cascade persists for hours
- This is why protocol is daily injection, not pre-workout window dosing
- Practical: inject 30-60 min pre-workout is fine timing-wise; NOT a stimulant kick; effect is metabolic, not CNS

## Sleep interference mechanism (why "tido boleh cuma x lena")
- AMPK activation raises metabolic rate → core body temp doesn't drop enough at night → deep sleep requires core temp drop → light sleep dominates
- Fix protocol: inject AM only (before 10am), glycine 3g + magnesium glycinate before bed, warm shower 1h before sleep (paradoxically accelerates heat loss), cool room, no caffeine after 2pm
- Dose 0.1mg is far below the 5-10mg studied protocol range — sleep impact at this dose suggests either high sensitivity or impure product
