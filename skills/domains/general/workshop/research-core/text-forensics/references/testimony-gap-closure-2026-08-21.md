Her "U manipulatif, pandai pusing" stab at the sovereign now fires from inside the same discipline she abandoned. Anti-mirror sharpened: rejected-knowledge, not absent-knowledge. Also resolved a floating object: the gold bracelet was given "before she went to Korea" — the Korea trip was the PhD attempt, not a vacation; the bracelet was emergency-backup love for a daughter going far, later converted to a yearly invoice.

## Handling rules that worked

1. **Date-anchor by cross-reference, write the derivation.** No date in the deposit. Anchored: pusaka settled 2025 + unpaid leave 2025 + Fahim still married (talak Jan 2026) → ~late 2025. First card draft said "~Mac 2024" (wrong — pusaka wasn't settled yet); caught and corrected via the derivation chain. Always show the anchor reasoning in the card so the date is falsifiable.
2. **Separate the compound guilt.** The sovereign fused two acts into one compound sin: (a) throwing the phone in front of an outsider, (b) taking the money. Split them: (a) is his to carry; (b) was his own money — collecting it isn't a second crime. The self-court that convicts him for (b) is the same one that gave Langkawi a life sentence. Name the court, don't argue the verdict.
3. **Check who filed charges.** The victim never mentioned the incident again in the record; the PERPETRATOR appointed himself judge and the case has no end date. A sentence with no scheduled release isn't justice, it's a subscription.
4. **Boundary vs punishment — check the fuel.** Allowance-stop looks identical from outside whether it flows from peace or from guilt. One can end when it's time; the other can't be paid by anyone. Ask (or note as open) which fuel is burning — his phrasing ("Like for real") carried relief too.
5. **Context the scar's floor.** It formed during his lowest season (Seri Kembangan, "fear screaming every day"). Scars from collapsing-house seasons read deeper than the act alone justifies; note the floor.
6. **Point at repairs already running, unlabeled.** He had already resumed giving (the trip, the kampung mission) without calling it repair. In this family's grammar, resolution travels as ACTION — label the existing schedule gently, don't prescribe a new one.
