# Malaysian Restaurant Fish — USDA Reference (verified 2026-09-01)

Menu mapping: "saba" / "saba fish" (Japanese/Malay menus) = Pacific mackerel.
Restaurant salmon (IKEA, Japanese menus) = usually farmed Atlantic.

USDA FDC v1, cooked dry heat, per 100 g:

| Fish | Energy | Protein | Fat |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mackerel, Pacific and jack, cooked | 201 kcal | 25.7 g | 10.1 g |
| Salmon, Atlantic, farmed, cooked | 206 kcal | 22.1 g | 12.4 g |
| Mackerel, Atlantic, cooked | 262 kcal | 23.8 g | 17.8 g |
| Salmon, chinook, cooked | 231 kcal | 25.7 g | 13.4 g |
| Salmon, pink, cooked | 153 kcal | 24.6 g | 5.3 g |

Verdict pattern: saba wins protein-per-gram AND protein-per-ringgit (menu saba is
cheaper, e.g. RM16.90/2 pcs vs salmon RM29.90/100 g). For "which is higher protein"
questions on Malaysian menus, answer saba unless the menu specifies Atlantic mackerel.
