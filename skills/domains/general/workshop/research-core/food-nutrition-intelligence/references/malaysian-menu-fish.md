# Malaysian menu fish — species mapping & benchmarks

Derived 2026-09-01 from a SADO-group menu photo (saba RM16.90/2pcs vs salmon RM29.90/100g).

## Species mapping for USDA queries
| Menu label | Actual species | USDA query |
|---|---|---|
| Saba / Japanese mackerel | Pacific mackerel (Scomber japonicus) | `mackerel Pacific cooked` |
| Salmon (restaurant) | Atlantic, farmed | `salmon Atlantic farmed cooked` |

## Cooked, dry-heat benchmarks (per 100g, USDA FDC)
- Pacific mackerel: 201 kcal, 25.7g protein, 10.1g fat
- Farmed Atlantic salmon: 206 kcal, 22.1g protein, 12.4g fat
- Atlantic mackerel (alt): 262 kcal, 23.8g protein, 17.8g fat
- Chinook salmon (alt): 231 kcal, 25.7g protein, 13.4g fat

## Verdict pattern
Saba wins on protein per gram AND protein per ringgit at typical Malaysian menu pricing. For gym-bulk nutrition questions (SADO group context), lead with saba and mention the price gap.
