# Worked example — PETRONAS chairman & President/CEO lineage (1974 to present)

> Use as a model of the spine table and source-status discipline, and as the
> factual starting point for any PETRONAS leadership question. Every row is
> VERIFIED on at least two public sources or explicitly flagged; keep the flags.

## The office pair

| Office | Appointed by | Function |
|---|---|---|
| President & Group CEO | PM / Board, from senior internal ranks | Runs the company |
| Chairman | Prime Minister (sole shareholder = Minister of Finance Inc) | Heads the Board; not the operator |
| Chairman **& Chief Executive** — 1974-1976 only | PM | Executive, cabinet-equivalent standing |

## What this example teaches

1. **The eleven-year interregnum (2004-2012) is the headline finding, not a
   missing name.** After the sitting chairman died in office, the chair was
   carried in an *acting* capacity by two successive Presidents/CEOs, and a
   substantive non-executive appointment only resumed in July 2012. Any search
   for a "chairman" inside that window returns nothing — the hole is the fact.
2. **The appointment mechanism changed twice.** Executive chair with
   cabinet-equivalent standing (1974-76), then a chair often drawn from the
   executive or the civil service, then from 2012 a non-executive appointment on
   a 3-year contractual cycle. Read the mechanism before judging the holder.
3. **Personal compensation is not symmetrically disclosed.** Individual pay is
   not published as a listed company must; board composition, directors' fees
   and meeting allowances appear in each Integrated Report's governance
   section. Write NOT FOUND rather than inferring a figure.
4. **Fan-out as applied:** one subagent per holder or era-cluster (1974-76,
   the two chair-and-chief holders, the 1980s chairmen, the long CEO tenures,
   the three recent chairmen), one separate task for the money lane, each task
   carrying the mandatory citation clause. The spine above was fixed by hand
   first so every child worked against a stable office/tenure map.
