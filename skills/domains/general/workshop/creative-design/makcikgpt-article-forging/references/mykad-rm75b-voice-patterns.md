## Voice Pattern: "Multi-Story Register" (serialised evidence rooms)

When one deal/corruption has multiple actors, timelines, or layers — don't flatten into one essay. Instead, **number each story as its own room**. Each room gets its own heading, its own evidence box, its own emotional beat.

**Structure:**
1. **Cover + hook** — lead with the human cost ("RM7.5 bilion, diminta secara lisan. Macam beli ikan kat pasar.")
2. **Cerita Pertama → Cerita Kelima** — each story is self-contained but builds on the last
3. **Each story gets a `fact-box`** — primary source citation, Bursa filing / SC record / court doc
4. **The thread between stories is structural, not chronological** — room order = escalation (not timeline)

**Room order logic for this article:**
| Room | What | Escalation |
|------|------|------------|
| 1: Mesyuarat Tanpa Kertas | How the deal was requested (verbally) | The method is the crime |
| 2: Tuan Baru | Who's buying it (Ishak Ismail + insider trading history) | The buyer is compromised |
| 3: Perang Dua Anak Murid | Internal power struggle (Farhash vs rival) | Both camps are Anwar's orbit |
| 4: Yang Nak Kena Kubur | Active scandals (RM303.7m frozen accounts, police reports) | GLC status would bury them |
| 5: Bau Lama | Historical precedent (Zahid RM6m cheques) | The pattern repeats |

**Why it works:** Each "cerita" functions like a chapter — reader can stop after any one and still get value. But reading all five reveals the full picture. This is better than one long essay for civic pieces because:
- It respects the reader's time (each room is ~300 words)
- It builds like an indictment (method → person → conflict → crime → pattern)
- Each fact-box is a standalone evidence artifact

**Anti-pattern:** Don't number stories if the topic doesn't have distinct actors/timelines. If it's one thread, use existing patterns (Accept Then Attack, Institutional Entropy Map). Multi-story only when there are genuinely separate rooms.

## Voice Pattern: "Soalan untuk Ahli Parlimen" (Parliamentary Accountability Register)

After the critique, close with **specific, numbered questions directed at elected representatives**. This transforms the article from commentary into a civic tool — readers can forward the questions directly.

**Structure:**
```
## Soalan untuk ahli Parlimen

1. [Direct question about procurement process]
2. [Direct question about valuation methodology]
3. [Direct question about regulatory inaction]
4. [Direct question about what happens to active investigations]
5. [Direct question about conflicts of interest]
6. [Direct question about suitability of key figure]
7. [Direct question about broader system risk]
```

**Rules:**
- Questions must be **answerable with documents** — not rhetorical
- Each question targets a **specific person or institution** (MoF, SC, Bursa, party whip)
- Questions are **short and sharp** — no essay-length framing
- End with the **human ownership frame**: "MyKad hang, pasport hang, subsidi anak hang. Semua tu milik hang."

**Why it works:** 
- Gives readers a concrete action (forward the questions)
- Establishes the article as evidence, not opinion
- Forces the named institution to respond or stay silent — both outcomes are valuable

## Voice Pattern: "System-of-Control Escalation" (connecting one deal to the control stack)

When one deal connects to broader infrastructure that controls citizens' daily lives — **make the escalation explicit**. Don't let readers think this is "just one deal."

**Technique used in MyKad article:**
```
MyKad → pasport → SALUR (subsidies) → identity verification
= ALL under one unaccountable hand
```

The SALUR fact-box is the escalation point. It takes a corporate deal (NexG) and reveals the endgame: "Kalau MyKad, pasport, subsidi, dan SALUR semua jatuh ke tangan yang sama — itu bukan sistem bantuan. Itu sistem kawalan."

**Trigger:** When the article's subject company deals with national identity, payment systems, data infrastructure, or anything that touches every citizen's daily life. The escalation reveals the stakes beyond the dollar figure.

**Anti-pattern:** Don't escalate on every article. Only when the deal genuinely connects to broader citizen infrastructure. Oil deal = money. Identity deal = control. Know the difference.
