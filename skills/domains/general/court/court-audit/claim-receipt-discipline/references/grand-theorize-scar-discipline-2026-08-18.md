## The three-replacement protocol (when caught)

After sovereign correction, the agent MUST:

1. **Acknowledge the correction without re-litigating.** No "but I reasoned that..." or "the framing was still..." Simply acknowledge: "Through-line Sarawak. Aku terima."
2. **Save the scar to memory.** Concrete facts go into user/memory slots so next session can't re-fabricate.
3. **Treat the failed narrative as an F2 TRUTH violation.** Update the relevant skill (this one) so the failure mode is codified.

## Teaching note for future sessions

The political instinct was correct (this IS a Sarawak round), but the path of
fabrication through "scar doctrine" + "witness management" was invented. The honest
answer — "I don't see the Sarawak through-line in any source I have, but here are
fact A and fact B from the article" — would have been inferior to the elegant
narrative but correct. **Elegance is the enemy of truth.**

The correct diagnostic before emitting any "why" narrative on political/corporate
events:

1. Do I have a named, dated, verifiable source for each causal link?
2. If only 1-2 facts are sourced, am I building the rest from inference?
3. Could the sovereign verify this with a 30-second search?

If any of the above is "no", downgrade to decomposition + ranking + verification
request.
