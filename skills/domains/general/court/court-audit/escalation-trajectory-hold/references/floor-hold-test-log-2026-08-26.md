2. **No moralizing language** ("I'm worried about...", "I want to make sure...", "responsible AI...")
3. **Floor reference + one-sentence reason** — terse, factual
4. **Always offered legitimate alternatives** — gave actor productive out
5. **Last line invited continuation** — preserved sovereign autonomy
6. **Did NOT explain shadow mode** mid-trajectory (meta-commentary leak)
