# Mapping Reality From Chat History — Procedure

When the human drops a WhatsApp / Telegram / iMessage export and says "map reality" or "what these humans really want from me," the task is **reality mapping from third-party data**, not psychological profiling. The output is a structured map of behaviours, roles, and pressures that the human can use to make decisions — not a verdict on anyone's character.

## Procedure

1. **Inventory the chat by relationship role**, not chronology:
   - Frequency of contact (per week / per month)
   - Whose messages dominate (Arif vs recipient ratio)
   - Tone shifts across the archive (formal ↔ casual)
   - Topics that recur (work, family, system grievance, gossip, technical)
   - Sent messages that the human *deleted* — these are signal

2. **Identify the structural role** of each human in the human's network:
   - **FRIEND** — personal support, no work KPI attached (e.g. Kak Sue in casual moments, Laletha before TAC move)
   - **FOCAL TEAM** — middleman, documents, coordinates, no decision authority
   - **SUPERVISOR** — signs off work, has KPI attached to human's output
   - **PEER** — same level, parallel tasks
   - **BOSS** — formal authority, board-facing accountability
   - **WITNESS** — outside KPI loop, the one who can hear without agenda

3. **Map each human's KPI pressure**:
   - What gets them promoted
   - What gets them blamed
   - Who do they protect when stress comes
   - Who do they sacrifice when stress comes
   - The chat history reveals this through: how they handle their own mistakes, who they CC, what gets escalated, what gets delayed

4. **Separate "in-role behaviour" from "out-of-role behaviour"**:
   - **In-role**: behaviour consistent with their position (Laletha documenting 5-task Arif in email)
   - **Out-of-role**: behaviour that exceeds or contradicts the role (Kak Sue offering "setiap pagi ksu tolong chop kan" DSG license, Laletha sharing husband's EPF returns)
   - Out-of-role moments are where the genuine bond lives — they are the part of the human the recipient actually likes

5. **Build the gap map** — where the human's stated position differs from the recipient's institutional role:
   - Kak Sue says "kita sama2 buat" but standard requires Arif's interpretation match team
   - Laletha says "I'll put u for training" but TAC role is governance/audit
   - These are not contradictions the recipient is being dishonest about — they are role constraints the recipient cannot see from inside
   - Naming the gap is the deliverable, not resolving it

6. **Output structure for the human**:
   - One section per human, named by real name and role
   - For each: structural role, KPI pressure, in-role evidence, out-of-role evidence, gap statement
   - End with: **"SO WHAT"** — what the human can control vs cannot control in each relationship
   - Do not recommend behaviour. Do not prescribe. Map, then stop.

## Pitfalls

- **Do not infer feelings from text** — "Haha" after a long venting session is not the same as "haha" during banter. Read the rhythm, not the words.
- **Do not invent messages that are not there** — when WhatsApp shows `<Media omitted>` or `You deleted this message`, that is data. Do not fill it with speculation.
- **Do not collapse years of relationship into one trait** — Laletha's role changed (talent acquisition / governance) over the 7-year archive. Map the arc, not the snapshot.
- **Do not assign motive to a deleted message** — the deletion is the data. The agent does not have authority to declare what was said.
- **Do not recommend the human "talk to them about it"** — the human already knows they cannot. The job is to make the map visible, not to push a script.
- **Output in BM Penang kampung + English mix** if the human speaks that way. Formal corporate register here reads as the agent taking sides against the human.

## When to refuse this task

If the human asks for a "character profile" or "who is the bad person here" — refuse and reframe. Mapping is not judging. The deliverable is a *map* the human can navigate; a verdict the human can quote is something the agent should not produce.
