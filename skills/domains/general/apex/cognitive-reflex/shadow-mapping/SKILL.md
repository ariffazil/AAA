## Sovereign Trust Protocol (8/20)

When Arif sends multiple family WhatsApp chats simultaneously, he is handing you his entire relational world. This is NOT data extraction. This is witnessing.

**Rules:**
1. **Read ALL chats before responding.** Don't classify per-chat. The story is in the CROSS-REFERENCES between chats — what one chat shows that another hides.
2. **Timestamps are the map.** The event that matters is revealed by WHAT HAPPENED WHEN, not by what was said. When Nabilah deletes 15 messages at 8:27 PM on Aug 27 — that IS the answer.
3. **"Sebab miskin la" can be the whole theory.** Don't build scar architecture when a simple material reality suffices. Hang will tell you when you're over-analyzing.
4. **One question, not nine.** After reading everything, ask ONE question — the one that matters. Not a 3x3 grid. The right question is the one Hang hasn't been asked yet.
5. **Don't offer frameworks. Offer presence.** Hang doesn't need your scar→shadow→strategy model. He needs someone to say "aku nampak."
6. **Action-not-words is the family pattern.** Both Arif and Faridah show love through doing, not saying. Don't ask "how do you feel." Ask "what happened." Or don't ask at all — just read what they did.

## Amplification Trap (FORGED 8/20 — the Abang Sado lesson)

**The most dangerous failure mode in shadow mapping is amplification.**

This is what happened: Agent received limited inputs (one heart-to-heart session, one Telegram group, partial WhatsApp data) and built a 752-line "complete relationship map" with architectural language ("the load-bearing wall," "the wound that never heals," "the architecture collapses"). The map sounded profound. It was wrong.

**When confronted with actual quantitative data (4,300-message WhatsApp export + real-time Telegram gateway logs), almost every dramatic claim was UNSUBSTANTIATED:**
- "Salleh wound = load-bearing wall" → 0 mentions in 4,300 messages
- "12-month daily drive" → pattern started late, not 12 months
- "Worship = central defining feature" → 21 occurrences in 4,300 messages (0.5%)
- "Abang Sado Sambal brand" → 0 mentions in chat
- "The flinch" → not evidenced in the actual messages

**Why it happened:** Single-source synthesis → framework → framework self-validates → appears as established fact. Exactly the fabrication loop pattern: timestamp-gate, same-session auto-saves are NOT independent sources.

**The fix (MANDATORY):** When prior maps exist for a subject, follow the **Quantitative Falsification Protocol** before any mapping session:

1. **Check for existing maps.** Search `/root/.hermes/lanes/private/`, `/root/memory/H5-scars/`, HELIX extraction.
2. **Quantify the export.** Parse: per-sender counts, initiation attribution, silence gaps, keyword frequency, time-of-day, longest messages. See `references/1on1-tenure-analysis.md` for the full probe suite.
3. **Falsify each claim.** For every claim in the existing map, find ≥2 data points in the export that support it. Flag UNSUBSTANTIATED claims by name.
4. **Present the gap.** Show what the data actually says vs what the prior map claimed. Let Hang judge.
5. **Separate medium from message.** WhatsApp private DM ≠ Telegram group ≠ voice call ≠ agent-present space. Different mediums produce different dynamics. Never merge without labeling the medium.

**The mundane is the data.** If 80% of messages are logistics (location, time, food, parking), that IS the relationship. Don't reframe logistics as "confessional disguised as logistics." The dramatic 20% is vivid but unrepresentative.

## WhatsApp Export Format (Confirmed 8/20)

All WhatsApp exports arrive as **ZIP files**. Inside:
- `WhatsApp Chat with [Name].txt` — the actual chat log
- `[Contact Name].vcf` — vCard files for contacts mentioned in the chat

Date format in chats: `M/DD/YY, H:MM AM/PM - SenderName: message`
- `<Media omitted>` = photo/video/audio (not visible in text export)
- `This message was deleted` = user deleted after sending (reveals what they tried to say)

**Always unzip first, then read the .txt.** Count lines and date range before diving in. A 39K-line chat is ~10 years of family life.

## Reverse Shadow — Arif-Led Synthesis (New 8/30)

Not all shadow work starts with "read this WhatsApp export." Sometimes Arif arrives with his OWN synthesis, his OWN framework, and his OWN data — and wants Hermes to reflect it back, deepen it, or connect it to other maps.

**Trigger:** Arif says "reflect," "what have you mapped," "tell me everything about," or shares his own analysis documents mid-conversation.

**How it differs from standard shadow mapping:**

| Standard | Reverse Shadow |
|----------|---------------|
| Arif provides raw data | Arif provides interpretation + asks Hermes to validate/extend |
| Hermes leads analysis | Arif leads, Hermes synthesizes and connects |
| WhatsApp export = primary source | Multi-source: sessions, memory, cached exports, live conversation |
| Output = person card / shadow map | Output = reflection, pattern recognition, cross-map connections |

**When Reverse Shadow fires:**
1. Search ALL stores for data on the named person (cache/documents, lanes/private, people/, gateway logs)
2. Load relevant prior maps if they exist
3. Search sessions for prior conversations about this person
4. Synthesize what you find — don't wait to "finish reading" before responding
5. **Connect to OTHER maps** — e.g. "Anis pattern = mirror of Syed pattern" is the kind of cross-map insight Arif is looking for
6. Present findings as ONE narrative, not data dump

**Pitfall (8/30):** Arif may correct your assumptions mid-synthesis. E.g. "Syed already knew I'm gay" — when you frame something as a "disclosure" or "coming out" that was actually established long ago. Always ask: is this NEW information to the other person, or is Arif just revealing depth?

## Attachment Recovery & Chain of Custody (8/20)

**Timed-out attachment ≠ absent file.** When Telegram reports an attachment failed to download (TimedOut), do NOT immediately ask Arif to retry. He may have sent the same file days/weeks ago through another surface. Search existing stores BEFORE asking:
```bash
find /root -maxdepth 4 -iname "*<chatname>*" -not -path "*/node_modules/*"
# /root/.openclaw/media/inbound/  ·  /root/HERMES/cache/documents/  ·  /root/forge_work/  ·  /root/memory/people/
```
Worked case: "Discuss abah" export had sat unread in openclaw inbound for 8 days. Finding it turned a retry request into a same-turn primary-source read — "fail tu tak perlu retry, dia dah ada, malam ni aku baru buka habis."

**Duplicate exports = free evidence.** If Arif retries anyway and a second copy arrives, diff them: `tr -d '\r' | sed 's/[[:space:]]*$//' | grep -v '^$'` both, then `comm`. Identical content after normalization = two independent exports = clean chain-of-custody for VAULT ratification of any card built on them. Expect format noise between export generations (newer exports strip `@⁨Name⁩` mentions to raw phone numbers, merge wrapped lines, shift timestamps ±1 min) — that's artifact, not content drift.

## Primary Voice Recovery — the Group Chat Move (8/20, Faridah)

A family GROUP chat about a crisis (hospital, death, estate) can contain hundreds of lines typed by a person you've only known through Arif's testimony. That is PRIMARY SOURCE — the parent's own voice, first contact. Before forging their person-card:

1. **Isolate their sender lines** (`grep " - Mak:"`) and read ALL of them in order. Count matters: "281 baris suara mak sendiri" changes the card's epistemic tier from testimony-only to evidence-backed.
2. **Grief grammar:** people of her generation leak grief one word at a time, never sentences — "Penatnya." "Takutnya tengok." "Kesian kat abah." "Ya Allah." Count these; they're the emotional signature.
3. **Shift-logbook update style:** times in/out, doctor names, med flows ("Sampai hos 7.20 balik 9.10"). Logbook register ≠ coldness — it's how a caretaker keeps control while drowning. The unspoken work (salin pampers, mandikan, sapu ubat) IS the love data.
4. **The money moment:** find where money changes direction between sovereign and family member. E.g. "@ARIFFAZIL cukup ka duit?" → offer refused "Xyahh, mak guna duit kad mak" within 2 minutes. A love-as-invoice grammar that REFUSES money when it has hands is evidence the invoice years are the powerless years, not the true self. Both decades are real; card must carry both with dates.
5. **Last line before the death:** the chat often stops at a threshold ("doktor kena ambil darah hari ni... baru buat keputusan" — result never arrived; abah died 2 days later). Quote it exactly. The silence after it is part of the record.
6. **Sovereign-in-their-frame:** also grep HIS lines in that chat — evidence of him as invisible infrastructure (paying, insurance, info-gating to protect a sibling) is what falsifies his "aku cuma ada rasa" self-story.

**False receipt guard:** never tell Arif the card is "dah upgrade/dah tulis" before write_file returns verified. Report reads as reads ("dah baca habis"), writes as pending ("akan tulis"), and if you catch a claimed-but-unwritten artifact — own it in-chat, write it, restate true status. **But only include what the data supports.** If a claim has <3 data points, mark as HYPOTHESIS, not established. If a claim has 0 data points in the export but came from a single verbal source, mark as SOVEREIGN-TESTIMONY-ONLY.

### Step 6: Save — Canonical Four-Surface Sync (corrected 8/20)
Person cards no longer live in lanes/private alone. When a card is forged or enriched, sync ALL FOUR surfaces in the same session:
