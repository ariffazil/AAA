### Theoretical Mode (sovereign directive)

   Key files: AAA/instructions/, arifOS/GENESIS/FLOOR_TABLE.json,

   carry_forward.json, vault999/seal_chain_head.json.

2. Derive minimal axiom set. What cannot be removed without losing
   explanatory power? What appears to be multiple things but is one

3. Verify against codebase. All major elements explained? Orphan
   concepts? Redundant axioms?

4. Output: single .md file. Theory only. No code/YAML/config.
   Open questions listed separately. Verification table included.

5. Align with repos: AAA (governance), arifOS (runtime),
   A-FORGE (execution), arif-fazil.com (public projection).
