# Pemex Collapse Parallel — NOC/SOE Death Spiral Framework

**Proven:** 2026-08-29, PETRONAS 1H FY2026 analysis
**Source:** Pemex (Mexico) collapse case study + institutional analysis

## The Death Spiral Formula

```
Production ↓ → Revenue ↓ → Government extraction ↑ (to compensate)
→ Less CAPEX for exploration → Production ↓↓ → Revenue ↓↓
→ Government extraction ↑↑ → Debt ↑↑ → Credit ↓↓
→ Refinancing cost ↑↑ → Free cash flow → 0 → Death
```

## 8 Pre-Collapse Signals (validated against PETRONAS 1H 2026)

### Signal 1: Production Declining While Price Rising
- **Pemex:** Production fell 3.3M → 1.7M bbl/d while oil rose to $100+
- **PETRONAS:** Crude production -17% (792 → 657 kboe/d) while Brent +28% ($71.87 → $92.31)
- **Reading:** Price tailwind masks operational decay. Revenue appears "resilient" while the production base erodes.

### Signal 2: Government Extraction Rising on Declining Base
- **Pemex:** Dividend rose from $5B to $10B even as production fell 30%
- **PETRONAS:** RM20B dividend floor + RM11.7B federal/state cash payments + RM13.8B GLC sales = RM46.5B/yr total dependency
- **Reading:** Government increases extraction to compensate for falling oil revenue, accelerating the production decline.

### Signal 3: Downstream Acquisition as "Strategic" Cover
- **Pemex:** Bought Deer Park (Texas) and Bajío refineries as "energy security" justification
- **PETRONAS:** Acquiring PRefChem 100% from Aramco as "energy security" justification; CAPEX 63% downstream vs 21% upstream
- **Reading:** Downstream acquisitions provide political cover for government extraction while upstream decays.

### Signal 4: BOD Captured by Political Appointees
- **Pemex:** Board stacked with political loyalists who approved value-destroying acquisitions
- **PETRONAS:** 3 BOD resignations (Oct 2024 – Jan 2025), thinned to 7 members, Sarawak director resigned, Jukhris appointment flagged as tripwire
- **Reading:** When technical directors are replaced by political appointees, the board's ability to challenge management decisions collapses.

### Signal 5: Debt Structure Shortening
- **Pemex:** Current borrowings rose from 8% to 25% of total in crisis years
- **PETRONAS:** Current borrowings +77.7% (RM13.5B → RM23.9B), now 18.9% of total (from 11.1%)
- **Reading:** Shortening maturity profile signals refinancing stress and reduced market confidence.

### Signal 6: Accounting Complexity Increasing
- **Pemex:** Used "negative substitution" (complex FX hedges) to mask underlying losses
- **PETRONAS:** PRefChem loss recognition (RM14.8B) + Searah JV gain + Other Income composition (+272%) + custom gearing formula
- **Reading:** Increasing accounting complexity = increasing difficulty for stakeholders to assess true financial position.

### Signal 7: Cash Reserves Declining
- **Pemex:** Cash fell from $15B to $5B in 4 years
- **PETRONAS:** Cash RM204.4B → RM193.6B (-RM10.8B in 6 months)
- **Reading:** Buffer erosion rate vs extraction rate determines runway. PETRONAS has 4-5 years at base case, 2-3 years at bear case (Brent $60).

### Signal 8: Refinancing Cost Rising
- **Pemex:** Financing costs rose from 3% to 8% of revenue
- **PETRONAS:** Financing costs +14% YoY (RM3.2B → RM3.7B per half), total borrowings RM126.8B with USD 87.1%
- **Reading:** Rising financing costs consume operating leverage and reduce free cash flow available for dividends.

## NOC Vulnerability Matrix

For any NOC/SOE, compute:

| Metric | Formula | Danger Threshold |
|---|---|---|
| Free cash flow after dividends | CFFO - CAPEX - Dividends | Negative = borrowing to pay government |
| NCI share of PAT | NCI PAT / Total PAT | >20% = wealth migration accelerating |
| Production per CAPEX dollar | Production / CAPEX | Declining = capital going to non-production |
| Cash buffer runway | Cash / (Annual extraction + CAPEX) | <3 years = approaching crisis |
| BOD technical ratio | Technical directors / Total BOD | <50% = governance capture |

## The Pemex Phase Diagnosis

| Phase | Characteristics | Timeframe |
|---|---|---|
| **Phase 1 (Peak)** | Production stable/growing, extraction sustainable, CAPEX adequate | Historical |
| **Phase 2 (Decline)** | Production falling, extraction increasing, CAPEX insufficient, debt rising | **Most NOCs are here** |
| **Phase 3 (Crisis)** | Buffer exhausted, death spiral visible, refinancing impossible | 3-7 years after Phase 2 |
