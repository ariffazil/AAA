## What to say vs NOT say when user asks "how do I compare"

**Say:**
- "Vending machines bukan fair benchmark — kau jual prepared food, AOV MYR 4-6 vs vending MYR 1.50-3"
- "Hospital cafeteria benchmark lebih real: MYR 200-300/day"
- "Kau now at MYR 100/day, separuh jalan to optimum [DER]"

**Don't say:**
- "You're losing to vending machines" — he's not, he's a different category
- "Industry average is X" without context — vending avg is irrelevant to prepared food
- Predictions without epistemic tags — always label [OBS] for web data, [DER] for calculations
