## Athlete name quirk (IMPORTANT for identity tracing)

The same competitor can appear under **different name spellings** across TegapTV surfaces. Proven case on EVjOukIg_bQ: YouTube title "Syed Khairuddin Morktarudin" vs tegaptv.com competition results "Mohd Khairuddin Hadzari" (Mr Body Smart 2014 4th / Mr Isagenix 2015 2nd runner-up, Below 170cm). Pattern: `Mohd`/`Muhd`/`Muhammad`/`Mohamed` prefixes swap; `Syed`/`Sayyid` honorific sometimes present, sometimes dropped; family-name transcription varies (`Khairuddin`/`Khairudin`/`Khiruddin`).

**Reconciliation recipe:**
1. tegaptv.com results tables (athlete tag number).
2. Category fit (Below 170cm physique build vs heavyweight mass).
