## Structural insights for MakcikGPT / civic intel

1. **"Kenapa import minyak?"** — Malaysia produces crude, but Malaysian crude ≠ Malaysian refinery config. Import needed for grade matching. Gas = net export; crude = net import. Both true simultaneously.
2. **Production decline = geological** — natural depletion, not negligence. Carigali explores, but results mostly gas.
3. **Hormuz = the real bottleneck** — not price per se, but physical delivery + insurance cost escalation.
4. **Petronas = amanah** — RM1.6T lifetime contribution; 20% of gov revenue. "Bukan nak pukul canang tapi inilah realitinya."
5. **"Efficiency is the first fuel"** — demand-side answer. Malaysia's per-capita consumption highest in ASEAN. Europe cut 15-18% gas during Ukraine-Russia crisis.

## Communication pattern

- Tan Sri style: measured, data-driven, paternal. Uses charts. "Bukan nak pukul canang" before stating facts.
- Host style (Aswad): populist translator, asks the warga net questions.
- Format: studio interview with breaks (ads/promos interleaved — ignore in transcript).
- Language: formal Malay with English technical terms mixed in (net exporter, planning horizon, efficiency).
