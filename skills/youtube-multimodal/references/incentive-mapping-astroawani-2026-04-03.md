# Incentive Mapping — Worked Example (2026-08-09)

## Source video

Astro AWANI Bicara Naratif Khas, 3 April 2026
Tan Sri Tengku Mohamad Taufiq (President & GCEO, Petronas)
Topic: Global energy crisis (West Asia conflict)

## The 7-Layer Map (worked)

### Layer 1: Narrative Inoculation
Oil at $120 → public will say "Petronas untung besar, rakyat menderita."
CEO pre-empts by explaining upstream profits but downstream cost squeeze.
RM1.6T contribution, 49% dividend payout = "we already give back."
**Verdict:** Classic NOC playbook before Parliament session.

### Layer 2: Soft Warning
"Bekalan terjamin sehingga akhir Mei" repeated 3x across 59 minutes.
45-day planning horizon (down from 3 months) = crisis posture.
Translation: "We're buying time. Don't panic yet."
**Verdict:** Expectation management disguised as reassurance.

### Layer 3: Policy Seeding
Thailand diesel comparison (RM6+/liter without subsidy).
"Beban subsidi semakin naik untuk kerajaan."
**Verdict:** Preparing public for future subsidy cuts or price increases.
The PM can't say it directly. The NOC CEO lays the groundwork.

### Layer 4: Institutional Mythology
Historical parallels: 1973-74 (Petronas born), 1980 (Iran-Iraq), 1991 (Gulf War).
Every crisis survived = Petronas existed.
**Verdict:** Constructing institutional origin myth. Petronas = Malaysia's energy shield.

### Layer 5: Market Signal
Three-scenario framework ($65-75 / $85-90 / $100+ years).
Vague on alternative sources: "tak sesuai umumkan dari mana."
**Verdict:** Dual-coded. Public: "we're prepared." Markets: "we're buying, send barrels."

### Layer 6: The Silences
- No ringgit figure for subsidy cost (would trigger backlash)
- No post-May specific plan (not finalised or politically sensitive)
- No Petronas financial reserves/borrowing capacity (competitive intelligence)
- No rationing mention (would cause panic)
- No Hormuz full-closure scenario (too scary)
**Verdict:** The silences reveal the real contingency planning shape.

### Layer 7: Venue Choice
Astro AWANI / RTM = state media, domestic audience only.
Not Bloomberg (investors). Not CNBC (global). Not regional forum (ASEAN).
**Verdict:** Political communication exercise dressed as public information.

## Decision test: Strategic comms or genuine info?

**Does the speaker have something to lose by NOT doing this interview?**
- If yes → strategic communication (this case: YES — without this, populist narrative fills the vacuum)
- If no → genuine information
- If unclear → present both

## Pattern applicability

This 7-layer framework applies to any public statement by:
- NOC/IOC CEOs during commodity price spikes
- Politicians announcing policy during crisis
- Corporate spokespeople during scandal/downturn
- Military/government briefings during conflict
- Any interview on state media during crisis

The specific layers shift in relevance but the框架 is reusable.
