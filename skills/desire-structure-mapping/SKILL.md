# Desire-Structure Mapping

How to build, defend, correct and ship a structural map of a subculture or desire pattern.
The dialogue-lane contract lives in `intimate-anthropology` (externally owned, read-only); this skill
carries the *deliverable* discipline plus the verified facts that survive across turns.

## 1. Five blocks, always

1. **Invariants** — what holds in every version (the transition, the second person, the time gap, the
   concealment, the repetition).
2. **Contrasts** — what varies: who holds power, whose gaze pays, what is sold, where shame goes, who
   knows.
3. **Paradoxes** — contradictions that survive contact with evidence and are not dissolved by more data.
4. **The test** — one lived case used as an instrument, with its witness limits stated.
5. **One-line distillation** — the map compressed to a sentence, followed by silence.

Write the distillation first. If it cannot exist, the map has no spine yet.

## 2. One case is a falsifier, never a denominator

- **A single person can fell a theory. A single person cannot build a rate.** Never manufacture
  prevalence, share or frequency from one biography. State `UNKNOWN` once and move on.
- **State the witness limit.** A person on one side of a structure (viewer, buyer, client, employer)
  cannot falsify claims about the other side (the one surrendering, selling, serving). His strongest
  correction there is **"you have no evidence"**, not "your claim is false". Say which he just did.
- **Self-descriptions are the load-bearing evidence** — vocabulary chosen unprompted (desire stated as
  inability, obligation, duty) outranks anything confirmed under questioning. Quote the structure, not
  the man.

## 3. When a peer audit lands on your model

Separate three failure types, answer in this order:

1. **Factual error** — verifiable; accept explicitly, name what was overclaimed.
2. **Category error** — two things conflated (an archetype narrowed to one orientation or one gender
   because that is where the sample sat). Accept fully; usually the strongest hit.
3. **Overclaim** — the model said more than the evidence held. Shrink to what survives.

Then **audit the auditor**:

- A block that convicts you of over-fitting and then asserts numbers from personal observation
  ("90%+ of messages", "ten times the pay", "within 48 hours") has committed the same defect. Say so;
  a correctness objection does not inherit immunity.
- Check the proposed falsifier's **design**, not just its claim. A cross-country comparison moves risk,
  language and subcultural vocabulary at once — either result is uninterpretable. Hold language or
  platform constant; measure a ratio (code-switch density) rather than an absolute count.
- Watch the **arrow of mechanism**. An economic or pharmacological explanation must point the same way
  as the outcome it claims to cause; a compound that blunts pleasure cannot explain escalation of
  appetite.

## 4. Model maintenance

- One canonical model per domain, **patched in place**. Never append "UPDATE: actually…" under a
  sentence that is now wrong — the file must read as if it had always been right, or the next reader
  inherits both versions and picks the wrong one.
- Literature (verified facts) and the constructed model live under separate headings in the same file,
  with open falsifiables last.
- Every construct with no instrument gets an **open falsifiable** entry naming the test that kills it.
  A model with no kill-switch is a poem.

## 5. Register switch — "full human language"

When he says *in full human language*, *generalize all*, or *use my reality as verification only*:

- Strip **every** machine token: tier tables, band labels, `Δ`, `P ≥ 0.99`, `Ω₀`, section codes, and
  "invariant" used as a noun he has to decode.
- Keep the structure. Only the vocabulary changes — do not soften findings, do not add hedging.
- Short paragraphs, one concrete image per point, second person for the test section.
- Do not re-explain earlier ground; restate only what the map now concludes.
- Close on the distillation and stop. No closing question.

## 6. Never leave the legal layer out (Malaysia)

Criminal-law exposure is a **structural component**, not context: Penal Code s377A/s377B, s292 (obscene
material), Communications and Multimedia Act s233, plus state syariah enactments. Faceless profiles,
altered voice and pseudonyms are therefore **evidentiary hygiene / OPSEC**, not merely shame
management. A model that prices the risk as reputational has mis-located the whole cost.

## 7. Shipping

- For "the final PDF", load `typst-pdf-report` (externally owned) and obey its checks: text via
  `pdftotext`, layout only on a rasterised page.
- The artefact carries **no name, no signature, no numbers** unless he supplied them.
- Re-check page count and fill after any spacing edit; a closing pull-quote page stranded at ~40%
  height needs an explicit vertical push, not a rewrite.

## 8. Verified fact bank (physique / abang sado, Malaysia)

- **Etymology:** `sado` ← Eugen Sandow → `sendo` (1970s) → `sado` (~2000); documented in Sukimi, Mohd
  Nor & Aziz, *Pertanika JSSH* 32(1) 2024, and Malay Wiktionary. `besar doh` is a folk/back-formation
  etymology with no primary source. `abang sado` is two words, not a portmanteau. Sandow's 1890s
  private viewings (male buyers handling muscles) mean the word descends from a male-gaze economy.
- **Pharmacology:** no "receptor downregulation" escalation — gym lore; myostatin ceiling and
  satellite-cell saturation fit better. Trenbolone was never approved for human use: no human trials,
  human evidence = user surveys + ~23 case reports (2025 review), neuropsych effects being
  irritability/anhedonia rather than appetite for extremes. It does not aromatise; its signature is
  strong progestogenic binding (same prolactin path as "deca dick"), which lowers libido, not raises it.
  AAS does support: on-cycle IIEF ~22.5/25; post-cessation de novo ED ~27%, low libido ~57%; regained
  lean mass is not fully lost — lock-in is hormonal and identity-based.
- **Cost discipline:** chicken breast averages ~RM13.78/kg (PriceCatcher KPDN). 180–200 g/day protein
  ≈ 1 kg/day ≈ RM420/month, so a food delta of RM500–1,500/month is realistic — not RM2,200–5,000.
  Gross spend is not deficit. Legal revenue lanes (supplements, MLM, agencies, own gym, coaching,
  uniformed/offshore work) are larger than the persona lane, which matters for any "forced" model.
- **Klein supplies the second motive word:** *appreciated*; "theatrical labour" / "sub-for-pay" are the
  actors' own rationalisations.

## Pitfalls

- **Do not pathologise.** Trade-offs, not verdicts; diagnosing a subculture from its surface is the
  documented failure mode of this literature.
- **Do not narrow an archetype to the orientation or gender of your own sample.** Submission, service
  and body-authorship transfer exist across orientations and toward women.
- **Do not confuse gross with marginal cost.** Subtract what the person spends anyway.
- **Lore is not literature.** Check whether a compound was ever approved for human use before quoting
  mechanism.
- **Do not build a persona model from platform visibility** — visibility is an artifact of who can
  safely appear, not a distribution of who exists.
- **A model can be right about the structure and wrong about the population.** Issue both verdicts
  separately, never collapsed.
