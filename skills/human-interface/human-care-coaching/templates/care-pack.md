# Care pack — starter outline

Copy, fill, delete empty sections. The pack is for the principal's hands, not for delivery to the
bonded person. Final register: his own, short lines, no headings.

```
1. Acknowledgment, then what he already did
   - three acts from the last day/week that already count as care (from the record, not flattery)
   - the naming correction if he said he is bad at this: bad at naming, or bad at caring?

2. Channels
   - thought: over-used / right-sized / empty — and what the over-use costs the other person
   - body:    same
   - time:    same

3. Acts — ordered by fidelity, three to five only
   a. presence: go, sit, be there with no reason that has to be explained
   b. live voice rather than text: one question, then stay quiet while he answers
   c. a small concrete thing that is already possible (meal, lift, errand, company to an appointment)
   d. a threshold: on the third recurrence, book the appointment instead of asking about it

4. Do not
   - crafted prose to someone who flinches at precision (reads as manipulation)
   - diagnosis or interpretation of the person
   - agent-sent messages as his voice
   - schedule, tracker, metrics — a project is not care
   - questions whose answers he does not want to hear
   - a public room for a private repair

5. Anti-outsourcing clause
   - one sentence: if the agent drafts, sends and tracks it, he becomes a spectator

6. Close
   - one sentence naming what is actually being asked of him
   - one next action with a time attached
```

## Fidelity ordering, and why

| Channel | Reach | Fails when |
|---|---|---|
| Presence | Highest — experienced, not received | Used only in emergencies, so it reads as rescue |
| Live voice | High — cannot be smoothed into "ok" | Replaced by text because text is easier to schedule |
| Text from the human | Medium — real, but competes with the agent's own traffic | The agent is also messaging the same person |
| Anything from the agent | Near zero for bonding | It is the only channel used |

## Pitfalls

- **A pack that only explains the recipient.** Accuracy about someone's family, childhood or wound is
  not care. Every line must end in an act the human performs with his own body this week, or be cut.
- **Timing.** A message that is right in content can be wrong in timing — sending a composed piece
  minutes after the recipient asked for less noise undoes it. Check the last thing the recipient
  actually said before recommending anything be sent.
- **Confusing delivery with action.** "Sent" is not "done": a send that failed silently is not care,
  and the agent should report which state it actually reached rather than assuming arrival.
