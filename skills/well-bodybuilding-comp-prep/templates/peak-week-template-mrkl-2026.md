## Emergency Adjustments

### If You Look FLAT (no fullness, muscles look empty):
- Add 50-100g carbs (white rice, rice cakes)
- Slightly increase water (500ml)
- Add 10g fat (peanut butter)

### If You Look WATERY (soft, smooth, no separation):
- Reduce water to 2L
- Increase sweating (sauna 15 min, hot bath)
- Reduce carbs by 50g
- Walk 30 min

### If You Look PERFECT:
- Don't change ANYTHING
- Maintain current intake
- Sleep and hydrate minimally
