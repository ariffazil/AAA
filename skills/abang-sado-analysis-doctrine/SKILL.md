## Etymology — VERIFIED (kills the folk tale)

- **Sandow → sendo → sado.** Eugen Sandow (won the 1901 Royal Albert Hall contest) → Tanah
  Melayu "sendo" active by the 1970s (also alive in Silat Seni Gayong orality — "Panglima
  Sendo Perak") → "sado" c. 2000. Source: Sukimi, Mohd Nor & Aziz, *Pertanika JSSH* 32(1),
  Mar 2024, Malay-youth "sado" commodification study (Selangor); corroborated by Wikikamus BM.
- "Besar bodoh / besar doh" = **folk etymology**, no primary source (Urban Dictionary +
  2013–15 blog posts only). Correct framing: possible **back-formation narrative** — a story
  told AFTER the word spread. Test that separates it from a real diffusion vector: the acronym
  story must predate the spread, and speakers must derive the meaning from it spontaneously.
- "Abang sado" is **not** a portmanteau. Two words.
- Irony worth keeping: if the root is Sandow, the word descends from the 1890s private-viewing
  economy (men paying to touch muscle). A "gym bro" term with a male-gaze ancestry.

## Malaysia legal layer — OPSEC, not psychology

Risk is **statutory**, not reputational: Penal Code **s377A/377B**, **s292** (obscene
material), **CMA 1998 s233**, plus state Syariah criminal enactments. Therefore faceless
profiles, altered voice, pseudonyms = **evidentiary hygiene**. Any analysis that stops at
*aib* / *maruah* is incomplete.

## Claims to REJECT (do not revive)

- **"Trenbolone / 19-nor causes new paraphilias."** No human clinical trials; trenbolone was
  never approved for humans. Human evidence = user surveys + 23 case reports (cardiovascular,
  hepatic, renal, neuropsychiatric). The documented neuropsychiatric direction is irritability,
  aggression, mood lability, anxiety, dysphoria, **anhedonia** — the opposite of novelty-seeking.
  Androgens plausibly change **intensity / disinhibition**; they do not install paraphilias.
- "Trenbolone manipulates estradiol" — it does not aromatise. Route is progestogenic (prolactin),
  i.e. LOW libido, not extreme appetite.
- **Any prevalence figure for Malaysia.** No instrument exists; sthenolagnia is not a DSM
  diagnosis; all samples are self-selected gym populations. UNKNOWN until counted.
