# Canonical collapse markers

Verifiable peak / terminal / trigger points. Reuse these instead of re-researching; each is
anchored to a filing, a wire report, or an archived price table, so it can be defended.

## Enron (NYSE: ENE)

| Point | Value |
|---|---|
| Peak | $90.75 (intraday high $90.56 close), 23 Aug 2000 — briefly the 7th largest US public company, ~$70bn cap |
| Audit / disclosure trigger | 16 Oct 2001 — $618m quarterly loss, $1.2bn equity writedown |
| Management rupture | 14 Aug 2001 — Skilling resigns after ~6 months as CEO |
| Restatement | 8 Nov 2001 — earnings overstated back to 1997 |
| Terminal print | $0.36 close, 29 Nov 2001, −41% on the day, record NYSE volume |
| Filing | Chapter 11, 2 Dec 2001 |
| Auditor | Arthur Andersen — signed every year, later dissolved |

Notable early tell: on the 17 Apr 2001 earnings call the CEO answered a routine analyst question
about the balance sheet by insulting the analyst on the public line. The number was unremarkable;
the reaction to an ordinary question was the signal.

## Serba Dinamik (Bursa: 5279)

| Point | Value |
|---|---|
| Peak | RM2.42 (post-bonus adjusted), Jan 2020 — ~RM6bn cap |
| Listing | Feb 2017, one of the largest IPOs that year |
| First suspension | 25 May 2021, last traded RM1.61 |
| Audit trigger | 31 May 2021 — KPMG raises issues over ~RM3.5bn of contracts; RM1.61 → RM0.86 within a week |
| Second suspension | 18 Oct 2021 at RM0.37 |
| Resume | 10 May 2022, −60% in a day to RM0.15; −60% again next day to RM0.06 |
| Terminal print | RM0.02, 17 Jan 2023 |
| Delisting | 5 Jun 2024 |
| Insider tell | CEO disposed of his entire warrant holding at ~7.5 sen, one day before the share suspension |

## Wirecard (DAX: WDI)

| Point | Value |
|---|---|
| Peak | €226.39, 3 Sep 2018, DAX constituent |
| Audit trigger | 18 Jun 2020 — auditors refuse to sign the accounts; €104.50 → €39.00 in one session |
| Collapse | 25 Jun 2020 — €1.9bn balance-sheet hole admitted, insolvency filed; €3.50 |
| Trough | €0.295, Sep 2021 |
| Terminal print | €0.05, 25 Jul 2024 |
| 2020 performance | −99.7% for the year |

The €1.9bn did not go missing — it never existed. That distinction is the whole case: an operating
loss is recoverable, a hole that was never funded is not.

## Shared silhouette

All three: long flat base → vertical parabola → roll-over with lower highs → one enormous red
candle on the audit/disclosure → graveyard of tiny candles near zero → suspension → delisting.

The audit candle is the moment the public caught up, not the moment the damage occurred — by then
price has usually already surrendered most of the parabola.
