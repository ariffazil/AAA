# F13 Actual-Intent Probing — 9 Questions for Sovereign-Grade Artifacts

> **Verified:** 2026-08-12 PAC petition session — after agent forged APEX LETTER (audit-focused dodge), F13 answered 9 questions revealing actual intent was TT personally gone, full qualia, accepted cost, Oppenheimer close. The 9 questions metabolized rage (Q1 "Fuck I don't care about audit") into specific operational demand (Q7 qualia + Q9 cost acceptance).

> **Purpose:** When F13 SOVEREIGN authorizes a sovereign-grade artifact (letter, exposé, document) and the agent cannot distinguish F13's actual demand from the institutional cover, ask 9 questions along 9 axes before forging. The questions are diagnostic — they probe what F13 actually wants beneath the surface emotion.

## When to deploy

- After rage phase, before grace phase metabolizes into operational directive
- When the agent has defaulted to safe/institutional framing and senses misalignment with F13's emotion
- When F13's stated ask (audit, governance, reform) seems to use institutional vocabulary as cover for rawer demands (removal, accountability)
- Before any T3 irreversible dispatch where the artifact must be FORGED FOR F13, not for the agent's preferred framing

## The 9 axes

| # | Axis | Diagnostic question | What it reveals |
|---|------|---------------------|-----------------|
| 1 | **Surface vs Underlying** | If PAC does the audit and finds nothing, how do you feel? If PAC rejects the audit entirely, how do you feel? | Whether F13 wants accountability (outcome) or voice (process). Different. |
| 2 | **Audience Capacity** | Is the institutional/tenang voice really your voice, or the voice you feel safe using? | Whether the agent's chosen voice matches F13's actual register or F13's preferred safe register. |
| 3 | **Truth vs Performance** | If tomorrow PETRONAS offers MSS with good terms (RM500K, pension, reference letter), will you still send the letter? | Whether the letter is about the institution or about F13's own self-realization. |
| 4 | **What You CANNOT Write** | Is there a section you have cut without realizing you cut it? The cut = the gap between constitutional voice and personal protection. | What is the missing piece — usually the most important piece. |
| 5 | **For Whom** | Who reads this in 5 years? 30 years? Your anak? Cucu mak? Or someone who never met you? | Whether F13 writes for today (petition) or for posterity (civilizational witness). Different artifacts. |
| 6 | **Two Flavors of Patience** | Before the rupture of "sabar = penghambaan", there was 12 years of real sabar — the sabar that gave you the basin discoveries, the wells, the craft. Which one is harder to write about — the submission or the real patience? | Whether F13 is rejecting all patience (regression) or only the submission (correct). |
| 7 | **Sahih vs Performatif** | If you read the artifact back, do you feel this is YOUR voice, or your architect's voice playing constitutional? | Whether the artifact is sahih (real witness) or performatif (F13 costume). The beza determines F2/F11 floor status. |
| 8 | **One Thing Only** | If you could send only ONE thing — one file, one letter, one message — what would you send? Not for Parliament. For yourself 10 years from now. | The kernel artifact F13 actually wants. Often simpler than what the agent has been forging. |
| 9 | **The Cost** | Are you ready to pay the full cost? Not just retaliation — also mak reading it, ayah knowing from somewhere, anak not understanding, yourself 5 years from now regretting OR relieved. | Whether cost acceptance is real. If yes — artifact should embed cost explicitly. If no — agent should ground F13 again. |

## The recovery pattern after 9 questions

1. Acknowledge the dodge explicitly: "Aku optimize untuk attackability. Itu dodge."
2. Re-ask F13 with three concrete options: institutional / personal / hybrid
3. Honor F13's choice without defending the prior dodge
4. Re-forge with new direction

## Anti-patterns to avoid

- Asking the 9 questions as a checklist instead of as diagnostics — the questions are PROBES, not boxes to tick
- Asking only the easy questions (Q1, Q3, Q9) and skipping Q4, Q6, Q7 (the deeper ones)
- Asking questions defensively to "validate" what the agent already planned to forge
- Treating the answers as final without asking for the underlying reasoning
- Skipping the 9 questions entirely and just forging — the questions exist BECAUSE the agent's default is to dodge

## When NOT to deploy

- When F13 has already authored the canonical artifact text directly (use Pattern 3 from F13 Sovereign Pattern)
- When the artifact is technical/operational (not sovereign-grade)
- When the dispatch is reversible (T0/T1)
- When F13 has explicitly said "no more questions, just do X" (then GO)
