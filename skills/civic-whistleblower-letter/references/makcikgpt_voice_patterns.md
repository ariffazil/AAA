# Bahasa Makcik Voice Patterns

The voice used by Arif Fazil's `arif-fazil.com/world/makcikgpt/` civic journalism series. This voice is the user's AUTHENTIC register, not AI slop to strip away. When the user is Arif and the task is civic whistleblowing, write IN this voice.

## Voice Characteristics

### Pronouns and Address
- `Hang` (you, familiar, slightly confrontational-but-friendly)
- `Makcik` (the narrator, auntie)
- `Pakcik` (when speaking about men, uncle)
- `Jiran` (neighbor, used when referring to ordinary people affected)
- `Depa` (they, colloquial plural)
- NEVER `anda`, `saya`, `kita` (too formal for this register)

### Sentence Structure
- Short sentences. Often 5-12 words.
- Punchy declaratives.
- Conversational asides: "Makcik tak faham semua tu."
- Repetition for emphasis: "Bukan cerita WhatsApp." / "Itu fakta. Bukan cerita."
- Direct question refrains:
  - "Makcik nak tanya..."
  - "Siapa yang untung?"
  - "Kalau semua orang senyap -- siapa yang jaga?"
- Ironic callbacks: "Hang pernah dengar... Makcik dengar jugak..."

### Diction
- Bahasa Pasar (colloquial Malaysian), not high-formal BM
- "Makcik kira duit" — Makcik counts the money (asserts numeracy without jargon)
- "Kerajaan makan" — government eats (confrontational, colloquial, devastating)
- "ATM" — automatic teller machine, used as metaphor for PETRONAS
- "Bakar" — burns (as in "burning money" / wasting capital)
- "Curi duit dalam ATM" — steals money from inside the ATM
- "Sebelah mata" — half-eye, used to describe missing oversight
- "Mata luar" — outside eye, the missing independent observer

### Numbers
- Always use BILION (billion) in numerals: "RM70 bilion"
- Always cite source: "Makcik kira, dari Integrated Report 2025"
- Always humanize: "RM32 bilion tu macam gaji 1 juta orang setahun"
- Never use jargon: "extraction rate 70.5%" — instead "Kerajaan ambil 70 sen setiap ringgit"

### Refrains That Work
- "Makcik tak ada penyelesaian. Makcik cuma ada soalan."
- "Ini fakta. Bukan cerita WhatsApp."
- "Hang baca betul."
- "Macam mana boleh jadi macam tu?"
- "Anak cucu dapat apa?"
- "DITEMPA BUKAN DIBERI."

### Refrains That DON'T Work (too AI)
- ❌ "Berdasarkan analisis data, dapat disimpulkan bahawa..."
- ❌ "Terdapat beberapa faktor yang perlu dipertimbangkan..."
- ❌ "Adalah penting untuk ambil perhatian bahawa..."
- ❌ Bullet walls with **bolded headers** in formal BM
- ❌ Passive voice: "Telah dapat dilihat bahawa..."
- ❌ Em-dashes, curly quotes, decorative unicode

## Structural Patterns

### The Four-Bunga (Four Stories) Pattern
The most effective civic essays use 4 parallel stories to make the point:

```
Cerita pertama: [the headline institutional fact]
Cerita kedua: [the human face -- jiran, anak, orang biasa]
Cerita ketiga: [the systemic pattern -- dua pintu, satu rumah]
Cerita keempat: [the institutional accountability gap -- siapa jaga?]
```

Each "cerita" opens with the refrain "Makcik nak cerita pasal..." Then closes with the implicit "Apa yang Makcik nampak."

### The Numbered Conclusions
After the four stories, conclude with numbered observations:

```
Apa Makcik nampak.

Satu: ...
Dua: ...
Tiga: ...
Empat: ...
```

Then the refrain: "Makcik tak ada penyelesaian. Makcik cuma ada soalan."

### The Closing Questions
End with 3-4 unanswered questions that the reader must answer themselves:

```
Siapa yang untung?
Siapa yang rugi?
Siapa yang patut tanya?
Kalau semua orang senyap -- siapa yang jaga?
```

This is the tersurat/tersirat technique in action — the questions are explicit, but the answers are left to the reader. The author does not need to draw the line.

## Example Paragraphs (use as reference)

### Opening that works
> Hang pernah dengar orang kata: "PETRONAS ni ATM kerajaan." Makcik dengar jugak. Dan Makcik kira duit. Betul. Memang betul. Kerajaan ambil banyak. Tapi itu bukan cerita habis. Itu baru permulaan.

### Number-citation that works
> Tahun 2022 -- PETRONAS untung paling besar dalam sejarah. Seratus satu bilion enam ratus juta ringgit. Satu tahun je. Kerajaan ambil berapa? Lima puluh bilion. Hampir separuh. Hang baca betul.

### Humanization that works
> Makcik ada jiran. Kerja PETRONAS dua puluh dua tahun. Bulan lepas kena MSS -- skim berhenti sukarela. Depa kata "strategic workforce rightsizing." Makcik tak faham semua tu. Makcik cuma nampak muka dia.

### Closing that works
> Makcik tak ada penyelesaian. Makcik cuma ada soalan: Kalau PETRONAS untung -- siapa yang untung? Kalau PETRONAS rugi -- siapa yang rugi? Kalau PETRONAS senyap -- siapa yang patut tanya? Dan kalau semua orang senyap -- siapa yang jaga? DITEMPA BUKAN DIBERI.

## What NOT To Mimic

The Makcik voice is for civic prose with bite. It is NOT for:

- ❌ Technical documentation
- ❌ Legal letters to courts
- ❌ Internal corporate communication
- ❌ Academic or policy briefs
- ❌ Crisis communications where formality signals reliability

Use it only where the reader is the Malaysian public and the goal is "tangkap attention + tangkap hati."

## What To Translate If User is Not Arif

If the user is NOT Arif, this voice is wrong. Replace with whatever the user's documented authentic register is. The civic-whistleblower-letter skill works for any user; the Makcik voice is one implementation among many.
