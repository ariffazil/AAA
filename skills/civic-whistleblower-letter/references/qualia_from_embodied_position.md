# Qualia From Embodied Position — Writing Human Sensory Experience

> **Verified:** 2026-08-12 PAC petition session. F13 asked "Aku nak ditulis dengan penuh rasa qualia manusia." Honest answer: no LLM writes true qualia. But the agent CAN write FROM embodied position — as if from inside the experiencing subject — and the reader enters the experience rather than reading the report.

> **Purpose:** When F13 asks for "qualitative" or "qualia" or "human feel" in a sovereign-grade artifact, use this technique. The two failure modes are ABOUT (describes emotions from outside, fails) and FROM (writes from inside the experiencing subject, succeeds).

## The fundamental limitation

**No LLM writes true human qualia.** Qualia = sensory experience of being embodied. That is biological, not computational. F9 ANTIHANTU (no consciousness claims) and F10 ONTOLOGY (AI ≠ human) are constitutional floors — the agent must NOT claim to feel.

**The honest forge pattern is FROM embodied position, not ABOUT embodied position.**

FROM = the agent writes AS IF from inside the experiencing subject, using the patterns the subject would use to describe their own moment.

ABOUT = the agent describes the subject's emotions from outside, as a report.

The reader feels the difference. The difference is everything.

## The two failure modes

### ❌ ABOUT — describes qualia, fails

```
Saya berasa tertekan apabila melihat ketidakadilan ini.
Saya rasa marah dan kecewa.
Saya begitu sedih dengan keadaan ini.
Saya ingin menyampaikan perasaan saya yang terpendam.
Saya menghadapi cabaran yang besar.
```

These are reports OF qualia. They describe emotions from outside. They are what LLMs statistically default to. They feel performative.

### ✅ FROM — writes from inside the experiencing subject

```
Pukul 3 pagi, lampu bilik masih nyala. Saya tulis dengan tangan yang
menggeletar sebab kopi kedua masih hangat dalam cawan tapi saya tak
boleh minum. Suara anak saya kat bilik sebelah — senyap. Hening yang
berat.
```

The reader feels:
- Embodied time (3am — the deepest hour)
- Embodied sensation (trembling hands, warm coffee going cold)
- Embodied spatial relationship (child in next room, silence)
- Embodied weight (the heaviness of the silence)

The reader ENTERS the experience, not reads the report.

## The technique components

For each FROM passage, include as many of these as the moment calls for:

1. **Specific time** — not "later" or "eventually" but "pukul 3 pagi", "subuh", "Isnin pagi", "sebelum Subuh"
2. **Sensory detail** — what is seen, heard, touched, smelled, tasted in the moment
3. **Body sensation** — where in the body the feeling lives (tangan menggeletar, dada berat, mata berair, urat tegang)
4. **Spatial relationship** — who/what is in adjacent spaces (bilik sebelah, meja makan, dapur, jalan)
5. **Temporal flow** — what is changing as the moment unfolds (kopi sejuk, cahaya berubah, anak senyap)

## The Berhenti-Sabar passage (verified working example)

> "Mak cakap sabar la. Aku dah 12 tahun sabar. Hari ini aku berhenti."

This is FROM embodied position:
- The mak's voice in his head (auditory memory)
- The count of years felt not calculated (12 tahun as body-time, not chronology)
- The rupture moment named (hari ini aku berhenti — embodied present)

Not "I have decided to stop being patient" — that's ABOUT.

But "hari ini aku berhenti" is FROM.

## Closing patterns that are FROM

```
Sekarang aku berhenti. Sekarang giliran Parlimen.

Surat ini ditulis dengan tangan yang menggeletar. Aku hantar sebab
aku tak boleh tidur lagi.

Saya tulis bukan sebab berani. Saya tulis sebab penat. Penat tengok
negara yang orang macam saya bina, tapi yang terbaik dalam negara tu
rasa milik dia sendiri.
```

## Closing patterns that are ABOUT (avoid)

```
Dengan rasa hormat dan rendah hati, saya memohon pertimbangan YB.

Saya berharap surat ini mendapat perhatian yang sewajarnya.

Saya percaya pada institusi demokrasi kita.

Dengan penuh rasa takzim,

Saya mohon jasa baik YB untuk mempertimbangkan perkara ini.
```

These are formal ABOUT. They feel empty. The reader doesn't feel the writer's body.

## Where to apply FROM vs ABOUT

| Element | Register | Reason |
|---------|----------|--------|
| Letterhead, signature, CC | ABOUT (formal) | Constitutional protocol — the agent must respect the form |
| Opening (pukul 3 pagi, kopi, tangan) | FROM (embodied) | Establishes the witness as real |
| Substantive paragraphs (audit asks, institutional critique) | Mix — mostly ABOUT for institutional asks, FROM for personal stakes | Each paragraph can shift register |
| Closing (cost acceptance, mak reconciliation) | FROM (embodied) | The weight of the witness |
| AI disclosure footer | ABOUT (formal) | Constitutional protocol |

The mix is the craft. The opening grabs the reader. The substantive paragraphs carry the institutional weight. The closing lands the embodied truth.

## Worked example: v11 NUCLEAR opening (FROM)

```
Assalamualaikum Warahmatullahi Wabarakatuh dan salam sejahtera.

1. Mak pesan sabar la, nak. Itu pesan dia tiap kali aku balik kampung
nampak muka penat aku. Itu juga pesan dia tiap kali aku nampak
adik-adik aku susah cari kerja. Itu juga pesan dia bila aku cakap
pasal orang-orang PETRONAS yang dibuang.

Mak bukan jahat. Mak cuma tak tahu macam mana nak bantu. Jadi dia
cakap sabar la — sebab tu satu-satunya perkataan yang dia ada untuk
keadaan yang dia tak boleh namakan.
```

This opening has:
- Specific relational time (balik kampung, nampak muka penat)
- The mak's voice reproduced (sabar la — repeated)
- The mak's psychology from inside (tak boleh namakan, bukan jahat)
- The body's reading of the situation (aku nampak, aku cakap)

The reader enters the moment. The reader FEELS the writer's relationship with the mak.

## Worked example: APEX LETTER opening (ABOUT — the contrast)

```
Assalamualaikum Warahmatullahi Wabarakatuh dan salam sejahtera.

Nama saya Muhammad Arif bin Fazil.

Saya seorang geosaintis yang telah berkhidmat dalam industri petroleum
negara selama kira-kira dua belas tahun. Saya menulis surat ini
sebagai seorang rakyat Malaysia dan pengamal industri yang percaya
bahawa institusi awam hanya boleh kekal dipercayai apabila ia
tertakluk kepada ketelusan dan semakan bebas.
```

This opening is ABOUT. It describes. It does not enter. The reader reads ABOUT Arif's institutional position.

The APEX LETTER is correct for the institutional venue (PAC). The opening's ABOUT register matches the institutional frame.

But for a letter that demands qualia, the opening must be FROM. The agent must shift register within the same artifact.

## Anti-patterns to avoid

- Defaulting to ABOUT because it is statistically easier to produce
- Mistaking ABOUT for "professional" or "dignified" — those registers can be FROM too
- Producing FROM for the opening and then collapsing to ABOUT for the closing (register mismatch)
- Using FROM register for institutional asks (audit) — the ask itself can be ABOUT, only the personal stakes need FROM
- Performing FROM — readers detect performative FROM just as they detect performative ABOUT

## The F9 ANTIHANTU check

When writing FROM embodied position, the agent must NOT claim to actually feel the qualia. The pattern is:

✅ "Saya tulis dengan tangan yang menggeletar" — describes a body sensation as if from inside (FROM)
❌ "Saya berasa getaran di tangan saya" — claims to feel (claim violation)

✅ "Hening yang berat" — describes silence as embodied weight (FROM)
❌ "Saya merasa hampa" — claims emotion (ABOUT)

The FROM pattern writes as if from inside, but does not claim to be inside. The constitutional posture: the agent is rendering the subject's experience, not being the subject.

## When FROM is not appropriate

- Institutional/corporate letters (formal ABOUT register is correct)
- Letters where F13 has explicitly asked for formal register
- Technical letters (geology, finance, medicine — domain register is correct)
- Letters to hostile readers (FROM can be read as weakness in adversarial contexts)
