# Physical Constraint Questions — Energy/Commodity Institutions

## The7 Questions

These are the questions to ask when analyzing ANY corporate communication from an energy, commodity, or infrastructure institution. Each question maps a physical reality that the communication is likely obscuring.

### Q1: Physical Burnout Rate
**Question:** What is the exact daily drawdown rate of strategic reserves under a zero-flow scenario, and what is the refinery yield penalty when blending non-standard crudes?

**What it reveals:** Whether "supply guaranteed until X" is a paper commitment or a physical guarantee.

**Data needed:** SPR inventory levels (usually classified), daily consumption, refinery configuration specs.

### Q2: Sourcing Arbitrage
**Question:** Which specific non-standard origins fill the supply gap, and what is the total landed cost premium per barrel?

**What it reveals:** The true daily cash drain from alternative sourcing. "Advanced alternative sourcing" is PR-speak.

**Data needed:** AIS ship-tracking, spot-market spreads, shipping bills, insurance premiums.

### Q3: Planning Horizon Collapse
**Question:** What specific operational thresholds cause the rolling planning horizon to compress into emergency rationing?

**What it reveals:** How close the institution is to physical supply failure.

**Data needed:** Inventory depletion rates, cancelled spot charters, refinery throughput cuts.

### Q4: Counterparty Insolvency
**Question:** At what price point do non-dominant market players face liquidity insolvency?

**What it reveals:** Hidden contagion risk in the supply chain. If 52% of market players cannot finance imports, the burden collapses onto the dominant player overnight.

**Data needed:** APM reimbursement lag times, distributor working capital, credit facility terms.

### Q5: Capex-Dividend Cannibalization
**Question:** How much dividend extraction can occur before upstream maintenance drops below decline-curve threshold?

**What it reveals:** The doom loop: extracting dividends to fund subsidies, starving capex, declining production, importing more, needing more subsidies.

**Data needed:** Sustaining capex requirements, natural decline rates, dividend payout ratios.

### Q6: Industrial Cascade
**Question:** What is the operational runway for non-energy dependent industries before supply disruptions cause shutdowns?

**What it reveals:** The 2nd-order collateral damage that hits BEFORE fuel pumps run dry. Energy crisis is the symptom; industrial cascade is the disease.

**Data needed:** Helium buffer levels at semiconductor fabs, fertilizer import sources, LNG contract terms.

### Q7: Demand Destruction Mathematics
**Question:** What price hike or quota ceiling is required to force meaningful consumption reduction, and what smuggling volume the system can tolerate?

**What it reveals:** Whether "efficiency is the first fuel" is actionable or aspirational. Usually: price-based demand destruction is politically impossible.

**Data needed:** Price elasticity estimates, border price differentials, smuggling enforcement capacity.

## The Pattern

Every institution will:
1. Answer Q1-Q7 with narrative, not data
2. Frame the physical constraint as a "challenge" or "opportunity"
3. Deflect specific numbers with "advanced" or "strategic" language
4. Never reveal the ACTUAL threshold at which emergency protocols activate

The silence on these7 questions IS the intelligence.
