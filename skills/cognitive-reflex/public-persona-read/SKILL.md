# Public Persona Read — Subject Not In His Life

Trigger: the sovereign forwards screenshots of a public profile (Instagram/TikTok posts, captions, bio, grid) and asks for persona / shadow / paradox analysis of that human; also "what kind of human is this" follow-ups, and cross-medium questions about how people's words differ by platform.

Boundary: the subject is a STRANGER. No person card, no lane file, no memory write exists for them and none may be created — the read is conversation-level only. Never claim access to the person; you read artifacts, and you say so.

## Depth: GENERAL by default (standing preference)

The deliverable is the person-shaped reading — structure, grammar, tensions — not forensics. UI metadata (bib numbers, like/comment counts, follower ratios, carousel-dot counts, exact handle spelling, device clock) is artifact, not the human; leading with it earns a flat cut-off and an order to restate at structure level. Go micro-forensic only when explicitly asked for verbatim text, counts, or an audit.

Follow-up shape: on "ni manusia macam mana", lead with ONE type sentence, then 3–6 short labelled traits. He escalates depth by asking — do not pre-empt with the full paradox grid.

## Procedure

1. Recover text from EVERY artifact before interpreting: caption verbatim, bio, highlights, dates, the other posts in the set. Vision/OCR transcripts are unreliable on repeated letters and on which of several similar handles is which — never rest a claim on a string read once.
2. Read the SET, not the post. Caption claim vs profile bio vs the next post's caption. Where the surfaces disagree is the finding; a single post yields only the persona.
3. Three moves, in order — **PERSONA** (grammar of the surface: which currency it trades — numbers, wound-arc, piety+grit) → **SHADOW** (only what is ABSENT: a mask is photographable, a shadow is not — read the COST the persona pays, never a hidden truth about a stranger) → **PARADOXES** (numbered pairs where both sides are true at once; hit the count exactly when he names one, e.g. "9 human paradox").
4. Close with the void named out loud — one frame of five, comments unread, read from the keyhole not the house — and tag the read corrigible: new posts may falsify it, and that is the contract.

## Never

- Forge or update a person card, lane file, or memory entry for a stranger.
- Psychologise: no motive, no diagnosis, no "the silence means X".
- **Convert a public record into a private-conduct claim about a named living person.** Charges,
  acquittals, verdicts and dates are the record, and may be reported as such. Sexual orientation,
  sexual conduct and private relationships are not the record. A direct "is he gay / bisexual /
  into younger men?" gets the documented timeline and stops there — no conclusion, no probability,
  no "most likely". A hedged verdict is still a verdict: it gets quoted back later as your finding.
  The failure mode to avoid is firmness in one turn and an invented conclusion in the next; decide
  the boundary once, in the first answer, and hold it for the rest of the conversation.
- Moralise the commercial layer. Profile links, sales tags, and defensive warnings mean identity is inventory — state it as structure (a wound that earns must keep the wound alive to keep working; a warning is a fence built before the person could be hurt), never as a charge.
- Adopt the profile's own framing as your vocabulary. Quote it as the surface's CLAIM, then read what claiming it costs.
