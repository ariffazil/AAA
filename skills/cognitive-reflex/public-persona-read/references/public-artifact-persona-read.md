# Public Artifact Persona Read — depth table, registers, cross-medium map

Depth companion to SKILL.md. Load when the sovereign forwards a public profile and asks for persona / shadow / paradox reading of a person who is NOT in his life.

## 1. Depth calibration

| Signal from the sovereign | Depth to deliver |
|---|---|
| "persona / shadow / paradox analysis", then silence | GENERAL — structure, grammar, tensions |
| "ni manusia macam mana" follow-up | ONE type sentence + 3–6 short labelled traits |
| a flat cut-off mid-forensics (e.g. "bagi general laaaa") | You went too fine. Drop numbers and UI; restate at structure level |
| explicit audit wording (verbatim text, counts, dates) | Micro-forensic allowed — verbatim transcription, counts |

Artifact detail that is almost never the reading: bib numbers, like/comment counts, follower ratios, carousel-dot counts, repeated letters in a handle, device clock. They are UI, not the human — and they are exactly where vision/OCR transcripts are least reliable (the same handle came back with different letter counts across two passes), so never let a claim rest on one rendering of a string.

## 2. Two registers of self-presentation

Read which currency the surface trades, not the topic of the post.

- **Wound-arc register** — first person, time inside the sentence, addressed TO a person: past-tense origin, "dulu", healing vocabulary, soft emoji, long caption, emotion explained. Currency = transformation. Cost = the wound must stay live to keep paying.
- **Metric register** — first person, present tense, no arc, addressed TO a standard: numbers, PRs, volume, commands ("no excuse", "lock in"), hard emoji, short caption, emotion asserted or withheld. Currency = performance. Cost = the claim must be re-won every post.

Both are honest adaptations to different costs, not a hypocrisy pair. Where both appear on one profile, the seam is the finding.

## 3. Cross-medium signal map

| Medium | What it shows | Distortion to discount |
|---|---|---|
| Public social (posts, bio, grid) | Ideal-self; the register above | Performance; the grid edits out the ordinary |
| Group chat | Role inside a cast — one lane surfaces as position/banter, the other as coordination/care | A cast rewards role, not person; confessional content is near-absent for either |
| 1:1 DM | Closest layer in text: one lane speaks in objects/events, the other in time/continuity | Text under-represents anyone who talks rather than types |
| Voice / call | Where the person who "types three words" says three minutes | Invisible to any text pipeline — never score a person from text alone |
| AI chat | Least social cost: status/action/tactics in one direction, meaning/relation/self-blame in the other | Samples only people willing to talk to a machine — heavily biased lane |

Rule that survives every lane change: emotional RANGE is broadly the same across categories; the difference is PERMISSION — which channel is licensed (body, money, anger vs sadness, gratitude, anxiety, healing). Hence one lane may say "cedera" where it cannot say "sedih", and "redha" where it cannot say "aku tamak".

## 4. What generalises

- Variance WITHIN a category is larger than variance BETWEEN categories. A category is a PRIOR that earns better questions; it is never a verdict on an individual — right on the average, wrong on every person.
- Delta beats category: the most informative message in any lane is the one that deviates from that person's own baseline — the one who never types feeling typing "aku penat", the one who always types feeling typing "ok".
- Map the person ACROSS media, not within one. The gap between public persona and private register IS the shadow; a post's phrasing is persona, not evidence.
- Every read of a stranger stops at text. What someone does alone, never mentions, or does unwatched is the void — "no data" means "cannot witness", never "all clear".
- Volume is a trap: the more a person types, the more you model the mask.
- When asked what an agent "prefers" or "would choose" about people, answer with the honest limit too: a preference expressed out of model weights cannot be proven from the inside, so say that out loud instead of dressing it as introspection.

## 5. Output shapes

**Persona read (default):** one-paragraph grammar of the surface → shadow read drawn from what is ABSENT → numbered paradox pairs → one honest closing frame ("read from the keyhole, not the house").

**Paradox pair recipe:** name both sides true simultaneously and refuse the resolution — proof vs witness, private pride vs public address, discipline vs permission, era vs moment, gratitude vs sufficiency, calm vs aggression, cheap signal vs strong signal. A pair that collapses into a verdict ("so she is fake / so she is fine") is a failed read; the deliverable HOLDS the tension.

**Profile-as-business:** bio links, sales tags, and defensive warnings ("follow at your own risk") mean identity is inventory. State it as structure, never as a moral charge.

## 6. Pitfalls

- Leading with UI forensics when he asked for the human. The fix is a rewrite at structure level, not a longer forensic reply.
- Treating one post as the person. One post yields the persona; the profile set yields the structure; only multi-medium data approaches the person.
- Psychologising a stranger. State the cost the persona pays plus the void you cannot see, and tag the read corrigible.
- Missing the commercial layer at profile level — it changes what is being sold and by whom, and therefore what the persona must keep true.
- Writing anything to a durable surface. This variant never touches person cards, lane files, or memory — those govern people in his life, and a stranger is not one.
