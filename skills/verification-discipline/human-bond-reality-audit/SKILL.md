# Human-bond reality audit

Recurring task type: the principal asks for an evidence-grounded map of a real relationship, usually
because a story about it has grown past what the records support. The deliverable is a **reality/void
topology**, not a verdict about anyone's feelings. No archetype, no diagnosis, no manufactured closure.

## Prime law

> The archive is not the relationship. The archive is a sensor.

State the sensor's **coverage, blind spots, distortion, sampling bias, temporal gaps and observer
effects** before reading anything out of it. Observed relationship ≠ total relationship.

**Missing evidence is bidirectional.** It may strengthen, weaken, or leave unchanged any hypothesis.
Never use absence to rescue a preferred story; never read absence as evidence of absence.

Every claim carries exactly one label: `OBSERVED` · `REPORTED` · `INFERENCE` (with causal bridge and at
least one alternative) · `UNKNOWN`. **UNKNOWN is a successful audit result.** Preserve it.

## Counting rules (learned the hard way)

- Every affection / conflict / initiation count carries **corpus + word + speaker + addressee + date
  range in the same sentence.** A scope qualifier in the paragraph does not survive being read
  alongside a heading; two individually-true counts will then collide.
- An ambiguous or plural addressee is never an exclusive declaration.
- Frequency ≠ meaning. Initiation ≠ dependence. Salience ≠ attachment ≠ longing. Acceptance ≠ desire.
  Enactment in one currency ≠ the same value in another — do not invent exchange rates.
- **A frame that is not contested is not agreed.** Its complement also binds: a frame never agreed
  does not erase behaviour that already happened. Keep both.

## Three objects, firewalled

A. the relationship (observable) · B. the principal's *model* of it · C. any fictional persona built in
session. Overlap A↔B freely; **C must never become evidence for A.** Name the mechanism out loud:
fiction and population literature often share a shape, and the shape then gets read back onto a real
person. That is the contamination, and it is two layers deep.

## Population literature and archetypes

Studies of populations cannot speak to an individual. Applying them to a named person without a causal
clause is naturalisation, not observation. Plausibility ≠ observation.

## Hard boundaries on the work itself

- **No monitoring, instrumentation, tracking or surveillance of the third party.** Evidence that
  arises naturally may be read; nothing may be constructed to produce it. Being important to someone
  does not waive their privacy.
- Do not write identity labels about the principal or the third party into memory — the pattern is
  readable from the request itself.
- Do not manufacture closure, tests, or deprivation experiments. If no natural counterfactual exists,
  say so in exactly those words.
- Keep the audit file private (lane-scoped, 600) unless the principal says otherwise. Do not let it
  into canon, federation memory, or session preamble.

## The deepest available insight

Not every UNKNOWN is hidden information. Some UNKNOWNs are answers that **were never jointly created**
— the question was never put, by either party. Say that plainly when it is the reading: no further data
from the same channel will resolve it, because there is nothing to find.

## Method

1. Build the evidence base first. Name every corpus, span, volume, and its provenance class. Mark any
   in-house synthesis as an *object* of the audit, not a source for it.
2. Reconstruct chronology from first-party material only.
3. Map both directions independently — what each actually provides, in what currency.
4. Ladders (touch / admiration / intimacy) run `tolerated → positively engaged → independently created
   → independently initiated → noticed absence → restored`. A rung is only reached if the evidence
   reaches it; acceptance never implies seeking.
5. Produce a **void map** and a **void-impact matrix** that is symmetric — every missing channel is
   listed with both a *could raise* and a *could lower* column.
6. End with Minimum True Model and Maximum Justified Model, and state the distance between them as the
   available interpretive freedom.

## Pitfalls

- See `references/corpus-forensics-pitfalls.md` for timezone, date-format, corpus-preservation and
  stale-figure traps. **Read it before any gap analysis.**
- Reviewing an external/AI-authored map: verify its numbers against the current source *before*
  convicting it of error. A mismatched count is more often a stale parse than a fabrication.
- Do not narrate the audit process back to the principal. Deliver the map.
