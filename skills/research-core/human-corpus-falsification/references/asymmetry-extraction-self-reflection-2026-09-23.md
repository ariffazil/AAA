# Worked Example: Asymmetry Extraction → Self-Reflection (Laletha + Kak Sue, 2026-09-23)

## Session shape
Arif sent two WhatsApp exports in a single turn (Laletha ~8yr, Sue Sukri 1-night) and asked:
*"Tell me what THEY want. And what they don't want. And how to achieve peace in MY life."*

Triple request, single message — wants, don't-wants, self-direction. The shape matters:
**it is not third-party profiling, it is relational asymmetry extraction as mirror for the principal's own choices.**

## What the request actually is
- Not a third-person dossier (he has these people's cards already, or doesn't need them).
- Not a content summary ("what did they talk about").
- It is: **read the relational contract from their messages; reflect back what Arif is paying into it; show him where he has surplus to spend elsewhere.**
- The implicit final move: *"I'm not happy giving a piece of my brain to them actually."* That is the **real** question. The "what do they want" framing is the request shape — the underlying state is extraction-fatigue.

## Procedure

1. **Run the structural parse first.** Per-speaker counts, date range, who-initiates-who, message-asymmetry. Without numbers, every later sentence is decoration.
2. **Surface wants (what they request):** enumerate the operational / emotional / professional asks visible in their lines. Group by class (errands, social glue, technical judgement, emotional containment). Show the *recurring* shape — single asks are noise, repeated asks are the contract.
3. **Surface don't-wants (what they never ask):** as important as what they DO ask. "Never requests emotional labour" / "never reciprocates technical depth" / "never asks how Arif is doing" are data points. They reveal what the other party considers out-of-scope for Arif — which is itself a boundary they are drawing *for* him, whether he notices or not.
4. **Surface the unspoken obligation Arif carries:** the load he absorbs without being asked — apologies, venting-receipt, patience under pressure, professional reliability above what the role requires. Count the "sorry"s, the emotional-containment offers, the times he minimizes his own cost.
5. **Connect to self-direction:** the asymmetry is the answer to "how to achieve peace". Not a meditation. Not advice. The mechanism is *visible in the corpus already*. State the mechanism, list 1-3 reversible moves (reply-length = their question length; stop apologising for things that aren't his fault; one honest test-conversation per relationship), stop.

## Pitfalls (rules)

### A. Don't build the third-party map
The temptation is to write a Laletha-card or a Kak Sue-card. The user didn't ask for that. The user asked for **his own peace**. The other party is a structural input, not the deliverable. Reading the export is a means; reflecting the asymmetry back to Arif is the end.

### B. Don't moralize the asymmetry
"It's unfair", "they're exploiting you", "you deserve better" — these are moralizing framings that produce a defensiveness cycle. The cleaner framing is **structural**: this is the exchange that exists; you can continue it, renegotiate it, or reduce it; each option has a cost and a benefit. The decision is yours.

### C. Don't recommend cutting contact (unless unsafe)
Arif's question was "how to achieve peace", not "should I leave". The default answer is **re-calibrate the exchange, not sever it**. Most colleagues can coexist at lower surface-area without drama; recommending severance when re-calibration works is over-engineering and reads as the agent preferring its own moral clarity to the user's actual life.

### D. The "sorry" count is a structural finding
When one party sends materially more apologies than the other in a working relationship — that asymmetry is the extractive pattern made visible. Don't gloss it. Name the count, name what each apology was paying for, name the asymmetry.

### E. Mirror-mode rules from `text-forensics/references/anis-mirror-decode-2026-08-15.md` still apply
- Lead with mechanism, not judgment.
- Escalate depth only on his signal (`"aku x faham"` → collapse to ≤4 facts + 1 binary).
- User veto is final, instantly.
- Close with one reversible move, not a programme.
- No unsolicited pings after.

## Reply shape (template)

```
[Operational wants — bullet class, 4-6 lines, named with date examples]
[Don't-wants — bullet class, 3-4 lines, named with absence]
[Asymmetry Arif carries — 4-5 lines, with count of "sorry"s and emotional-containment events]
[3 reversible moves, ordered lightest to heaviest]
[One line acknowledging the harder truth: the extraction is system-shaped, not these-people-shaped]
[One open question or stop]
```

BM Penang, short paragraphs, no tables, no verdict labels. Chat export → structural read the principal can use tomorrow.

## What NOT to do (this session's near-misses)

- Don't do a tier-1 to tier-5 forensic dump from `text-forensics` Phase 5. That's the third-party profile template; the request shape is different.
- Don't use `[OBS]/[DER]/[INT]` labels. The principal reads human language, not epistemic-state markers.
- Don't open a fourth "what about Syed" thread. Syed was in the prior turn's context but the current turn is Laletha + Kak Sue — stay on the asked lane.
- Don't offer to draft a "boundary-setting email". A test-text (one line, calm, factual) is reversible and dignified; a full email is the agent over-engineering.
- Don't ask 4 sub-questions to fill corpus gaps. The structural parse already tells you enough to reflect back; ask only one focused thing if needed.
