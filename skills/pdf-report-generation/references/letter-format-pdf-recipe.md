# Letter / Memorandum PDF Recipe

Proven on 2026-08-09 (open letter to Malaysian MPs about PETRONAS governance). Refined 2026-08-12 after external Claude audit surfaced 3 false factual claims and AI-feel failure modes — see "Pre-write F2 audit" and Pitfalls sections below.

**For soul-first civic letters (open letter to Parliament, "BIG BANG" dispatch, writer's survival depends on being HEARD)** — see `references/civic-letter-recipe.md` instead. That recipe carries the Marhaen voice, hash-handling pattern, and apex-judge isolation flow for T3 dispatches.

## When to use
- "Surat terbuka" / "open letter" / "memorandum" / "formal letter" requests
- Any text-heavy PDF that is NOT a chart-heavy data report

## Key difference from chart-heavy reports
The main `pdf-report-generation` skill is optimised for chart-heavy SOVEREIGN-grade briefings (14mm side margins, chart-first layout, KPI hero rows). Letter-format PDFs are the opposite: **text-first, no charts, no KPI rows, standard document margins**.

## Margin rules (Arif: "Buruk margin. Fail.")
- **Letter/memorandum:** `@page { margin: 20mm 22mm; }` — standard professional letter margins. NOT the 14mm used for chart-heavy reports.
- **If the letter has tables:** keep margins at 20mm/22mm. Tables should fit within the text column, not bleed to edges.
- **For a formal letter addressed to officials:** margins can go wider (25mm sides) but NEVER wider than 30mm — Arif rejects "too much white space."

## Tersurat/tersirat writing technique (proven 2026-08-09)
For political commentary letters, Arif wants "tersurat tersirat" (explicit/implicit) layers:

- **Tersurat (explicit):** Corporate governance critique — board structure, financial metrics, peer comparison
- **Tersirat (implicit):** Systemic exploitation pattern — reader connects the dots without explicit naming

Example from the PETRONAS letter:
> "Apabila seseorang mengambil sesuatu yang bukan miliknya, dan apabila yang diambil itu tidak pernah cukup, dan apabila setiap kali dia mengambil lagi dia memberi alasan yang sama — itu bukan strategi. Itu naluri."
This technique avoids inflammatory analogies while making the implicit message clear to Malaysian readers. The Gemini audit scored the direct geopolitical analogy 1/10; the tersurat/tersirat version passed Apex review.

## Pre-write F2 audit (applies to corporate letters too) — caught 2026-08-12

For soul-first civic letters (open letter to Parliament, "BIG BANG" dispatch, when the writer's survival depends on being HEARD), see `references/civic-letter-recipe.md` instead. That recipe carries the voice, signoff, hash handling, and apex-judge isolation patterns for T3 dispatches.

For corporate letters specifically, the F2 audit still applies — but the failure modes are different. Live audit caught these in the 2026-08-09 PETRONAS corporate letter:

| Claim in original | Audit finding |
|---|---|
| "Shell & BP tidak membuang 5,000 pekerja" | BP potong 4,700 + 3,000 kontraktor; Shell jual Deer Park, Mobile | FALSE — defeats comparison |
| "CEO menerima perpanjangan tanpa siasatan awam" | Ada Jawatankuasa Pilihan Khas Parlimen Okt 2025 | FALSE — MP tahu dari pengetahuan sendiri |
| "Tuan punya tanah ambil alih" → imply cause-effect dengan letupan | Punca rasmi: 25-year subsidence, bukan keputusan CEO | FALSE — geoscientist pun tahu |

Audit workflow for corporate letters: list every factual claim, verify each against primary sources, especially peer comparisons (verify BOTH sides) and cause-effect (separate technical from governance cause). Cost of skipping: kerjaya + reputasi. Audit cost: 20 minutes. Use independent model (Gödel Lock E3); self-audit is F9 violation.

## Pitfalls

- **Using chart-report margins (14mm) on a letter** — makes text bleed too close to edges, looks unprofessional. Arif rejected this with "Buruk margin. Fail."
- **Font choice** — Times New Roman or Georgia for formal letters. NOT sans-serif (that's for reports/dashboards).
- **`text-align: justify`** on body text — standard for formal letters in BM/English. Left-aligned looks casual.
- **Double-line border on h1** (`border-bottom: 3px double #000`) — standard formal letter convention for the main title.
- **Tables in letters** — use `font-size: 10pt` (smaller than body) to keep them compact. Full-width with collapse borders.
- **AI branding in footer** — Gemini audit flagged "Hermes ASI" and "arifOS Kernel" as "pseudo-scientific jargon" in parliamentary context. Use neutral attribution: "Warga Agentik AAA" or just the constitutional reference without AI model names.
- **Geopolitical analogies** — Gemini scored direct Israel/Palestine comparison 1/10 ("inappropriate, logically incoherent, completely undermines professional credibility"). Use tersurat/tersirat instead.
- **AI-sounding institutional sender names** (added 2026-08-12) — even in corporate letters, generic "AGENTIK INTELLIGENCE STATE AGENT" / "Hermes ASI" / "Lane 333-AGI × 777-FORGE × 888-APEX" labels read as AI-generated. Keep corporate labels short and procedural ("Saksi: AGENTIK AAA · F13 SOVEREIGN · Lihat rujukan"), not theatrical.
- **"PENDING" in seal block** (added 2026-08-12) — letter said "Constitutional Seal: VAULT999 SEQUENTIAL — pending seal" inside a block titled "SIGNOFF". The text itself contradicted the seal claim. Remove or actually seal before sending.
- **Number understatement** (added 2026-08-12) — "150 cedera, 200 rumah rosak" when real numbers are 87 musnah+148 perlu baiki+1,254 terjejas makes the writer look like they're softening for political reasons. Use official figures, never round down.
- **Sending to 99 recipients = announcement, not targeted submission** (added 2026-08-12) — applies to corporate letters too: ONE targeted recipient (the actual decider) beats 99 broadcasts. The PAC chair for governance critique, the relevant committee chair for technical critique, the ministry for policy critique.
