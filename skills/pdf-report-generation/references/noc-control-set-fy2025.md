## Reading rules

- **Payout %**: 70–80% is the NOC median, NOT an anomaly. PETRONAS sits on the median. Aramco's 91.5% is the high outlier; Petrobras' 37% is the low outlier (capex-cyclical).
- **Net debt / capital**: 15–25% is normal for cash-rich NOCs; 0–10% is Gulf-state balance sheet (Aramco, ADNOC); 30%+ is over-leveraged for an NOC.
- **FCF after capex + dividend**: must be positive to be investable. NOC median of post-capex+div FCF is negative on these ratios — exactly the "extraction crisis" reading that AMEND-2026-08-03-001 was engineered to flag. Treat that flag as a structural NOC phenomenon, not as a Petronas-specific signal.
- **Governance composition**: most state-controlled NOCs have a dual-hat chair (executive on parent + chair on subsidiary) and a state CFO on the subsidiary board. PETRONAS's Gentari (Taufik + Liza Mustapha dual-hat) is on the spectrum, not off it. Independent NED ratios are universally low; the scoreboard needs the comparator to be informative.

## Use protocol

1. **Build the table BEFORE the verdict line.** If you cannot populate at least 5 of 6 rows, do not publish the verdict — flag as INADMISSIBLE-QQQ-INCOMPLETE.
2. **Tag every cell OBS** with the source URL or document; DER if modelled; INT if a sovereign target.
3. **Show the median and range explicitly.** Single-NOC single-number verdicts are the W-007 defect.
4. **ConocoPhillips-style IOC exits are leading indicators** for cost-of-capital shifts inside a jurisdiction — they belong under SPINE-layer readings, not as headline events.
5. **If the target's own data point changes between sessions**, recompute the ratio against the latest peer snapshot — do not back-fit.
