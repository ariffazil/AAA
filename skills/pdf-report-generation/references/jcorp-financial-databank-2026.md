## Diversification Model

JCorp demonstrates **organic compound growth** through:
1. **Healthcare first** — KPJ grew from single hospital to ~20 facilities over decades
2. **Cross-vertical synergy** — healthcare supports plantation worker welfare, property supports hospital catchment
3. **Market-driven expansion** — each vertical grew because there was demand, not because of "roadmap" mandates
4. **Reinvestment discipline** — ~40% of earnings reinvested vs extracted

## Comparison Framework (Petronas vs JCorp)

### Diversification Score
- **Petronas:** 4 segments (Upstream, Gas, Downstream, Corporate) but 85% revenue from oil/gas
- **JCorp:** 5 segments with healthcare dominant (56%) but meaningful agri (23%) + property (17%)

### Growth Score
- **Petronas FY2025:** Revenue -17%, PAT -18%, declining reserves
- **JCorp FY2025:** Revenue +10%, PAT +116%, expanding operations

### Resilience Score
- **Petronas:** High commodity exposure, dividend extraction >70% of PAT
- **JCorp:** Lower commodity exposure, dividend ~15% of PAT, operational reserves

## Key Insight for Reports

**Both are GLCs under government ownership.** The divergence is NOT about ownership structure — it's about:
1. **Capital allocation discipline** (extract vs reinvest)
2. **Market-driven vs mandate-driven growth**
3. **Organic compounding vs forced diversification**

JCorp proves that a GLC can achieve sustainable growth without being tied to commodity cycles. Petronas attempts top-down forced diversification (Gentari, Mesra café) that generates zero revenue while bleeding cash.
