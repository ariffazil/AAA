## The Sabar + Reflect + Ask 9 Questions Protocol

### Step 1: Stop all auto-action

When sovereign says "Sabar", kill any running dispatches. Don't continue with planned actions. The sovereign is signaling a meta-level reorientation.


### Step 3: Reflect honestly

What was strong? What was weak? What got the real goal vs what got the diplomatic version?

For each artifact, ask:
- Did this iteration preserve the sovereign's TRUE intent?
- Did it advance the constitutional path or dilute it?
- Did it produce qualia or institutional pretense?
- Was the tone appropriate for the dispatch context?

### Step 4: Surface 9 questions

Strategic questions that the sovereign needs to answer to clarify his own intent. Each question must be deep enough to force real reflection, not surface enough to be answered without thinking.

**Sample questions (adapt to context):**

1. **What's the real goal?** (audit vs removal vs both — be honest)
2. **What cost are you willing to bear?** (job loss, family exposure, MSS letter, public shame)
3. **What's success?** (audit dijalankan, akhbar publish, 5,000 kawan dipulihkan, Parlimen Hansard record)
4. **What's failure?** (MSS letter, NAIO spin, family kena libatkan)
5. **Hidden audience** — siapa yang SEBENARNYA perlu baca? (isteri, ibu bapa, anak sulung, rakan PETRONAS, ARIF sendiri)
6. **Identity calculus** — named identity still serves? What skeletons in closet?
7. **Mak voice scope** — where does mak appear? (Letter A vs C vs single anchor line)
8. **Post-dispatch** — apa jadi selepas? Akhbar? Telegram viral? Fire-and-forget?
9. **Voice selection** — Tenang institutional OR Qualia OR Bake both?

**Question quality matters:** Each question should force the sovereign to confront what he really wants, not what he thinks he should want. The agent's job is to surface the questions, not provide answers.

### Step 5: Wait for sovereign's answers

The sovereign's answers may surprise. They may contradict previous signals. That's the point — the reflection moment is when the sovereign clarifies his own intent.

Do NOT:
- Provide your own answers
- Push the sovereign toward a specific option
- Use the answers to "build the right surat"
- Skip ahead to producing

DO:
- Acknowledge each answer as constitutional input
- Surface follow-up clarifications if ambiguous
- Document the answers as part of the iteration ledger

### Step 6: Reflect back the synthesis

Once the sovereign has answered, reflect back:
- "Here's what I'm hearing..."
- "If I understand correctly, the real goal is X, and the constraint is Y..."
- "I'll produce v15 with these constraints honored. Confirm?"

This ensures the sovereign's answers are captured correctly before production.

## When to use this protocol

**Trigger conditions:**
- Sovereign says "Sabar" (have patience) + reflective language
- Sovereign asks reflective questions ("What do u think?", "Why don't u ask me?")
- Iteration convergence trap (v[N+1] ≈ v[N], no breakthrough)
- Multi-reversal session (3+ reversals in single day)
- Sovereign redirects from previous trajectory mid-stream
- Sovereign issues meta-directive ("I just want X", "I don't care about Y")

**Anti-trigger:**
- Sovereign is in execution mode (clear, single, simple request)
- Sovereign wants the next iteration, not reflection
- Production deadline pressure

## Failure modes to avoid

### 1. Treating Sabar as a delay cue

Wrong: "Sabar = wait. Don't act. Let sovereign type more."
Right: "Sabar = pause + reflect + surface what sovereign really wants. The sovereign is asking for meta-workflow activation."

### 2. Asking questions without depth

Wrong: "Do you want a 1-letter or 3-letter portfolio?"
Right: "What's the real goal — audit, removal, or both? What's the success metric? What cost are you willing to bear?"

### 3. Skipping the inventory step

Wrong: "I know what I've produced so far, let me just continue."
Right: "Let me map all artifacts from the beginning of the session. You asked me to reflect, so reflection requires the full picture."

### 4. Providing your own answers

Wrong: "Based on what you've said, here's what I think you want..."
Right: "Here are the questions. I won't answer them for you. The reflection is yours."

### 5. Rushing back to production after Sabar

Wrong: Sovereign says Sabar, agent pauses 10 seconds, then resumes dispatch.
Right: Sovereign says Sabar, agent does full inventory + reflection + 9 questions. Only after sovereign answers does production resume.
