## What worked / what didn't
- **Worked**: quoted-phrase web_search on FB page content → snippets carried prices, hours, phone, promo dates ("Here our New Rate for 2025", "MERDEKA PROMO RM70... applicable on 31st August 2023", "closed 29th & 30th June 2023"). IG location page `instagram.com/explore/locations/50413008/dpopeye-gym` rendered logged-out via browser (owner handle, 3,066 posts, price level, open/closed). Wong Hong.net athlete interview gave full owner bio (DOB, height 5'3", contest weight 65-68kg, career, injury history). NPC News Online contest page extractable.
- **Failed**: web_extract on all FB URLs (empty), browser_navigate to www/m.facebook (login dialog, clicking Close didn't help), IG profile page `instagram.com/<handle>` (HTTP failure), FB video content itself (title only).
- **Namesake trap**: Reps-ID article about "Iwan Setiawan" (One Gym, Jakarta, b. 1976) is a DIFFERENT person; Wikipedia hit is an Indonesian footballer (b. 1958). Disambiguation via DOB/residence/occupation.
