## Key design decisions

### DIGNITY got a chair (666)
Old surface: dignity was a sub-bullet inside CRITIQUE_PROMPT (555_critique). New surface: 666 ⚖ DIGNITY is first-class, same rank as JUDGE and SEAL. The weakest stakeholder now has a hook on the 000-999 ladder.

### PLAN was added (222)
Old surface had no verb for "architecture of action" — agents jumped from SENSE (111) to REASON (333) without mapping reversibility. 222 🏛 PLAN fills that gap.

### REMEMBER was promoted (555)
Old surface: `arif_memory` was an orphan tool (no matching prompt). New: 555 🗂 REMEMBER = "Memory without provenance is not truth" — one of the most cited federation rules, now a first-class hook.

### GOVERN carries 4 invariant gates
- Gödel lock: every loop touches reality before becoming doctrine
- Anti-sink: Signal → Verify → Reality → Integrate (no amplified garbage)
- Entropy: ΔS ≤ 0 per cycle
- VOID discipline: dead branches stay dead without new evidence

### Legacy wisdom distillation
Legacy prompts contained 500+ lines of distilled governance (F2 provenance gate, F5/F6 dignity scan, EVOI discipline, hypothesis falsification). The 33/13 refactor extracted the 3-5 most binding rules from each legacy prompt and embedded them in the new hook's meta description + prompt body. No wisdom was dropped; the prose that carried it was.

## What was removed and why

- 8 legacy aliases (111_sense through arif_init_prompt_v3): duplicated zen-sigil prompts, caused agent confusion (two canonical names for one concept)
- 4 dynamic-number indices (atlas-repo, aaa-index, aforge-index, vault999-index): numbers change hourly, agents expect static truth
- arifos://resources/index (alias), mcp-alignment (technical), mcp/surface-map (duplicate of schema)
- 6 atlas333 sub-resources merged into atlas333/paradox/list
- 3 wisdom sub-resources merged into wisdom/quotes/all
- arifos://index shrunk from 30KB to a 5-chamber pointer (map smaller than territory = meta-chaos)
