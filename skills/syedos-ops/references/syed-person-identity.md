### Open laparotomy recovery context (educational)

- **Incision:** 10-15 cm midline or transverse (NOT keyhole)
- **Healing:** 6-8 weeks minimum for fascia; full tensile strength ~3 months
- **Dressing cadence:** 2 weeks/visit is normal for clean healing wound
- **"Surgeon sendiri nak dressing":** In government hospital (HKL), dressing is normally done by junior doctor or staff nurse. When the operating surgeon personally follows up, it signals (a) strong ownership, (b) wound needs senior eyes. **Preserve this doctor relationship** — ask this surgeon directly about Op 2 timing.
- **Line removal** = surgeon thinks the line is no longer needed = good recovery signal
- **Watch signs (ER escalation):** wound berair (oozing), berbau (foul smell), merah meluas (spreading erythema), atau fever recurrence
- **One question per follow-up visit:** "Doktor, bila nak schedule operation kedua untuk CBD tu?" — don't overload Syed with question checklists; the surgeon is already handling the rest.
