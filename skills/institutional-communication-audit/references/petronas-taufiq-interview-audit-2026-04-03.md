## Phase 4: 7 Physical Constraint Questions

### Q1: Physical Burnout Rate
- Malaysia has **no formal SPR** (reviewing as of Jul 2026)
- Commercial reserves: **~30-45 days**
- Under zero-Hormuz: **~1 month** before minimum operating level
- Yield penalty on non-standard crudes: **2-5%** on middle distillates

### Q2: Sourcing Arbitrage
- Non-Hormuz landed cost premium: **+$30-45/bbl**
- Additional daily drain: **~$17.5M/day** (RM75M/day)
- Annual impact: **~$6.4B** additional import cost

### Q3: 45-Day Blind-Spot
- Already implementing pre-rationing: RON95 cap 300L→200L/month (Apr 2026)
- Diesel unified to RM2.10 nationwide (Jul 2026) to curb smuggling
- Next compression = 15-day emergency rationing

### Q4: Counterparty Insolvency
- Non-Petronas (52% market) need ~RM3.6B/year working capital float
- Shell-Petronas active litigation (~RM1B outstanding) = systemic risk
- If major distributor pulls back, 100% burden collapses onto Petronas

### Q5: Capex-Dividend Cannibalization
- Capex fell 31% H1 2025
- Dividend reduced 38% (RM32B → RM20B) but PAT also declining
- 350K bpd is a **declining curve**, not constant — requires capex to slow

### Q6: Industrial Cascade
- Qatar helium (33% global) offline since March 2026
- Semiconductor impact window: **NOW** (August 2026)
- Fertilizer prices +15-20%, 30% of global trade transits Hormuz
- Malaysia plantation sector + food security directly affected

### Q7: Demand Destruction Math
- RON95 subsidy gap tripled (RM0.55 → RM2.03/liter in 9 weeks)
- No price point achieves 15% reduction without smuggling explosion
- Malaysia-Thailand price gap too large for price-based demand destruction
- Government using **quantity rationing** (caps), not price

## Phase 5: Synthesis

### What They Said
Petronas President went on government TV to explain the energy crisis,
reassure the public about supply, and counter populist attacks.

### What They're Preventing You From Calculating
The total daily cash drain on the Malaysian economy: ~RM50-80M/day
additional cost vs pre-crisis baseline. RM1.5-2.4B/month. The number IS
the crisis.

### The Shadow
59 minutes explaining why the house is hot while never mentioning the
fire in the basement (Petros crisis, Shell litigation, governance decay,
5,200 layoffs, capex starvation). External enemy real; internal enemy
is the CEO himself.

### The Meta-Observation
The interview is a **controlled disclosure** that:
- ✅ Addresses external crisis (global oil, Hormuz, supply)
- ✅ Addresses public concerns (why import, price breakdown)
- ✅ Addresses political attacks (Petronas untung, contribution)
- ❌ Avoids internal crisis (Petros, Shell, governance, BOD)
- ❌ Avoids structural crisis (PAT decline, rightsizing, talent drain)
- ❌ Avoids accountability (who decided SEARAH? who approved Shell litigation?)

**Score: 3 addressed, 9 NOT addressed out of 12 paradoxes. 25% coverage.**
