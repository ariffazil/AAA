## Key Insight: The Silence IS the Signal

When an institution stops making public projections about supply:
1. They can no longer guarantee timelines
2. They're managing at the molecule level (ship-by-ship)
3. Any public statement would cause panic or be immediately disproven
4. The crisis has transitioned from "acute shock" to "structural bleed"

## The Doom Loop Identified

```
Extract dividends → Fund subsidies → Starve upstream capex
    → Production declines → Import more → Pay premium
    → Need more subsidies → Extract more dividends → REPEAT
```

Every ringgit extracted for today's crisis is a ringgit NOT invested in tomorrow's production. The 23% capex cut in FY2025 suggests this cannibalization is ALREADY happening.

## Methodology: How to Build an August Anchor

1. **Re-probe prices:** What is the commodity actually trading at NOW?
2. **Check guarantee expiry:** Did the stated timeline hold?
3. **Look for silence:** What stopped being reported? Silence = operational blackout
4. **Trace capex:** Is investment continuing or being cut?
5. **Check industrial spillover:** Are 2nd-order effects (helium, fertilizer) materializing?
6. **Assess demand response:** Has consumption actually changed, or just been invisible-rered?
7. **Map the trajectory:** Improving, stable, or deteriorating? Each parameter gets a direction.
