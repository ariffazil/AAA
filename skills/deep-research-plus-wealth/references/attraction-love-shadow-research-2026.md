## Core studies (OBS — experimentally verified)

| Study | Claim | Falsifier |
|---|---|---|
| Zajonc 1980, "Feeling and Thinking: Preferences Need No Inferences" (Am. Psychol. 35:151) | Affective primacy: feelings precede cognition; subliminal mere-exposure creates preference without recognition | No priming effect at suboptimal exposure |
| Murphy & Zajonc 1993 (JPSP 64(5):723) | At suboptimal (subliminal) exposure only affective primes shift judgment; at optimal exposure cognitive primes dominate | Reversal of the exposure×prime interaction |
| LeDoux 1996, The Emotional Brain | Dual pathway: low road (senses→thalamus→amygdala, ~20 ms, subcortical, unconscious) vs high road (→cortex→amygdala, ~200 ms, conscious). Body responds before mind knows | Lesion mapping disproving the amygdala fast path |
| Wedekind et al. 1995 (Proc. R. Soc. B 260:245) | Sweaty t-shirt: women (not on contraceptive pill) prefer scent of MHC-dissimilar men — immune-compatibility screening at olfactory level, pre-conscious | No MHC-scent preference under controlled conditions |

## INT layer — Jung (archetypal, not experimentally falsifiable)

- Jung 1938/1969, The Archetypes and the Collective Unconscious: **persona** = mask for the world; **shadow** = rejected unconscious content ("aku bukan macam tu"); the larger the persona, the larger the concealed shadow (ratio fixed — one cannot grow without the other).
- **Sado archetype reading** (the user's own intrigue, "how abang sado jadi sado"): small/weak boy → builds muscle as a wall over the shadow ("aku takkan kembali ke benda tu") → every kg lifted is wall, not identity → kampung/mak = the one place the wall is useless, hence the pull home. The quietness at the kampung is the shadow surfacing.
- Attraction to the persona-shadow gap: people are drawn to the tension between what someone shows and what they hide — but the shadow is inaccessible until the person chooses to reveal it. Falling for the persona, frustrated by the shadow.
- Love (synthesis): biology + awareness — "aku tahu ini bukan semua aku yang pilih, tapi aku pilih untuk tinggal."

## Synthesis (DER/INT)

- **Filter tak jatuh — filter memang tak pernah naik.** The conscious "who is she?" (200 ms high road) arrives ~180 ms after the low road locked in (20 ms). What feels like "filters crashing down" is the conscious mind catching up to a decision already made.
- **Dopamine→oxytocin handoff:** dopamine dies on a clock (~6–14 month decay); oxytocin builds slowly on shared experience. Relationship dies if oxytocin doesn't arrive before dopamine fades.
- **Omega question:** if attraction is pre-conscious (MHC, dopamine, low road, Fisher template all decide before awareness), who is morally responsible for who they love? Culture judges entry (no choice) and exit (possibly biologically correct) — biology explains mechanism, does not settle the ought.
