agriculture, informal lag). Pattern: advance headline sticks in media, final
revision gets no coverage. **Never quote advance as fact — check the final date.**

## The 7-Holes framework — "how institutions lie with (correct) statistics"
Trigger: Arif says "I don't believe that number" / "institution tipu dengan
statistik". Frame first: **DOSM is not lying** — SNA 2008 methodology + IMF
SDDS; the methodology itself has gaps. "Nombor betul boleh menipu" (correct
numbers can deceive). Then deliver all seven, each with a Malaysia example AND
a falsification test:

1. **Advance vs final** — advance published on partial data, final revised
   quietly; headline sticks, revision doesn't. Test: check the final on release
   date (14 Aug for Q2 2026).
2. **Per capita vs median** — GDP per capita ~US$15,085 vs B40 median
   ~RM3,500/mo; growth accrues to foreign capital (factory wages +2.5% vs GDP
   +5.6% = 3.1% gap). Test: median income trend, not mean.
3. **Transfer pricing / re-exports** — total exports count re-exports at full
   value; value-added may be 5–10% (chip in from TSMC RM100, test+pack RM15,
   out to Intel RM115 — GDP counts RM115). IMF Article IV flags raising
   domestic value-added. Test: domestic vs total exports gap.
4. **Subsidies inflate GDP** — subsidies sit in G (government final consumption
   expenditure): subsidy up → G up → GDP up, while households only avoid
   choking. Circular: subsidy ↓ CPI → ↓ deflator → ↑ real GDP. Test: deflator
   vs CPI divergence.
5. **Deflator is chosen** — real GDP = nominal ÷ deflator; low deflator
   flatters growth. Test: compare GDP deflator to CPI.
6. **Informal economy invisible** — ~30% of workers informal (World Bank 2024);
   pasar malam sellers, grab drivers, maids, kampung trades undercounted;
   headline GDP misses them and the distribution is wrong. Test: Informal
   Sector Survey share (>25% = headline misleading).
7. **One state pulls the average** — Penang+Johor = 57.9% exports; national %
   hides flat Kelantan/Terengganu/Kedah. Test: GDP-by-state.
