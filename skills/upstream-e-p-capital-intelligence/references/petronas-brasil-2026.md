## Key lesson
The "loss" headline is mostly accounting timing: forward Sépia-2 capex commitment (~US$1.4bn nominal) discounts heavily, and producing-asset cashflow (TV/EM-III repaid its US$1.3bn acquisition in ~7 yrs — Welligence: "likely already repaid acquisition cost") is the hidden offset. Always split gross / accounting-sunk / economic-sunk before delivering a verdict.
