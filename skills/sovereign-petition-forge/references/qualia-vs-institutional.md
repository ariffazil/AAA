## What is Qualia?

Qualia = "the felt experience, the inner quality of being" — not the logical
structure, but the *way it feels to be in this situation*.

In a sovereign petition, qualia means:
- Sensory anchors: "Pukul 3 pagi, cahaya bilik saya tak padam"
- Embodied detail: "kopi sudah sejuk", "mata yang lain", "tangan menggeletar"
- Real voices: verbatim WhatsApp messages from family, not archetype
- Ambiguity preserved: "Aku tak tahu apa jadi lepas ni — tapi aku tak boleh diam lagi"
- Conflict without resolution: sabar vs berhenti — two truths, no resolution

## What is Institutional Tone (and why it fails)?

Institutional tone = clean, defensible, audit-scope language. Examples:
- "Saya tidak menulis untuk meminta sesiapa dihukum" (defensive × 3)
- "Sekiranya audit menunjukkan semuanya terurus..." (diplomatic)
- "Tuan Pengerusi, saya sertakan keempat-empat audit ini..." (procedural)
- "Sabar bukan pengganti audit. Sabar bukan pengganti ketelusan." (slogany)

This reads as ROBOT. The reader (MP's assistant at 9am) discards.
