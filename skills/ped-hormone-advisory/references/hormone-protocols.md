# Hormone & TRT working notes

## TRT with fertility preservation

| Compound | Dose | Frequency | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Testosterone enanthate | 150 mg/wk | split 2× (75 mg ×2) | androgen cover; splitting flattens peaks and reduces aromatization |
| hCG | 500 IU | 2–3×/wk | impersonates LH → Leydig cells → intratesticular testosterone; keeps testis size and endogenous production |
| hMG | 75 IU | 2×/wk | FSH → spermatogenesis; **add only if semen analysis shows a problem** |

hMG without hCG is the common error: FSH matures sperm but the testis still has no intratesticular testosterone to work with. Start hCG first; most men never need hMG. hCG is cheap, hMG is not — don't burn money on both from day one.

Timeline: spermatogenesis ≈ 74 days → nothing measurable before 3 months, full effect ~6 months. Baseline semen analysis before starting, repeat at month 3 and 6.

Real-world caveat: underdosed UGL product can make a "150 mg" protocol behave like replacement-level or less, which is one reason labs look unexpectedly clean.

## Bloodwork panel

Baseline before anything, then every 3 months for the first year, then every 6.

- Total T — aim mid-range (~600–900 ng/dL on 150 mg/wk), not max
- Free T + SHBG (SHBG up ⇒ free T down: the tamoxifen trap)
- Estradiol — **sensitive assay**; standard assays are unreliable in men
- LH, FSH — expect suppressed; that is the mechanism, not a fault
- Hematocrit — the one that actually hurts. >52% act, >54% stop. Donation/phlebotomy or dose reduction.
- PSA (baseline then annual), lipids (HDL falls with dose), liver enzymes
- Semen analysis — the only test that answers fertility

Red flags to stop on: hematocrit over 54%, PSA jump, sustained blood-pressure rise, exertional breathlessness or unilateral calf pain (clot), chest pain.

## E2 management

Measure first, with a sensitive assay. In range and no symptoms → do nothing. High with symptoms (water retention, mood drop, dead libido, nipple sensitivity) → act in this order:

1. Split injections more frequently (or EOD). Lower peaks, less aromatization — this is the safest and most effective lever.
2. Only if that fails: anastrozole 0.25–0.5 mg, 1–2×/wk. Never prophylactic.

Estradiol is needed for libido, joints, mood and bone; crashing it is worse than mild elevation.

## Kisspeptin (KP-10 / KP-54)

Acts upstream at the GnRH neuron, so it can drive LH while androgen feedback sits on the pituitary. Human data (Dhillo 2005, KP-54 infusion): LH 4.2 → 10.8 U/L, FSH 3.2 → 3.9 U/L, testosterone 21.7 → 24.9 nmol/L. Note how little FSH moves — kisspeptin does **not** protect spermatogenesis.

Continuous infusion desensitizes the receptor (rat data: testicular degeneration on Alzet pump). It must be delivered as pulsatile boluses. Community doses of 50–100 mcg SC 1–2×/day are not clinical. Not a substitute for hCG when fertility matters.

## Compounds commonly asked about

- **MOTS-c** — mitochondrial-derived 16-aa peptide, AMPK → SIRT1 → PGC-1α. Community protocol 5–15 mg SC 2–3×/wk, 8–12 weeks then 4 weeks off (hormesis rationale, not clinical data). On the WADA prohibited list since 2025; not FDA-approved. Commonly cited cofactors: magnesium glycinate 200–400 mg elemental (evening), CoQ10/ubiquinol 100–200 mg, B-complex, alpha-lipoic acid 300–600 mg, NAD+ precursor, omega-3, D3+K2. The cofactor stack is plausible physiology but has **no human trial showing it improves MOTS-c outcomes** — present it as claimed, not established.
- **NAD+ precursors** — NMN 500–1000 mg or NR 300–600 mg, morning fasted, same time daily (steady level, not spikes).
- **Proviron (mesterolone)** — DHT-derived oral; binds SHBG (frees testosterone) and lowers perceived E2 activity. Produces the dry/hard look in the last 4–6 weeks of prep at 25–50 mg/day. Takes weeks to build — starting it hours before a show does nothing.

## Final-hours prep (stage day)

Last ~60 minutes before going on stage: a small fast-carb top-up 30–45 min out (40–50 g gummy bears / rice cake), a pinch of salt with 2–3 sips of water, then a light 10–12 min band/dumbbell pump (rear delts, lats, arms, calves, abs) starting ~20 min out. Do not eat a large meal, do not drink large volumes, do not graze on sugar from morning (water retention reads as blurry rather than dry), do not pump to exhaustion.
