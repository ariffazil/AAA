## The constitutional invariant (witness before act)

Most AI: `observe → act` or `observe → learn → act` (reflex / conditioning).
Institutional lineage: `observe → witness → lesson → law → system → act`.

The three extra layers are the noise-floor filter. Observation→LAW directly turns noise into law; observation→WITNESS→LAW drops noise before it becomes constitutional. This is why telemetry must NOT be coupled straight to behaviour.
