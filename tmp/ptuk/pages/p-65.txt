Currency risk sensitivity analysis
Sensitivity analysis for a given market variable provided in this note, discloses the effect on profit or
loss and equity as at 31 December 2025 assuming that a reasonably possible change in the relevant
market variable had occurred at 31 December 2025 and been applied to the risk exposures in
existence at that date to show the effects of reasonably possible changes in price on profit or loss and
equity to the next annual reporting date.
Reasonably possible changes in market variables used in the sensitivity analysis are based on implied
volatilities, where available, or historical data for equity and commodity prices and foreign exchange
rates. Reasonably possible changes in interest rates are based on management judgement and
historical experience.
