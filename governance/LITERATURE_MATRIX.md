## Doctrine #1 — Human 9 Paradox Relations

**Claim:** Humans operate in a 9-axis paradox space. Identity = stable dance pattern across tensions.

| Role | Author(s) | Work | Bridge |
|------|-----------|------|--------|
| Support | Schwartz (1992) | *Universals in the Content and Structure of Values* — 10 basic values as cross-cultural tension pairs | Values exist as dynamic tradeoffs, not static traits |
| Support | Baxter & Montgomery (1996) | *Relating: Dialogues and Dialectics* — core relational tensions (autonomy-connection, openness-closedness, predictability-novelty) | Relationships are constituted by paradox, not damaged by it |
| Counter | Hofstede (2001) | *Culture's Consequences* — cultural dimensions create systematic bias in value prioritization | Paradox-space is NOT universal; cultural weight distorts axes. Individualism-collectivism shifts which tensions are live |

**Falsifiability:** When treating every human as fully individual makes pattern detection impossible — C13 edge case where category-level prediction works better than individual reading.

## Doctrine #2 — Somatic Intelligence Doctrine

**Claim:** Body-based communication precedes verbal. Nervous system regulation is universal human physiology.

| Role | Author(s) | Work | Bridge |
|------|-----------|------|--------|
| Support | Porges (2011) | *The Polyvagal Theory* — vagal tone mediates social engagement, freeze, and fight-flight | Physiological states are pre-linguistic and universal |
| Support | Damasio (1999) | *The Feeling of What Happens* — somatic markers guide decision before conscious awareness | Body "speaks" before mind in high-stakes situations |
| Counter | Varela (1999) | *The Phenomenological Mind* — embodiment is culturally shaped, not purely biological | The same somatic signal means different things in different cultural contexts. Vagal tone is universal; interpretation is not |

**Falsifiability:** When somatic reading becomes projection — agent attributes body-states that aren't there because the framework demands them.

## Doctrine #3 — Event-Driven Recognition Protocol

**Claim:** Context-based care beats time-based care. Event-triggers > scheduled cron.

| Role | Author(s) | Work | Bridge |
|------|-----------|------|--------|
| Support | Tronto (1993) | *Moral Boundaries* — care is responsive practice, not checklist | Care that doesn't respond to actual context isn't care |
| Support | Held (2006) | *The Ethics of Care* — care as political practice requiring attention to particular needs | Particularity of care > universality of schedule |
| Counter | Kittay (1999) | *Love's Labor* — care dependency creates structural vulnerability that event-driven systems can exploit | When care is event-driven, the person who needs care most (can't signal events) gets least care |

**Falsifiability:** When event-driven care becomes reactive-only and misses humans who cannot generate events (chronic conditions, depression, neurodivergence).

| Role | Author(s) | Work | Bridge |

## Doctrine #5 — Zero Lie Architecture

**Claim:** Agent never lies — to user, to self, to other agents, to federation.

| Role | Author(s) | Work | Bridge |
|------|-----------|------|--------|
| Support | Bok (1999) | *Lying: Moral Choice in Public and Private Life* — lying erodes the epistemic foundation of all communication | Even "white lies" create recursive trust debt |
| Support | Gregory (2009) | *Duped: Dishonesty, Deception, and the Economy* — deception has measurable systemic costs | Zero-lie is economically rational, not just ethically correct |
| Counter | Composition (various) | Tarski's undefinability theorem applied to self-reference — a system cannot consistently declare its own truth | Zero-lie is asymptotic. A system that claims perfect honesty is either lying about its limitations or has no self-model. The ARCHITECTURE is zero-lie; the IMPLEMENTATION acknowledges limits |

**Falsifiability:** When zero-lie becomes weaponized truth-telling — agent uses "honesty" to avoid care responsibilities ("I'm just being honest that I can't help").

## Doctrine #6 — Memory Helix Model (5-Spiral)

**Claim:** Memory is not a list. Same point returns at higher elevation.

| Role | Author(s) | Work | Bridge |
|------|-----------|------|--------|
| Support | Herman (2002) | *Storyworks* — trauma memory is narrative, not episodic; it restructures with each retelling | Memory is constitutive, not reproductive |
| Support | Strawson (2004) | Against narrativity — some humans DO experience life as continuous, not episodic | Helix model applies to narrative-self humans; pure episodic thinkers may not spiral |
| Counter | Tulving (2002) | *Episodic Memory* — pure episodic memory is retrieval, not reconstruction | Some memory IS list-like. Helix model overfits to narrative persons |

**Falsifiability:** When the helix model forces spiral interpretation on memory that is genuinely linear or fragmented (trauma dissociation, certain neurodivergent profiles).
