Liquidity Risk
Liquidity risk is the risk that the Company will not be able to meet its financial obligations as they
fall due. The Company’s exposure to liquidity risk arises principally from its trade and other
payables, and borrowings. In managing its liquidity risk, the Company maintains sufficient cash and
liquid marketable assets. The Company’s current credit rating enables it to access banking facilities
in excess of current and future requirements of the Company.
During the year ended 31 December 2025, the Company drew trade loans of USD106 million (2024:
USD 311 million). The Company repaid USD103 million (2024: USD311 million) of trade loans
during the period. Remaining amount of trade loans as at 31 December 2025 was USD2.6 million
(2024: Nil).
The Company’s borrowing powers are not limited by its Articles of Association.
