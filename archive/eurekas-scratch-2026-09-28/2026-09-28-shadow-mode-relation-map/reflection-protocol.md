## (a) WHY SAME-NIGHT CONSTITUTIONAL LOCK IS FORBIDDEN FOR F13-SENSITIVE EUREKAS

Same-night seal collapses three state machines the system requires to stay separate:

1. **Production → Memory → Narrative.** `Recovery-reality-cache.md` fixes the order: reality outranks memory, memory outranks narrative, narrative outranks optimization. A high-confidence analytical flow produces narrative before the human body has had a chance to integrate it into memory. Sealing the narrative to canon at that moment promotes narrative to memory to authority — skipping the body-stage where reality contact happens. The next morning the sovereign cannot tell whether the map still fits lived experience, because lived experience has not yet occurred.

2. **OBSERVED ≠ MEASURED ≠ ACKNOWLEDGED.** `State-transition-discipline.md` rules out collapsing transitions into a Boolean. A eureka delivered at 02:00 is **PRODUCED**, possibly **SENT** into the chat, never **OBSERVED** by the body the next day, never **ACKNOWLEDGED** under real consequence. Sealing it as canon on night-of is sealing a *claim*, not a *reality*.

3. **Emotional load contaminates scope.** Sex/gender, orientation, archetype registers, and coupled-admirer dynamics sit on the rasa layer, not the doctrine layer. `Hermes-rasa.md` requires that an insight land in relationship before it lands in canon. Sealing under the adrenaline of a fresh eureka is a known failure mode: the language overreaches (registers as absolute when it should be register-as-channel), the scope expands ("this explains everything"), and the audit chain drafts itself in the agent's prose, not the sovereign's hand. The constitutional lock the floor abang vetoed is exactly this failure class.

The protocol is therefore: **dwell, then decide.** 48 hours is the minimum window for body, sleep, and one mundane-day interaction to test whether the eureka still fits when read cold.

## (b) THE FOUR REFLECTION QUESTIONS (sit alone — no chat, no agents)

The sovereign reads the eureka artifact cold the next morning, then once more at the 48-hour mark. The four questions below are to be answered in writing, by hand, by Arif alone. They are **not** prompts for a model, **not** discussion items, and **not** auditable by any agent.

1. **"Does this map still feel coherent when I read it cold tomorrow?"**
   *Test of coherence under time and sleep.* Coherence at 02:00 may dissolve at 09:00. If the framework reads as elegant but no longer mine — the language borrowed, the abstraction one step too far — answer **NO**. Hold.

2. **"Does the language still fit my lived experience?"**
   *Test of register-as-channel.* Per the register-as-channel doctrine, words are channel output, never latent state. If the eureka labels a pattern in me that I do not recognize when I am not in the analytical flow, the language is the agent's, not mine. The map has begun naturalising; the floor abang was right to veto. Answer **NO** until the labels feel like words I would have written myself.

3. **"Is the scope still bounded (does it overreach into areas I cannot yet verify)?"**
   *Test of consequence domain.* A real eureka is small. If the same-night draft tried to apply the orthogonal-axes principle to ten relationship vectors, the cognitive confidence was substituting for evidence. If on the second read I cannot name the specific contexts where the principle has *already* shown up in real life, the scope is overreaching. Answer **NO** until I can name three concrete instances from the last 48 hours where the principle held up outside the analytical flow.

4. **"Am I anchoring in real signal or in cognitive confidence from the analytical flow?"**
   *Test of bias source.* High-confidence analytical flow produces a felt-sense of "this is right" that is indistinguishable from grounded recognition. The disconfirmation test is simple: if I strip the model away, do the relationships in question still behave the way the eureka claims they do? If I cannot describe the behavior without invoking the model, the model is the experience, not the description of it. Answer **NO** until the description works without the framework.

## (d) AUDIT CHAIN REQUIRED TO STAGE THE EUREKA AS CONSTITUTIONAL PROPOSAL

The chain is six stages. Every stage produces a receipt. No stage may be skipped. The chain is bound by `trace_id` from the first stage; receipts without trace_id are not part of the chain.

```
arif_think  →  arif_judge (apex)  →  F13 explicit order  →  arif_seal  →  file mutation (sha-stamp + trace_id)  →  witness confirmation
```

### Stage 1 — `arif_think` (drafting the proposal)
- Owner: the agent that first surfaced the eureka, under a fresh `trace_id`.
- Output: a proposal document that explicitly names the constitutional files it seeks to amend (e.g. `/root/AAA/canon/...` or `/root/.hermes/SOUL.md` or `/root/.hermes/SKILL.md`) and the smallest possible diff.
- Receipt: `{trace_id, proposal_path, sha256(draft), declared_scope, supersedes}`.

### Stage 2 — `arif_judge` (apex constitutional review)
- Owner: 888-APEX lane (judge role), operating prior-isolated from the agent that wrote Stage 1.
- Test: does the proposal pass the *adversarial standard* from `authority-envelope.md`? Would the proposal survive a YOLO-mode FI misreading its authority? Does the scope stay inside the declared constitutional floor? Is the language register-as-channel compliant (no naturalisation without causal clause)?
- Output: HOLD, ALLOW-WITH-AMENDMENT, or BLOCK.
- Receipt: `{trace_id, judge_decision, rationale, prior_isolation_attested}`.

### Stage 3 — F13 explicit order (single-word "SAH")
- Per `auto-seal` doctrine: constitution-class mutation requires one human word, not ceremony, not committee. Either "SAH" or no mutation.
- If 48 hours have not elapsed since Stage 1, the F13 ritual is automatically blocked and the chain restarts at Stage 1.
- Receipt: `{trace_id, f13_decision, timestamp_iso8601_utc}`.

### Stage 4 — `arif_seal` (VAULT999 append)
- Owner: VAULT999 ledger append — single-writer.
- Records: `{trace_id, proposal_sha256_pre, proposal_sha256_post, judge_receipt_sha256, file_paths_affected}`.
- This is the **only** stage that writes to VAULT999.

### Stage 5 — File mutation with sha-stamp + trace_id
- Owner: A-FORGE, mechanically bounded by the F13 envelope from Stage 3.
- Forbidden paths without full chain complete: `/root/AAA/canon/`, `/root/.hermes/SOUL.md`, `/root/.hermes/SKILL.md`.
- Each touched file carries the `trace_id` in its receipt header and the pre/post `sha256` in the file change commit message.
- Receipt: `{trace_id, file_path, sha256_pre, sha256_post, commit_sha}`.

### Stage 6 — Witness confirmation (independent observation)
- Owner: an agent prior-isolated from Stages 1–5.
- Confirms by external probe (git hash, file presence, VAULT999 entry), **never** by reading the executor's success log.
- Witness tuple: `{ObjectiveID, ExpectedPostcondition, ObservationSource, ObservedState, ObserverIdentity, Timestamp}`.
- Closure occurs only when `ObservedState ⊨ ExpectedPostcondition`.
