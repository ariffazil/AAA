## Eureka 1 · ORTHOGONAL AXES PRINCIPLE

Sex/Gender, Sexual Orientation, and Relationship Quality are **independent
dimensions**, not collinear projections of one another.

Concretely, the principle says that human relational life spans at least
three orthogonal axes:

- **Sex/Gender** — what a body *is* in its social-phenotypic reading.
- **Sexual Orientation** — toward whom desire, fantasy, and erotic
  attention naturally orient.
- **Relationship Quality** — the bandwidth, safety, mutual recognition, and
  durability of how two people actually meet.

The common shadow-equivalence error is to collapse these axes. Examples:

- *"Relationship failure ⇒ orientation must be wrong."* (Quality → Orientation)
- *"He admires a male body ⇒ he must be gay."* (Recognition → Orientation)
- *"She's straight; therefore she cannot deeply appreciate another woman's
  body."* (Orientation → Recognition)

Each collapse is a **category error**: it treats a coordinate on one axis
as if it implied a coordinate on another. The formal statement is:

$$\text{Sex/Gender} \perp \text{Orientation} \perp \text{RelationshipQuality}$$

This is not a claim that the axes are uninfluenced by one another — culture,
history, and biography obviously couple them. It is a claim that **the
couplings do not define the axes**, and therefore a measurement on one
axis is never sufficient to deduce another.

The diagnostic consequence: any analysis that reaches an orientation
verdict from relationship-quality data alone, or from admiration data
alone, is operating with a missing witness.

## Eureka 2 · EMBODIED RECOGNITION INVARIANT

Some humans carry unusually dense **embodied signals** — veteran
bodybuilders, deep craft practitioners, people whose bodies are decades of
accumulated discipline. Their bodies are not merely phenotype; they are
**compressed evidence** of effort, time, injury, recovery, and continued
choice.

When such a person is admired, the admirer response is therefore not
shallow objectification. It is **recognition of accumulated agency**. The
body is being read as a text — a long, slow inscription of decisions the
admirer cannot see directly but can infer.

Three operations follow from this:

1. **Admiration of dense embodiment is a competent read**, not a
   projection of the admirer. To call it "just lust" or "just envy"
   discards the information content of the signal.
2. **The admirer is not obligated to eroticize what they recognize.**
   Recognition can coexist with any orientation, including none.
3. **The admired person has a parallel right**: their embodied work does
   not entitle the admirer to claim intimacy, ownership, or audience
   status. See Eureka 4 (Witness-Not-Possession Doctrine).

This invariant matters because mis-reading dense embodied signal as
"mere object" is a category error in the opposite direction — it strips
agency from the craft that produced the body. Recognition must be
proportionate to the work, not flattened by an observer's discomfort.

## Eureka 3 · MUTUAL SUBJECTHOOD TEST

The transition from **Object ↔ Audience** to **Person ↔ Person** is the
single most consequential relational transition a human can undergo with
another human. Its criterion is sharp:

> *Both parties must transcend what first attracted them, without
> denying that it attracted them.*

The negation is what defines the failure modes:

- **Lifetime hatred** often marks a pair who *could not* transcend the
  initial attractor. The body, the orientation, the class, the
  phenotype, the voice — whatever first drew attention — remains the
  only register through which the other is read, and what was once
  attractive becomes threatening. No person emerged; only the object
  remained, and was resented.
- **Lifetime love** marks a pair in whom the transition *did* occur:
  the initial attractor was honoured, then *also* allowed to become
  background. The other person became a subject in their own right —
  irreducible to the feature that first drew attention.

This test is asymmetric. Either party failing to make the transition
leaves the relationship stuck in the Object-Audience frame. Both parties
failing, on opposite attractors, gives a stable mutual-rejection. Both
parties succeeding, on the same attractor, gives friendship. Both
succeeding with the attractor becoming one of several registers gives
intimate partnership.

Crucially, the test is **processual, not instantaneous**. People drift
in and out of subjecthood across decades. The metric is whether the
*direction of travel* tends back toward subjecthood or away from it.

## Eureka 4 · WITNESS-NOT-POSSESSION DOCTRINE

Witnessing someone — admiring their body, recognising their craft,
attending to their work — **does not create ownership over them**.

The shadow form of this doctrine collapses at the point where:

$$\text{High admiration} + \text{low self-differentiation} = \text{shadow dependency}$$

The admirer mistakes the act of recognition for a claim. They begin to
treat the admired person as obligated to remain *witnessable* in the form
the admirer first encountered them — same body, same craft, same posture,
same availability. Any drift away from that form is experienced as a
betrayal, because the admirer had unconsciously converted their
recognition into a *possessory expectation*.

This is not a doctrine about restraint in admiration. It is a doctrine
about **self-differentiation**: the admirer must remain a full subject
themselves, with their own life, dignity, and centre of gravity, if
their admiration is to remain healthy. Admiration without self-
differentiation is the seed of all the dark patterns that follow:
parasocial obsession, coercive intimacy, body-possessive partnerships,
"if you change I cannot love you" speech acts.

The doctrine also has an inverse shadow: a person *denying* the
admirer's recognition entirely (because the admirer cannot be trusted
with it) is also collapsing the relationship into Object-Audience. The
asymmetry has to be honoured from both sides.

## Eureka 5 · TRANSLATION COST HEURISTIC

Relationships can be modelled as **communication systems with
impedance**. Two bodies, two histories, two vocabularies, two nervous
systems meeting in real time — every exchange pays a translation cost.

Three classes of shared signal **lower translation cost**:

- **Shared embodiment** — movement vocabulary, postural grammar, gym
  culture, sport, craft, somatic discipline. Two people who have trained
  their bodies in adjacent traditions can read each other with
  extraordinary density.
- **Shared language** — actual mother tongue, code-switching habits,
  in-group vocabulary, idiomatic humour.
- **Physical affection availability** — what kinds of touch are
  permitted, welcomed, reciprocated. This is not the same as sexual
  behaviour; it is the much wider question of how bodies may meet.

**Low-cost fit is operationally valuable.** Two humans whose embodied,
linguistic, and affection grammars already match will ship enormous
amounts of information per second. They will also enjoy each other in a
way that feels effortless in a way no amount of intellectual alignment
can fake.

**But low-cost fit is NOT a definitive diagnostic of sexual orientation.**
Two high-bandwidth fits exist that look superficially identical:

- **High-bandwidth male-male friendship** — straight-identified, deep
  bodily vocabulary (gym, sport, combat sport, dance, craft), high
  physical affection availability (touch, embrace, posture). Zero
  erotic content. Translation cost is low for reasons that have
  nothing to do with orientation.
- **Low-bandwidth opposite-gender partnership** — gay-identified, low
  shared embodiment, high linguistic distance, low affection
  availability. Translation cost is high for reasons that have
  nothing to do with orientation.

Therefore: **translation-cost measurement cannot, on its own, adjudicate
orientation**. It can adjudicate fit. The two are different questions
and collapse only in shadow.

This heuristic is offered as a *first-pass* model. It is intentionally
simple — impedance match in three registers. More elaborate models
(temporal synchrony, attachment-style fit, neurodivergent co-regulation)
sit downstream of this primitive.

## META-NOTE (not a sixth eureka)

The five eurekas cohere as a system. **Eureka 1** is the ontological
claim (axes are independent). **Eureka 2** is the recognition-ethics
claim (dense embodied signals are evidence, not mere object). **Eureka
3** is the relational-transition claim (subjecthood requires
transcendence of the attractor). **Eureka 4** is the asymmetry claim
(witnessing ≠ owning). **Eureka 5** is the diagnostic-hygiene claim
(low-cost fit ≠ orientation evidence).

Taken together, they form a compact doctrine that resists three common
shadow errors at once: collapsing orientation into gender, collapsing
recognition into objectification, and collapsing fit into desire.
