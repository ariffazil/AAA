Astronomers confirmed Elias 2-24 b, a gas giant in the Ophiuchus star-forming region about 450 light-years out, as the youngest known planet - under a million years old, still whirling inside its natal disk of dust and gas. A 'born-again' star just changed evolutionary stage while astronomers watched NEW

Sakurai's Object, one of the fastest-changing stars ever observed, has been confirmed to have entered a new stage of its evolution - a rare chance to watch stellar evolution happen on a human timescale rather than a geological one. The star is executing a 'born-again' helium-shell flash: it had settled into a white dwarf, then reignited and swelled back into a giant within years. Textbooks describe this transition in millions of years; here it is dated in observing seasons.
Source: phys.org 17 Sep 2026, 'Born-again star offers rare chance to watch stellar evolution in real time'; innovationnewsnetwork.com same week naming Sakurai's Object. W=2.

Protein in 20 Homo naledi teeth came back all-female - a possible sex-specific burial ritual NEW

Analysis of proteins preserved in teeth from 20 different Homo naledi individuals from the Rising Star cave system found all 20 were female. The authors raise the possibility that this small-brained hominin practised sex-specific burial - deliberate body disposal in deep, hard-to-reach chambers, which would push the origins of funerary behaviour deeper into our family tree than conventionally accepted. The finding is published in the September/October Archaeology Magazine world roundup and is checkable there.
Source: Archaeology Magazine September/October 2026 issue, World section (archaeology.org). W=1 origin - single-magazine summary of the underlying study; flagged as such. Protein-based sex identification is the stated method.
