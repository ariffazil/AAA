Eureka

A 50-year-old blood mystery was solved and there is now a 47th human blood group NEW

Scientists have identified the gene behind the AnWj antigen, a puzzle open since 1972, and the answer is the MAL gene. That makes MAL an official human blood group system, ISBT 047. A single protein is enough to produce the antigen, which is what closed the case. It matters because a genetic test can now find the rare patients and the rare donors before a transfusion goes wrong.
Source: ScienceDaily, 19 September 2026 (NHS Blood and Transplant-led work); Sci.News, New Blood Group System Discovered: MAL. Two write-ups of one primary paper - W=1 for the finding.

Plague did not leave Europe when the Black Death did - it stayed for centuries NEW

Ancient DNA shows Yersinia pestis returning to Europe in repeated waves for 350 to 400 years after the Black Death, evolving into fresh lineages and travelling along the same routes as trade, migration and war. The pandemic ended. The pathogen did not go anywhere. A useful corrective to how we talk about things being over.
Source: ScienceDaily, 20 September 2026 (University of Tartu and partners); Archaeology News Online Magazine, September 2026 (more than 350 years). Primary study, two write-ups - W=1.

Perfect uniformity makes a network fragile; balanced disorder makes it stable NEW

Physicists at Northwestern found that carefully balanced variation - disorder - can make networks more stable, not less. Power grids, ecosystems, neurons and materials all improved when their parts were not perfectly alike. It runs against the instinct that order is always the safer bet.
Source: ScienceDaily, 19 September 2026 (Northwestern University). Single origin unit, W=1, single primary study.

Nerve fibres may not be smooth tubes. Textbooks may have been wrong for a century NEW

A century of diagrams show axons as smooth tubes. New imaging suggests they naturally resemble strings of tiny pearls. If it holds, a lot of drawn neurobiology is a simplification rather than a picture.
