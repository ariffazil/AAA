Eureka

The first stellar stream found outside our galaxy - pulled out of old Hubble data NEW

Astronomers identified the first globular-cluster stellar stream beyond the Milky Way: a faint ribbon of stars trailing from an ancient cluster around the ultra-diffuse galaxy UGC 9050-Dw1, about 115 million light-years away. Stellar streams trace a galaxy's gravity, so this one gave the team a way to weigh dark matter in a galaxy far outside our own. The find came out of the Hubble archive - the data already existed.
Source: Nature 2026 (s41586-026-10878-w); ScienceDaily 21-23 Sep 2026; EurekAlert release. W=2 (paper plus release). Checkable against the Nature paper.

Webb found water surviving beside the Milky Way's black hole NEW

Using JWST, astronomers detected oxygen-rich dust and water in the extreme environment around Sagittarius A*, the supermassive black hole at the centre of our galaxy - the first water detection there. Molecular material is surviving radiation intense enough to be expected to destroy it, which means stars close to the black hole can keep feeding material back into their surroundings.
Source: ESA/Webb release weic2617; Space.com 23 Sep 2026; Earth.com 13 Sep 2026. W=2 (ESA primary plus broadcaster).

Titanosaurs nested far south of the equator, eggshells show NEW

Fossil eggshells place titanosaur nesting grounds far south of the equator, widening the known breeding range of the long-necked giants. Nesting sites are how you reconstruct where these animals actually lived and moved, rather than where their bones happened to fossilise.
Source: Sci.News 22 Sep 2026 (Paleontology), reporting the eggshell study. W=1 flagged; checkable against the underlying paper.
