The exact list of Sarawak laws PETRONAS is asking the Federal Court to strike down NEW

PETRONAS's petition BKA-1-01-2026(W) is not a general challenge. It names Sarawak's Environment (Reduction of Greenhouse Gases Emission) Ordinance 2023, the Sarawak Land (Carbon Storage) Rules 2022, sections 7, 7A and 8 of the Distribution of Gas Ordinance 2016, the Oil Mining Ordinance 1958 and the 1954 boundary order that pulls the continental shelf into the definition of State land. Leave was granted on 16 March 2026 and the judgment was recorded on 30 April 2026. Carbon storage regulation in Sarawak sits inside the scope, which is the part that touches the CCS chain directly.
Source: [2026] 4 MLRA 491, Federal Court, Hashim Hamzah CJM, Motion No BKA-1-01-2026(W) - https://www.elaw.my/JE/01/JE_2026_20.html

Brent at about USD 104 - up 14 per cent in a month and 56 per cent in a year NEW

Brent settled at USD 104.16 on 18 September, a third consecutive daily fall, after Saudi Arabia started routing crude around the damaged Yanbu terminal and said it could restore half the East-West pipeline's capacity within days and full capacity within six weeks. This is a disruption premium rather than a demand move: Urals is at USD 121.61, gasoline is up 76 per cent year on year, and the near-month futures backwardation is doing the work. The falsification test is whether the bypass holds - shipping is already shifting through ship-to-ship transfers near Oman's Sohar port, which rose to 2.7 million barrels a day from 1.5 million in August.
Source: TradingEconomics Brent, 18 September 2026 (104.16 USD/bbl, +13.69% month, +56.21% year). Anti-thesis search run: 'Iran ceasefire September 2026 oil prices fall Hormuz reopened' - returns the April and June ceasefire rallies collapsing prices, i.e. the premium has unwound twice this year already, so 'oil is structurally high' is NOT supported.

The Fed raised rates on 16 September - first hike since 2023 NEW

The target range moved to 3.75-4.00 per cent on a 12-0 vote, with the committee naming oil-driven inflation in the statement itself and the Chair calling inflation too high for too long. Projections show another hike possible this year, none pencilled in for 2027, headline PCE at 3.7 per cent and unemployment at 4.1 per cent. The dollar is at 100.2 on the index while the ringgit sits at 4.098, so the pressure lands on the same side of the ledger as the oil price.
Source: CNBC, 16 September 2026 - https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/16/fed-rate-decision-september-2026.html ; FOMC statement same date

The haze now has an operating threshold, and it has been used once this year NEW

A district in Sarawak was placed under a state of emergency on 4 September when its air reading passed 500 - the first such declaration of 2026 - and the emergency was lifted three days later. On 15 September, 38 areas were still reading unhealthy, with Johor carrying the most; Serian peaked at 188 and the Klang Valley ran above 150 from Cheras to Shah Alam to Putrajaya. The consequence is a work-planning fact before it is a health fact: the threshold in the standing instructions defines when field activity and school activity actually stop, and it has now been tested once this year.

The most sensitive dark matter detector in the world has one unexplained event NEW

LUX-ZEPLIN, ten tonnes of ultrapure liquid xenon a mile underground in South Dakota, found a single interaction in 220 live days of data that its own team cannot explain with known backgrounds. Significance is 2.6 sigma against the 5 sigma needed to claim a discovery, and the collaboration states plainly that it is not claiming dark matter. If it is real, the particle would be at least 200 times the mass of a proton.
Source: Berkeley Lab, 1 September 2026 - https://newscenter.lbl.gov/2026/09/01/lz-sees-surprising-result-in-search-for-dark-matter/

Diplodocus has been found outside North America for the first time NEW

Every confirmed Diplodocus since 1878 has come out of one formation in the western United States. Fourteen tail vertebrae and several chevron bones unearthed at La Tejeria near El Castellar in Teruel, Spain, belong to the genus - a 25-metre animal that lived about 150 million years ago, described in the Journal of Vertebrate Paleontology. It is the strongest evidence yet that dinosaurs moved between the two continents across temporary land bridges when the proto-Atlantic shallowed.
Source: EL PAIS, 15 September 2026 - https://english.elpais.com/science-tech/2026-09-15/first-diplodocus-found-outside-north-america-discovered-in-spain.html

BepiColombo reached Mercury after eight years and cut its engine loose NEW

The Mercury Transfer Module separated from the two science orbiters on 3 September 2026, ending an eight-year cruise that used flybys of Earth, Venus and Mercury itself because carrying enough fuel for a direct route was never possible. Two orbiters - one European, one Japanese - will now be inserted into different orbits to measure the planet from the inside out. Nothing built by humans has flown this close to the Sun for this long.
Source: European Space Agency mission updates - https://www.esa.int/Science_Exploration/Space_Science/BepiColombo/Latest_updates_BepiColombo_s_arrival_at_Mercury
