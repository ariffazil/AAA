What is in it

  1. The void this fills. WEALTH could do capital arithmetic but had no political economy, no distribution of consequence, no horizon past the current decision. Knowledge-axis coverage L0–L10 with the two remaining gaps named.
  2. POLIX — power topology. Six actors with mandate, opacity and capture risk; four rent flows with beneficiary and cost-bearer; three rule asymmetries. Capture score MODERATE — highest at state governments, lowest at Bank Negara. The concentration that matters most sits at the centre, not in a regulator. Second seed case covers PETRONAS / Khazanah / PNB.
  3. CIVX — civilizational horizon 2027 to 2040. Five assumption axes, three explicit paths (best / base / worst), six resilience axes per path, irreversibilities named per path. Every figure tagged INTERPRETED, status SAFE_TO_STUDY.
  4. Limits and calibration debt — what would have to be measured before any of this becomes decision-grade.
