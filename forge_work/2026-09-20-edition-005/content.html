Yanbu pipeline: Aramco targets half capacity in days, full repair in four to six weeks MOVED

Aramco is now moving to restore about half of the East-West pipeline's operational capacity within days, with full repairs estimated at four to six weeks per Kpler. The 10 September drone strike disabled multiple pumping stations along the 1,200 km line from Abqaiq to Yanbu; the pipe itself is largely intact. With Yanbu stock covering only three to five days of loadings and no crude having loaded from Yanbu since the attack, the partial restart is the constraint on European diesel supply for September and the SUMED workaround via Ain Sukhna continues to carry the bulk of what flows into the Mediterranean. If half capacity is restored next week, the Brent premium for September cargoes should ease; if repairs slip past 22 October, the diesel-tight Europe story runs into Q4.
Source: Kpler, 17 September 2026; Arabic Trader, 18 September 2026; HNGN, 17 September 2026.

Eureka

NASA's Roman Space Telescope has enough fuel for 22 years of science, double the design life NEW

Post-launch analysis of NASA's Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope shows it carries enough propellant for at least 22 years of science operations, more than double its original 10-year design life. The 5,300-pound observatory launched on a SpaceX Falcon Heavy and is now heading toward its operational orbit around the Sun-Earth Lagrange point 2, about a million miles from Earth. Project manager Jackie Townsend called the ride magnificent. The longer life directly increases the catalog of exoplanets and dark-energy measurements the telescope can return before the propellant runs out.
Source: ScienceDaily, 18 September 2026; BBC coverage of the launch; ESA/NASA mission page. W=2.

Cold muonium atoms produced at PSI - the gravity test that pits particle physics against Einstein is now possible NEW

Researchers at the Paul Scherrer Institute in Switzerland have, for the first time, produced muonium atoms in a 'cold' state - a hydrogen-like atom made of an antimuon orbited by an electron. Cold muonium is the prerequisite for an experiment that drops muonium under gravity and asks whether antimatter falls the same way as matter. The standard model says yes; general relativity says yes; no measurement yet says yes. The PSI result is the engineering gate that opens the actual measurement, not the answer itself.
Source: Phys.org, September 2026.

Hubble has caught a 10-sided atmospheric wave circling Saturn's south pole NEW

Hubble observations in early September 2026 revealed a giant, evolving ten-sided atmospheric wave encircling Saturn's south pole. It is the first time a large, regular-sided jet pattern has been documented in Saturn's southern hemisphere; a similar six-sided hexagon has been known at the north pole since Voyager. The mechanism that locks the wave shape and number of sides is the same kind of question that ties to jet-stream modelling on Earth - the shape is a visible consequence of physics that is normally hidden in the lower atmosphere.
Source: ESA/Hubble press release 2 September 2026.
