An amoeba that multiplies at 145F pushes the ceiling for complex life NEW

Scientists have named a 'fire amoeba' - a eukaryote, the same domain of life as everything you can see - that reproduces at 145F and keeps moving up to 147F, the highest recorded for complex life. Bacteria manage hotter; cells with nuclei were thought to top out lower. It matters because the temperature ceiling for complex cells is one of the filters in every 'could life exist there' argument, including subsurface and geothermal settings.
Source: NPR 22 Sep 2026 science report naming the species and the two temperature records. W=1 origin, flagged. Checkable against the underlying study.
