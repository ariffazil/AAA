Brent fell three sessions running to USD 103.21 while Aramco cancelled European cargoes MOVED

Brent settled at USD 103.21 on 18 September, a third consecutive daily fall, down 1.2 per cent on the week but still up 12.7 per cent on the month and 54.8 per cent on the year. The fall prices the Hormuz reroute, not a repair: satellite tracking shows 2.8 million barrels a day moving back through Hormuz over six days against 700,000 a day in August. The physical side reads the other way - Aramco has cancelled or delayed European crude cargoes and Yanbu stock covers three to five days of loadings. Correction to Edition 003, which printed 104.16 against 18 September: the same source now reads 103.21 for 18 September and 104.82 for 17 September, so the settled figure stands.
Source: Trading Economics Brent page, 18 September 2026 (actual 103.21, previous 104.82). Anti-thesis search run: 'Yanbu pipeline repair weeks diesel shortage Europe refinery runs cut September 2026' - it returns Aramco cancelling European cargoes and a trade reading of no crude for Europe until November, i.e. the cheap-side thesis is NOT clean. Kpler, 17 September 2026; OilPrice.com on the cargo cancellations.

The attack took the pumping stations, not the pipe NEW

The 10 September drone strike disabled multiple pumping stations along the 1,200 km East-West pipeline that carries Saudi crude from Abqaiq to Yanbu and around Hormuz. The pipe is largely intact; the stations are the constraint. West-coast refineries are cutting throughput onto inventory, diesel into Europe is the tight product, and tanker freight has risen across the route. The question is whether Aramco restores half of the flows as it says it can, or whether the stations take longer than the pipe ever would.
Source: Kpler market note, 17 September 2026 - https://www.kpler.com/blog/yanbu-pipeline-attack-ripples-across-crude-freight-and-gas-markets ; Euronews, 17 September 2026

The Houthis took Mocha, and now sit beside Bab el-Mandeb NEW

Houthi forces seized the Red Sea port city of Mocha on 10 September in their most serious push along Yemen's coast in years, putting them roughly 80 km from Bab el-Mandeb. Both chokepoints on the Gulf-to-Europe route - Hormuz and Bab el-Mandeb - are now contested at the same time, which is why the answer to the first one is not simply a cheaper route. For anything priced off delivered barrels rather than wellhead barrels, this is the part of the story that never appears in the Brent number.
Source: Guardian, Al Jazeera, NPR, The National, 10-11 September 2026

The shape that tiles forever without repeating also twists light NEW

The Spectre - the chiral aperiodic monotile found in 2023, a single shape that covers a plane without ever repeating and without needing its mirror image - turns out to scatter laser light into pinwheel diffraction patterns that expose its chirality directly. Reported 14-15 September. For a geologist this is the quasicrystal family again: order without periodicity, the same structural idea that took the 2011 chemistry Nobel and that was later found to occur naturally in a meteorite from the Khatyrka river in Russia.
Source: ScienceDaily, 14-15 September 2026 - https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/09/260914102432.htm

A gut-bacteria by-product of pomegranates and walnuts helps repair the gut lining NEW

A compound produced when gut bacteria digest foods such as pomegranates, walnuts and berries appears to help repair and protect the intestinal lining, reported 17 September. The surprise is which side of the pathway it comes from: the effect rides a mechanism usually studied for inflammation rather than repair. It is early laboratory work and not a prescription, but the lever is dietary rather than pharmaceutical.
Source: ScienceDaily, 17 September 2026 - https://www.sciencedaily.com/news/health_medicine/

A new frog species was hiding in plain sight in the western Amazon NEW

Scientists working in the flooded forests of the western Amazon described a new frog species on 17 September, separated from near-identical relatives by DNA and by a call that is audibly different even where the animals look the same. It is the old field-biology lesson in a new place: in a flooded forest, sound separates species faster than eyesight does, which is why acoustic surveys keep out-performing visual ones.
Source: ScienceDaily, 17 September 2026 - https://www.sciencedaily.com/news/fossils_ruins/

The most sensitive dark matter detector in the world has one unexplained event NEW

LUX-ZEPLIN, ten tonnes of ultrapure liquid xenon a mile underground in South Dakota, found a single interaction in 220 live days of data that its own team cannot explain with known backgrounds. Significance is 2.6 sigma against the 5 sigma needed to claim a discovery, and the collaboration states plainly that it is not claiming dark matter. If it is real, the particle would be at least 200 times the mass of a proton.
Source: Berkeley Lab, 1 September 2026 - https://newscenter.lbl.gov/2026/09/01/lz-sees-surprising-result-in-search-for-dark-matter/

Diplodocus has been found outside North America for the first time NEW

Every confirmed Diplodocus since 1878 has come out of one formation in the western United States. Fourteen tail vertebrae and several chevron bones unearthed at La Tejeria near El Castellar in Teruel, Spain, belong to the genus - a 25-metre animal that lived about 150 million years ago, described in the Journal of Vertebrate Paleontology. It is the strongest evidence yet that dinosaurs moved between the two continents across temporary land bridges when the proto-Atlantic shallowed.
Source: EL PAIS, 15 September 2026 - https://english.elpais.com/science-tech/2026-09-15/first-diplodocus-found-outside-north-america-discovered-in-spain.html

BepiColombo reached Mercury after eight years and cut its engine loose NEW

The Mercury Transfer Module separated from the two science orbiters on 3 September 2026, ending an eight-year cruise that used flybys of Earth, Venus and Mercury itself because carrying enough fuel for a direct route was never possible. Two orbiters - one European, one Japanese - will now be inserted into different orbits to measure the planet from the inside out. Nothing built by humans has flown this close to the Sun for this long.
Source: European Space Agency mission updates - https://www.esa.int/Science_Exploration/Space_Science/BepiColombo/Latest_updates_BepiColombo_s_arrival_at_Mercury
