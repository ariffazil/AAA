## 3. Truth dimensions — orthogonal (binding schema)

Every Claim/Evidence/Question must carry **four orthogonal fields**, not one. They measure different things and never merge.

### 3.1 Field definitions

| Field | Measure | Cardinality | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| `authority_grade` | Source authority (E0–E5) | single | `E2_COMPANIES_HOUSE_FILING` |
| `classification` | Epistemic status | single | `VERIFIED` / `REPORTED` / `INFERRED` / `HYPOTHESIS` / `UNKNOWN` / `CONTRADICTED` |
| `confidence` | Confidence in the atomic proposition | numeric 0.000–1.000, three decimals, banded | `0.970` |
| `verification_scope` | Exactly what has been verified, in plain English | short string | `filing_exists; underlying_statement_truth_not_verified` |

### 3.2 `authority_grade` (E0–E5)

1. **E5** — court judgment · regulator finding · independent audit opinion.
2. **E4** — authenticated internal record, lawfully held, provenance-preserved.
3. **E3** — at least two *genuinely independent* E2/E1 sources converging.
4. **E2** — Companies House / SKK Migas / MPM / Bursa / SEC equivalent / audited financial statement / official signed statement.
5. **E1** — single-source public assertion (press release, news article, single LinkedIn post).
6. **E0** — rumour, hearsay, recollection without contemporaneous record.

### 3.3 `classification` (epistemic status)

- **VERIFIED** — proposition matches the registered/signed authoritative artifact within scope.
- **REPORTED** — single authoritative party or press has asserted the proposition; no independent corroboration.
- **INFERRED** — reasoned reading that follows from established facts but is not itself documented.
- **HYPOTHESIS** — falsifiable conjecture awaiting distinguishing evidence.
- **UNKNOWN** — material information unavailable or unresolved; default for any un-evidenced node.
- **CONTRADICTED** — competing authoritative sources disagree; both retained, winner pending.

### 3.4 `confidence` band table (deterministic)

| Conditions | Confidence |
|---|---:|
| Direct regulator / court finding, identity and date matched | `0.990` |
| Direct registry filing, proposition limited strictly to filing content | `0.970` |
| Official signed corporate statement about its own action | `0.930` |
| Two *genuinely independent* authoritative sources (distinct originating records) | `0.900` |
| One official corporate assertion without underlying instrument | `0.850` |
| Secondary reporting corroborated by an official statement | `0.800` |
| Below the typed-fact threshold | **Encode as `Question`, not `Claim`** |

**Override rule.** Confidence **must fall** if any of the following is unresolved:

- Entity identity (homonyms, shell-vs-operating, predecessor-vs-successor).
- Effective date (when the fact became true vs when it was filed vs when it was announced).
- Terminology match (`discovered reserves` vs `discovered resources` vs `prospective resources`).
- Source independence (single lineage dressed as two URLs).

### 3.5 `verification_scope` (mandatory short string)

Examples:

- `filing_exists_only; underlying_commercial_fact_not_independently_verified`
- `director_name_matches_filing; direct_relationship_not_confirmed`
- `address_current_as_of_<date>; address_history_superseded_by_<filing_id>`

### 3.6 Corroboration rule (corrected)

- `authority_grade` = source authority. Stays at E0–E5.
- `corroboration_level` = `C1` (single source) / `C2` (one corroborating source) / `C3` (≥ 2 independent corroborating sources).
- **Corroboration raises confidence but cannot automatically supersede a higher-authority source.** A regulator filing at E2 outranks two C3 press reports, even if the press is louder.
- **Independence requires distinct originating records, not distinct URLs.** Two press reports repeating the same press release count as one evidence lineage, not two.

### 3.7 Special overrides

- A later Companies House filing **supersedes** an earlier one (e.g. *current* registered office). Attach `superseded_by` edge; do not silently edit.
- A regulator notice supersedes press reporting.
- An audited account supersedes a newspaper figure.
- Public articles and a separate shareholders agreement **may both apply**; treat them as partial governance evidence, not a complete deadlock system.
