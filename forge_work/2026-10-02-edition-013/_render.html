Two meteor showers this month - one from Halley's own dust NEW

The Draconids peak 8-9 October, usually a quiet handful per hour, shed by comet 21P/Giacobini-Zinner. The Orionids peak 21-22 October, up to 20 an hour, from dust trail of comet 1P/Halley - the same comet that visits every 76 years. You are sleeping under its litter. Both peak nights have a waxing-to-new moon window, which favours Kedah skies.
Source: timeanddate.com October 2026 sky guide; Natural History Museum and EarthSky shower calendars
