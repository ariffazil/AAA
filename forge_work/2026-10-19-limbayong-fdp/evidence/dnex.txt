1.      INTRODUCTION

        The Board of Directors of DNeX (“the Board”) wishes to announce that Ping Petroleum
        UK PLC (“PPUK” or “Seller”), a wholly-owned subsidiary of Ping Petroleum Ltd, which in
        turn is a 90% owned subsidiary of DNeX, has entered into a Memorandum of Agreement
        (“MOA”) with MISC Berhad (“MISC” or “Buyer”) on 20 August 2026, for the Proposed
        Disposal of Excalibur Floating Production Storage and Offloading (“FPSO Excalibur” or
        “Excalibur”) for a total disposal consideration of USD35,000,000 based on the terms and
        conditions as set out in the MOA.

        Further details on the Proposed Disposal are set out in the ensuing sections.



2.      INFORMATION ON THE BUYER
        MISC is a public company listed on the Main Market of Bursa Malaysia Securities Berhad.
        Founded in 1968, MISC is an energy-related maritime group with more than five decades
        of industry experience. The group’s businesses cover energy shipping and related
        activities, offshore floating solutions, marine repair and conversion, integrated marine
        services, port and terminal management, as well as maritime education and training.

        disposal consideration of USD35,000,000 (“Disposal Consideration”) to be satisfied

3.2   Basis and justification of arriving at the Disposal Consideration

      The Disposal Consideration was arrived at on a “willing-buyer willing-seller” basis, taking
      into consideration the following:

      (i)      based on the NBV of Excalibur of which the Disposal Consideration represents a
               pro forma gain on disposal of USD5.86 million or RM23.81 million after taking into
               account capital gains tax payable as well as the tax authorities’ recovery of capital
               allowances that was previously claimed by the Company in prior years; and
      (ii)     the fair value of the Excalibur as ascertained by ABS Consulting ranges between
               USD22.5 million to USD43.5 million, based on replacement cost appraisal method
               and market comparable appraisal method. The Disposal Consideration is within
               the estimated range of the fair value as appraised by an independent valuer.



3.3   Mode of Settlement for the Disposal Consideration
      The Disposal Consideration will be satisfied entirely in cash as follows:-

      (a)    a booking fee of USD2.5 million was paid upon signing of exclusivity letter with
             PPUK in April 2026;
      (b)    a deposit of USD3.5million will be payable within 3 days from signing of the MOA;
             and
      (c)    the balance Disposal Consideration of USD29 million will be payable within 3 days
             from delivery of Excalibur.

3.4   Utilisation of proceeds

      The entire disposal proceeds of USD35.0 million less settlement of payment of tax
      obligations associated with the disposal will be used for working capital purposes of the
      DNeX Group of Companies (“Group”). The disposal is expected to generate a net cash
      proceeds of USD14.11 million upon completion of transaction.


4.    RATIONALE FOR THE PROPOSED DISPOSAL

      The Proposed Disposal of the FPSO Excalibur represents a prudent and proactive
      strategic decision by the Group, reflecting the evolving fiscal, regulatory and development
      landscape in the UK offshore energy sector. The UK’s policy direction increasingly
      encourages new developments to be tied back to existing, larger infrastructure, which has
      reduced the commercial attractiveness of the vessel's originally intended deployment.

      This divestment enables the Group to optimise capital allocation and preserve value by
      avoiding the costs and potential value erosion associated with an extended warm-stacked
      period.


5.    RISKS FACTORS

5.1   Non-completion or delay of the Proposed Disposal

      The completion of the Proposed Disposal is subject to compliance with, among others, the
      warranties and covenants under the terms of the MOA. The Seller will endeavour to take
      necessary steps to ensure that the warranties and covenants by the Seller are fulfilled in
      a timely manner to facilitate the completion of the Proposed Disposal.


5.2   Foreign currency risk

      The Disposal Consideration will be settled in USD, and therefore the Disposal
      Consideration if and when converted into RM may be impacted by the fluctuation of USD
      against RM, which may be addressed through the Group’s strategies for managing foreign
      currency fluctuation.
