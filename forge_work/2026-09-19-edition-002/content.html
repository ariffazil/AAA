Nomination voiding silently on a change of marital status OPEN

A nomination made before a marriage is not automatically the nomination that applies after it. The failure is silent and is discovered by survivors. The fund itself has publicly corrected circulating messages on this, which is the reason to take it from the fund rather than from a forwarded message.
Source: EPF public statements correcting viral nomination claims

Eureka

AI-generated intimate images fall inside the criminal definition NEW

The 2026 cybercrime amendments bring synthetically generated intimate images within the offence rather than leaving them outside it. Worth knowing before it is needed, because the person who needs it is usually already under attack when they find out.
Source: Cybercrimes Bill 2026, clause 24

Electricity protection threshold reverts 800 to 600 kWh on 1 January 2027 OPEN

105 days from this edition. The extended band was a temporary widening; the underlying automatic fuel adjustment mechanism continues underneath it and its December reading is projected at the highest of the year. The reversion and the adjustment land in the same window, which is where a household bill can move twice.
