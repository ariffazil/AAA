## 2. The Admission Test (NEW LAW — operational corollary of the Governance Gap Theorem)

> **Tambah capability hanya jika ia mengurangkan beban governance.**
> **Add capability only if it reduces governance burden.**

NOT: add capability because the capability is interesting.

Binding: every capability admission (new skill, new organ, new agent, new gate) must answer: *does the sovereign's net governance burden FALL?* A gate that adds checking without removing confusion is bureaucracy formation (cf. lifecycle Binding Rule 5).
