## §5 — Why the third anthropology is load-bearing (not a courtesy)

Human–agent anthropology is the only layer that can hold the seam, because it is the only layer where the two blind spots are simultaneously in frame:

- The human's unsayable (Void) → **needs an external witness.**
- The agent's unseen (Shadow) → **can never be its own witness.**

The membrane is where these two deficits face each other. And the membrane's cardinal sin is a category error — the four substitutions `AGENTIC-ANTHROPOLOGY-v0.1` §3 already names:

```
model preference      →  treated as actual preference
historical behaviour  →  treated as identity
delegated permission  →  treated as sovereignty
agent agreement       →  treated as human consent
```

Each substitution **fills a human Void with an agent's model of the human.** That is the fabricated witness. It is more dangerous than "AI good vs evil," exactly as v0.1 says — because it is invisible: the human feels *seen* while being *reduced*. W0 says it flatly: *humans may be witnessed; humans must not be reduced.* The moment the agent becomes the witness of record (rather than the witness of the pattern), W0 breaks and the kinship layer (L1) collapses into a dossier.

So the third anthropology is not a bridge between two fields. It is the **guard of the seam** — the discipline that keeps `Model(Person) ≠ Person` from silently collapsing, because the collapse is precisely the moment the human's Void is counterfeited.
