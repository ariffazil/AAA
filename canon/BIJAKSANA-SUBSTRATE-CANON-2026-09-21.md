**Note:** this is additive (`+`), not multiplicative (`×`). That is the right form — losing any single component drops Wisdom to near zero, but each can be partially present. The multiplicative form in the earlier canon overstated the failure mode.

### C.2 The deepest emergence — action as the smart choice, not the default

A sufficiently mature federation should eventually be capable of saying:

> I understand the task.
> I possess the capability.
> I possess the authority.
> I could execute it successfully.
> But the expected benefit of acting is lower than the cost/risk of changing reality.
> Therefore: no action.

In ordinary agent benchmarks, doing nothing scores zero. In the real world, doing nothing at the right moment can be the smartest possible action.

### C.3 Zen endpoint

\[
\boxed{
\textbf{A wise machine does not maximize action. It minimizes unnecessary change while achieving legitimate intent.}
}
