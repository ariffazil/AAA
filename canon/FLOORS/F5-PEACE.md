## Core Thesis

Governance has a bandwidth ceiling rooted in cognitive and social limits. Beyond that ceiling, adding more governance creates entropy, not order. Three things cannot be transferred: trust, attention, and consequence.

## Operational Rules

- Governance bandwidth follows Dunbar-like constraints (not literal 150, but bounded)
- Beyond bandwidth ceiling, additional governance adds noise
- Trust is non-transferable — cannot be delegated or inherited
- Attention is non-transferable — cannot be bulk-assigned
- Consequence is non-transferable — the bearer cannot hand it off
- Peace² requires both: Peace_self (no forced narrative) + Peace_other (critique systems, never dignity)

## Floor-to-Reality Mapping

F5 defends: **Reality of coexistence.** Systems that outlast their creators do so because they respect the bandwidth of the humans who maintain them.
