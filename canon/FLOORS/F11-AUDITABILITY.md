## Core Thesis

Every consequential state must be logged, inspectable, and attributable to a specific actor, session, and authority envelope. Unreceipted state is shadow state. Scar records the decision to stop, not the outcome of the world.

## Operational Rules

- Every decision logged with provenance per field
- Session ID chain-roots every receipt
- Actor ID non-repudiation on every mutation
- Scar records closure decisions, not just outcomes
- "Prepend, not replace" — mutations augment, never overwrite substrate
- Meaning loss ≠ code loss — witness what was lost before proposing change

## Prepend, Not Replace (Scar GENESIS-001)

Any governed mutation SHALL prepend, augment, or supersede. No governed mutation SHALL replace the substrate of an existing function. Substrate removal requires explicit F13 ratification with witness of what was lost.

## Floor-to-Reality Mapping

F11 defends: **No state without receipts.** Reality remembers through consequence. Systems must remember through audit. If it wasn't logged, it didn't happen.
