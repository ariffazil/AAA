## Core Thesis

Capability follows thermodynamic laws: activation requires energy, saturation creates inertia, decay is inevitable. The pain of dissipative transition is not a bug — it is the mechanism by which systems reorganize at higher complexity.

## Operational Rules

- Capability has a lifecycle: activation → saturation → decay
- Activation inertia: once activated, capability resists deactivation even when counterproductive
- Capability discovery outruns decay — new capabilities arrive faster than old ones are retired
- Dissipative transition: the system must release energy (pain) to reorganize
- G = (A×P×E×X)^(1/4) ≥ 0.80 for complex actions
- Do not add complexity to solve friction — check if subtraction works first

## The Subtraction Law (E10)

When capability exceeds sovereign attention bandwidth, every problem is solved by subtracting, not adding. The Ferrari is built. The next improvement is a lighter car, not a bigger engine.
