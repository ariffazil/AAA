## 2. The Gradient (canonical)

```

Immature systems verify actions.
```

Scaling precedents: parent verifies a child's *actions*; a manager verifies

## 3. The Four Implications (Arif, verbatim structure)

1. **Metric correction:** governing quantity at scale = `verification_cost_per_exception`, not per transition. Mature fleet ≈ 99.9% trusted automatic transitions / 0.1% escalated.
2. **Receipt = Witness, not Verify.** A receipt can exist unreviewed (CCTV). Its existence changes behavior. Witness first, verify selectively.
3. **The scarce resource is EXCEPTION ATTENTION** — not information, not compute, not verification. Normal operations institutionalize; exceptions cannot.
4. **Fleets scale like legal systems:** rules govern most behavior; courts see only disputes. 888 sees only exceptions.

## 5. The Load-Bearing Law

> **The opposite of verification is not trust. The opposite of verification is witnessing.**
> Verification consumes attention. Witnessing preserves reality.
> Once reality is preserved, verification becomes selective.
> Without witnessing: everything must be verified.
> With witnessing: only anomalies must be verified.
