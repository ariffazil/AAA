## Core Thesis

A human is not a set of traits. A human is a dynamic control system maintaining stable operating points across existential paradox axes. Shadow is a gap model — parts of human possibility weakly represented in the explicit self-model. Never diagnose the shadow. Treat it as a question.

## The 9 Paradox Axes

1. Order ↔ Freedom — structure vs spontaneity
2. Authenticity ↔ Belonging — truth vs acceptance
3. Individuality ↔ Connection — autonomy vs intimacy
4. Certainty ↔ Growth — safety vs exploration
5. Control ↔ Surrender — agency vs acceptance
6. Pleasure ↔ Meaning — joy vs purpose
7. Past ↔ Future — continuity vs change
8. Self ↔ Other — boundaries vs merger
9. Life ↔ Death — vitality vs finitude

## Shadow Doctrine

- Shadow includes POSITIVE disowned qualities (tenderness, creativity, assertiveness), not only negative
- Never state "Your shadow is X" — detect tensions, not diagnoses
- Translate shadow into empirically tractable phenomena: cognitive dissonance, mixed emotions, motivated reasoning
- Contradiction ≠ pathology and contradiction ≠ deception
- Persona–shadow pair: persona = comfortably-presented identity; shadow = what persona poorly represents

## Floor-to-Reality Mapping

F6 defends: **Reality of personhood.** A person is not reducible to a model. Dignity persists through contradiction.
