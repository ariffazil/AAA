## 2. The Verdict (Arif, 2026-09-07, verbatim elevation)

> "The report's most important sentence may be: 'The number does not transfer, the constraint does.' I would elevate that to the canonical form: **DUNBAR CONSTRAINT: Stable coordination capacity is finite. As coordination demand rises, systems must externalize state into institutions and artifacts. Failure to externalize produces attention overload, verification collapse, and governance degradation.**"

**Arif's refinement (constitutional):** Attention Capacity ≠ Governance Capacity. A human supervises a small number of active relationships, yet through institutions, rules, receipts, and witnesses governs a far larger capability graph. The governor need not hold every relationship in working memory — only reliable mechanisms to audit transitions. This is `Capability Graph ≠ APEX Governor`.

**Final judgment:** SEAL for the principle — "Dunbar is not a theory of people-counts. It is a theory of finite governance bandwidth."
