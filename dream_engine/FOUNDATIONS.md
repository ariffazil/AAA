# Dream Engine — Research Foundations

> Canonical reading set for the arifOS Dream Engine architecture.

## 1. Complementary Learning Systems

McClelland, McNaughton & O'Reilly — *Complementary Learning Systems* (1995). [https://stanford.edu/~jlmcc/papers/McCMcNaughtonOReilly95.pdf](https://stanford.edu/~jlmcc/papers/McCMcNaughtonOReilly95.pdf)

Supports: Separate episodic record from slowly earned generalized lessons.

## 2. Dyna

Sutton — *Dyna* (1991).

Supports: Experience → model → simulated planning loop.

## 3. Prioritized Experience Replay

Schaul et al. — *Prioritized Experience Replay* (2015). [https://arxiv.org/abs/1511.05952](https://arxiv.org/abs/1511.05952)

Supports: Replay should prioritize surprise, failure, and consequence, not token volume.

## 4. DreamerV3

Hafner et al. — *DreamerV3* (2025, Nature). [https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-08744-2](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-08744-2)

Supports: World models can learn behavior through simulated experience.

## 5. Continual Learning and Catastrophic Forgetting

van de Ven et al. — *Continual Learning and Catastrophic Forgetting* (2024). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.05175](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.05175)

Supports: Every promotion needs plasticity test AND retention test.

## 6. Generative Agents

Park et al. — *Generative Agents* (2023). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.03442](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.03442)

Supports: Observation → reflection → planning from stored experience.

## 7. Reflexion

Shinn et al. — *Reflexion* (2023). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.11366](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.11366)

Supports: Verbal lessons from episodic feedback, but reflection is candidate not ground truth.

## 8. MemGPT

Packer et al. — *MemGPT* (2023). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.08560](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.08560)

Supports: Explicit memory hierarchy; storage tier ≠ epistemic authority tier.

## 9. Sleep-time Compute

Behrouz et al. — *Sleep-time Compute* (2025). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.13171](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.13171)

Supports: Use idle compute for indexing, hypotheses, rehearsal — not irreversible action.

## 10. When Can LLMs Actually Correct Their Own Mistakes?

Kamoi et al. — *When Can LLMs Actually Correct Their Own Mistakes?* (2024). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.01297](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.01297)

Supports: Self-correction unreliable without external feedback; dreamer must not be own witness.

## 11. Strictly Proper Scoring Rules

Gneiting & Raftery — *Strictly Proper Scoring Rules* (2007).

Supports: Calibration measured by Brier/log scores, not self-assessed confidence.

## 12. Provenance and Governance

W3C PROV (2013). [https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-dm/](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-dm/) + NIST AI RMF (2023). [https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework)

Supports: Provenance and governance substrate for dream-generated policies.
