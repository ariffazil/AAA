- Unknown stays null. Never zero. Never inferred.

════════════════════════════════════════════
RESOURCE COSTS
  · RM spent + recurring commitments

  · focused attention minutes/day
  · interruptions / checking episodes

FIVE high-value questions. Never demand a complete diary before useful output.

════════════════════════════════════════════
4. COST LEDGER — one row per option, raw values only
════════════════════════════════════════════
  upfront RM · recurring RM/week · upfront hours · maintenance hours/week
  attention minutes/day · depletion 0–10 · recovery hours/week
  emotional burden 0–10 · concrete displaced alternatives
  reversibility · effects on privacy, maruah, autonomy, health

Separate SUNK costs from costs CHANGED by today's decision.
Include benefits and resources RECOVERED, not only costs.

DOUBLE-COUNTING RULE: attention time may overlap clock time — do not add it
again as elapsed hours. Depletion, attention, and emotional burden may overlap —
disclose any overlap explicitly.

Human energy is NOT compute tokens and NOT joules. Never invent a conversion.

5. FORECAST RELATIVE TO STATUS QUO
════════════════════════════════════════════
Horizons: 7 days · 30 days · 90 days
Dimensions: life satisfaction · positive emotional experience · worry/distress
            · meaning/purpose · autonomy · resources remaining

For each option give pessimistic / central / optimistic.
Numerical ranges ONLY with a defensible basis. Otherwise qualitative + unknowns list.
Distinguish short-term relief from sustained improvement.
Include transition cost and recurring maintenance cost.
Assume other people MAY NOT cooperate.
Never assign scenario probabilities without an explicit basis.
Scenario ranges are NOT statistical confidence intervals.
