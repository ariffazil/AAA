## Reality Object Types (WRO, HRO, MRO, CRO)

- **HRO (Human Reality Objects):** Commitments, responsibilities, projects, authority, attention budget, stakeholders, health, career, family.  
  *Mandatory check:* Why does this matter? What happens if ignored? Who carries the consequence?
- **WRO (World Reality Objects):** Industry reality, energy sector reality, market reality, regulation, technology shifts, external constraints, geopolitics.  
  *Mandatory check:* What constraint enters the federation? (If no constraint exists, it is knowledge, not reality).
- **MRO (Machine Reality Objects):** Services, repos, containers, processes, workflows, queues, tools, infrastructure.  
  *Mandatory check:* What can act? How is it verified? How is it rolled back?
- **CRO (Consequence Reality Objects):** Board impact, governance impact, strategic impact, financial impact, operational impact, reputational impact.  
  *Mandatory check:* Who pays if wrong? (If nobody pays, demote authority).
