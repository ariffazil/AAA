```
U_i = w_o·O_i + w_l·L_i + w_v·V_i + w_t·T_i
```

- O = optionality retained

**Confusing Uᵢ with APEX Gᵢ = semantic drift.** They are different math objects answering different questions.

### Stage 2 — GROUND
Per candidate, decompose into OBSERVED / DERIVED / ASSUMED / UNKNOWN / CONTESTED.

Path success depending on ASSUMED masquerading as OBSERVED loses probability.

### Stage 3 — SIMULATE
For each pᵢ: T+5m, T+1h, T+1d, T+1w, T+1m consequences.

**P18 observer paradox applies**: measurement changes behavior. Future-state tree **includes** side-effect of acting.

### Stage 4 — FALSIFY
Each pᵢ attacked from 6 angles:
1. Positive: everything works as intended
2. Failure: primary assumption is wrong
3. Adversarial: apparent success while actually failing
4. Inversion: what makes opposite better?
5. No-action: what if we do nothing?
6. Second-order: what behavior agents learn if policy repeats 10,000×?

> *You are not merely choosing an action. You are training an institutional habit.*

### Stage 5 — APEX_GATE
Apply `G_i ≥ G_min` (geometric mean ≥ floor).

Weakest dimension collapses the whole candidate. No weighted sum continues.

### Stage 6 — MEANING_GATE
Check `M_i^hard_veto`. If any veto dimension fails, ELIMINATE.

### Stage 7 — PARETO
Remove dominated paths. If `p_j` safer, cheaper, more reversible, better evidenced, equally effective as `p_k`, `p_k` disappears.

12 → 6 → 3 → 1.

### Stage 8 — ROBUSTNESS

### Stage 9 — COLLAPSE
