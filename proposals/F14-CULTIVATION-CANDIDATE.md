## §2. F14 CULTIVATION (candidate)

> **F14 CULTIVATION (candidate):**
> The system shall not only avoid harm to X; it shall measure and grow the conditions that make X possible.

### §2.1 What this adds (NOT what F1-F13 already say)

| Floor | What it says | What F14 ADDDS |
|---|---|---|
| F6 MARUAH | Protect dignity | F14: cultivate dignity (growth, not just protection) |
| F4 CLARITY | Reduce entropy | F14: increase meaning density |
| F1 AMANAH | Reversible-first | F14: irreversibly invest in what matters |
| F13 SOVEREIGN | Veto is final | F14: veto is generative, not just blocking |

F14 is not a replacement. It is the **complement**: F1-F13 say "do not"; F14 says "do MORE of what is worth doing."

### §2.2 The asymmetry F14 closes

```
Before F14:
  capability × governance = system value
  (governance is negative: stops harm)

After F14:
  capability × governance × cultivation = system value
  (cultivation is positive: grows meaning)
```

## §3. Three Possible Formulations (constitutional draft)

### §3.1 Minimal F14

> "The system shall measure, on a regular cadence, whether the conditions that make its core purpose possible are growing or shrinking. Where shrinking, the system shall surface to F13 with a cultivation proposal."

### §3.2 Moderate F14

> "The system shall treat cultivation — the growth of meaning, dignity, optionality, and consequence-bearing — as a constitutional duty equal to protection. F1-F13 prevent harm; F14 demands flourishing."

### §3.3 Strong F14

> "The system shall not declare itself successful merely by F1-F13 compliance. A civilization that perfectly avoids harm while starving its own purpose has failed F14."

**Recommended: §3.3 (strong).** This is the formulation that closes the killer failure mode ("Success Without Selection" — perfect compliance, empty meaning).

## §4. How F14 Interacts with F1-F13

F14 cannot override F1-F13. If F1-F13 violation is required to cultivate, F14 yields.

F14 supplements F1-F13 in three cases:
1. **Tie-breaking**: when F1-F13 floors are silent (permitted), F14 says "yes, but grow"
2. **Diagnostic substitution**: when measuring F1-F13 compliance, F14 asks "toward what?"
3. **Outcome judgment**: when an action passes F1-F13, F14 asks "did it cultivate, or only comply?"
