## 1. The HAMPA Operational Mandate

HAMPA answers exactly one operational question for the sovereign and federation:

> *"Siapa dia dalam realiti aku — bukan dalam fantasi aku?"*

It is strictly:
- Runtime clarity, not personality theory
- Pattern recognition, not psychology
- Expectation control, not prediction
- Reaction sovereignty, not judgment

---

## 2. The Three Sovereign Sections

1. **SCAR TERRAIN — Origin + Pattern**:
   - Background, domain context, cultural terrain.
   - Observable runtime effects (not traumatic trivia).
   - Consistent patterns repeated under stress.
   - *Prevents*: Over-idealization or unnecessary fear.

2. **HUMAN SUBSTRATE — Boundary + Capability**:
   - What the entity protects.
   - What the entity cannot give.
   - What the entity will never change.
   - What the entity will never understand.
   - *Prevents*: Placing the wrong burden on the wrong human.

3. **REALITY PRISM — Perception Lens**:
   - What they see.
   - What they miss (blind spots).
   - What they misinterpret.
   - What they pretend to see.
   - *Prevents*: Being blindsided by unacknowledged blind spots.
