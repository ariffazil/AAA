## 1. Thesis

The mature dream engine is not a generator; it is an **admission institution**. Generation is cheap and must stay unconstrained; governance belongs at the membrane where candidates enter institutional persistence. *"Which imagined futures deserve institutional attention?"* is a different — and more tractable — question than *"how do we dream more?"*

## 3. Why admissibility is the right primitive (grounded in ratified doctrine)

- **Attention is scarce** — ratified 2026-09-11 family (`attention-kill-criterion`: *"doctrine without kill is decoration"*; attention-scarcity economics). An admission gate is the keep/kill mechanism for imagined futures.
- **Witness before mutation** — admission requires counterfactual + provenance + witness (F2/F3/F7); the dream skill's seal gate already embodies this.
- **Memory Promotion Gate** (Witness ≠ Seal ≠ Memory) — memory admissibility already governs what enters institutional memory; dream admissibility is its structural twin at the imagination boundary.
- **Institutional Memory Strata (S0–S3)** — admission *is* the promotion ladder between strata.
- **Write-price collapse** — the recurring federation disease: prose describes more capability than the filesystem contains (this session: FI-003 endorsement falsified; APEX surface drift confirmed). Admissibility as a *measured, gated act* is the direct countermeasure to map-over-territory.
- **F9 boundary holds** — "dream" stays mechanical (metabolize · compress · shadow-commit). No phenomenology claims.

## 4. Proposed admission pipeline (draft)

```
GENERATE  (free · private · uncapped)
   ↓ candidate
CANDIDATE (spool — the G3 inbox = first membrane)
   ↓ score + challenge
SCORE (admissibility vectors) → CHALLENGE (counterfactual rehearsal) → SHADOW (observation window)
   ↓ promote only when gates pass
PROMOTE (governed) → CANON (F13)
```

Admissibility vector draft — each would require a measurement card (per floor-discipline):

- **Novelty** — vs existing canon; not repetition
- **Witnessability** — can it be evidenced / falsified?
- **Consequence** — blast radius if adopted
- **Reversibility** — rollback cost
- **Provenance** — source refs complete
- **Dissent** — survives counterfactual challenge

## 5. Anti-patterns

1. **Suppression at source** — filtering what may be dreamed sterilizes the generator. Filter at the membrane, never at generation.
2. **Admission inflation** — admitting everything is noise; the admission rate is itself a governed number.
3. **Goodhart on admission scores** — the scorers are attack surfaces; evaluate them adversarially (same lesson as the APEX metric audit).
4. **Admission as popularity** — loudness ≠ admissibility; witnessability ≠ agreement.
5. **Gate without kill** — an admission gate that never rejects is decoration.

## 6. Falsifiable research questions

1. Does an explicit admission gate reduce canon churn **without** reducing useful novelty? (measure: canon edits vs proposal yield over N cycles)
2. What is the healthy admission-rate band? (hypothesis: reject the majority, promote the minority; band = F13-set)
3. Do admissibility vectors predict downstream failure of promoted items? (calibrate on `dreamer_receipts` + `memory_audit_log`)
4. Does counterfactual challenge measurably reduce hallucinated canonical statements?
5. Witness independence: do two independent challengers agree on admission? (the same independence problem as W³)
