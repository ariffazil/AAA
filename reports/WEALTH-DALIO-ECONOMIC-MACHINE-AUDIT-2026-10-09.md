## Dalio model audit

### What survives scrutiny

| Dalio primitive | Audit | Kernel treatment |
|---|---|---|
| One party’s spending becomes another party’s income | **CLAIM** as an aggregate accounting linkage, subject to imports, taxes, saving, and financial leakage | Encode as a stock-flow identity with explicit sector boundaries |
| Bank lending creates credit and matching balance-sheet entries | **CLAIM**, but “out of thin air” needs regulatory, capital, funding, collateral, profitability, and borrower-demand constraints | Observe credit creation by sector and instrument, not as unconstrained magic |
| Credit can pull expenditure forward | **CLAIM** for many borrowing arrangements | Track maturity, refinancing, currency, fixed/floating rate, and use of proceeds |
| Debt service can compress later spending | **CLAIM** | Make debt-service ratio a first-class stress variable |
| Productive credit differs from consumption-only credit | **CLAIM** directionally; classification is contextual | Estimate incremental cash flow, productivity, resilience, externality, and distribution |
| Asset prices, collateral, creditworthiness, and lending form feedback loops | **CLAIM** | Model collateral-price and underwriting loops explicitly |
| Deleveraging is multi-channel | **CLAIM** | Represent fiscal, restructuring, monetary, income-growth, inflation, and maturity channels separately |
| Productivity governs long-run living standards | **CLAIM**, but distribution and ecological/resource constraints affect realised welfare | Track productivity beside median income, resilience, energy/resource efficiency, and dignity |

Modern central-bank explanation supports the key endogenous-money point: commercial bank lending creates matching deposits, while monetary policy influences rather than mechanically dictates the quantity through a reserve multiplier.[^3][^4] BIS evidence also supports elevating debt-service ratios: DSRs measure interest plus amortisation relative to income and act as useful early-warning indicators, especially at shorter horizons.[^5][^6][^7]

### Where the machine breaks

| Limitation | Why it matters | Required correction |
|---|---|---|
| Fixed 5–8 and 75–100 year clocks | Cycle length is an empirical outcome, not a physical constant; BIS research finds financial cycles often around 10–20 years and difficult to identify in real time.[^8][^9] | Infer regime probabilities from data; never advance state by elapsed time alone |
| Representative-agent aggregation | Household, corporate, bank, government, and external-sector balance sheets can move in opposite directions | Maintain sectoral ledgers and counterparty matrices |
| Debt quantity overemphasis | Currency, maturity, rate reset, holder base, collateral, and income volatility can dominate gross debt | Compute debt quality and refinancing topology, not just debt/GDP |
| Simplified central-bank control | Policy rates do not guarantee credit demand, bank risk appetite, pass-through, or productive investment | Add lending standards, spreads, bank capital/liquidity, defaults, and borrower demand |
| Inflation simplified to excess spending | Supply shocks, imported inflation, exchange rates, administered prices, expectations, market structure, and wages matter | Separate demand, supply, imported, fiscal, and expectation channels |
| “Printing money” language | It merges reserves, deposits, asset swaps, fiscal transfers, and monetisation | Preserve instrument-level balance sheets and transmission paths |
| Distribution treated late | Who owns assets and owes debt affects consumption response, legitimacy, and political stability | Add household deciles, SMEs, labour share, asset ownership, and weakest-stakeholder impacts |
| Policy optimisation underspecified | A “beautiful deleveraging” is a goal, not an executable policy rule | Use scenario sets, Pareto trade-offs, uncertainty bands, and sovereign judgment |
| Correlation-to-causality risk | Similar historical shapes do not establish identical mechanisms | Require causal hypotheses, counterfactuals, and disconfirming evidence |

**PLAUSIBLE — Dalio is best treated as a causal graph and teaching prior, not a universal macro law.** His framework is valuable because it forces stock-flow consistency and delayed feedback thinking; it becomes hazardous when a persuasive narrative is mistaken for a uniquely identified state of the world.

## Malaysia reference implementation

Malaysia demonstrates why the model must be sectoral. Official 2025 data show labour productivity per hour grew 3.7% for the year, while government fiscal documents projected household debt at 84.8% of GDP as of June 2025, with median DSRs of 33% for outstanding and 41% for newly approved household loans.[^10][^11] Federal debt-service charges were projected at RM54.3 billion in 2025, or 16.3% of revenue, while the baseline debt-sustainability path estimated federal debt at 65.8% of GDP in 2026, declining toward 60% by 2030; the combined-shock scenario was materially worse.[^12]

These facts cannot be collapsed into a single “Malaysia is late-cycle” label. A valid AAA state would separately represent improving measured productivity, high household leverage with currently sound median servicing, fiscal interest burden, external debt/currency exposure, property collateral, banking asset quality, and distributional stress. Bank Negara’s national summary page also exposes timely national accounts and external-debt series suitable for the observation layer.[^13]

Recommended Malaysia observation pack:

- Household, corporate, federal, banking, and external debt stocks.
- Debt-service ratios, rate-reset schedules, maturity walls, and delinquency transitions.
- Credit growth and credit impulse by purpose: housing, vehicles, consumption, SME, productive investment.
- House prices, rents, transaction volumes, collateral haircuts, and construction supply.
- Nominal and real income by decile; unemployment, underemployment, and labour share.
- CPI decomposition, PPI/import prices, MYR, fuel/food administered prices, and inflation expectations.
- Productivity per hour and worker by sector, not only aggregate GDP.

## Red-team findings

### Critical

**HOLD — sovereign approval is not cryptographically transaction-bound.** The arifOS documentation acknowledges that irreversible acknowledgement and actor signatures remain payload fields rather than proof bound to a unique transaction, state, nonce, and scope. Until corrected, no capital-moving path should trust `ack_irreversible=true` as sufficient authority.

**HOLD — ledger integrity lacks independent anchoring.** An internally held hash chain can reveal accidental or external changes but not a full rewrite by the holder with recomputed hashes. Publish or externally sign periodic chain heads before describing the record as independently attestable.

### High

**CLAIM — tool-surface drift is real.** Live WEALTH exposes 15 tools, while repository prose advertises 20 and retains historical counts. Generate README, `llms.txt`, MCP manifests, and badges from the runtime registry, then fail CI on mismatch.

**CLAIM — clean-room reproducibility is incomplete.** Focused test collection did not run under the available fresh environment because of missing/incompatible dependencies. Publish a hermetic lock, container digest, test command, and machine-readable test receipt.

**PLAUSIBLE — verdict namespace collision can cause authority confusion.** WEALTH’s output schema contains constitutional-looking words such as `SEAL`, `HOLD`, and `VOID` while the organ is compute-only. Rename domain outputs to `assessment` or namespace them (`WEALTH.SAFE_TO_STUDY`), reserving bare constitutional seals for arifOS.

### Medium

**PLAUSIBLE — every “pure computation” writing a receipt is a hidden side effect.** The live descriptions disclose this, which is good, but callers and tests must distinguish analytical purity from audit mutation. A read-only dry-run mode should compute without durable write and return a prospective receipt hash.

**PLAUSIBLE — scalar confidence is not calibration.** Confidence must be evaluated against realised outcomes using Brier/log scores, coverage of prediction intervals, and reliability curves. Until sufficient observations exist, mark `calibration_class: UNVALIDATED`.

**PLAUSIBLE — model monoculture risk.** A compelling economic story can dominate routing even when alternative causal mechanisms fit. Enforce ensemble diversity and record dissent rather than averaging it away.
